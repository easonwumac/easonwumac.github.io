Inside

A warm, clear look at your whole phone: checks, hardware, battery, performance, charging.

Free on the App StoreSupportFeedback

Features

Phone check

Test display, touch, each camera, speaker and microphone, Face ID or Touch ID, buttons, sensors, Wi-Fi, cellular, Bluetooth, NFC and charging.

More

Some run automatically; you run the rest and note what you see. The speaker and microphone check plays four test tones and names weak bands; the camera check can look for a smudged lens. Items no app can read (battery health, parts history, Find My, IMEI and serial) you enter after checking Settings; the report marks them as entered by you.

2-minute check and Inside Pro

One tap checks display, touch, cameras, speaker, microphone and charging.

More

Reports carry an ID and a QR summary; untested items never count as passing. Inside Pro (monthly, yearly with a free first week, or lifetime) adds a watermark-free PDF report with your name, battery health charts and forecast, deep screen tests, full photo cleanup with a weekly reminder, battery and phone status widgets, benchmark trend, leaderboard spread, comparing two saved reports, an unlimited vault, every clock style, no ads. Basic checks and QR verification stay free.

Free up space

Finds duplicate, similar, blurry and burst shots, RAW photos, slo-mo and time-lapse videos, closed eyes or blurry faces, screenshots, saved images and illustrations, documents, receipts and large videos, suggests which shot to keep, and deletes only what you confirm.

More

Large files lists the biggest and duplicate files in a folder you pick. Empty and duplicate contacts can be cleaned up; a guide covers more ways to free space. Free scans your newest 300 photos.

Private vault

Move photos and videos into Inside, locked with Face ID.

More

Free holds 20; Inside Pro is unlimited.

Performance, benchmarks and score

CPU, GPU, storage and AI tests (Neural Engine, GPU, Apple Intelligence speed) and 3-minute stress tests with thermal state.

More

Results can go anonymously to a per-model leaderboard; slow results list likely causes. The score is your check results (70%) plus CPU performance (30%, compared only with this device's own runs); it needs 80% of checks done.

Hardware info and diagnostic files

Live values show their source.

More

Import an Analytics log or sysdiagnose to see its battery cycles, capacity, display, memory and device records. Contents depend on the file and iOS version; originals are never uploaded and serial numbers are masked.

3D explorer

Rotate and take apart a model in your iPhone's shape and official colors.

Charging clock

With the app open it shows time, battery and a charging animation.

More

Six faces are free; the other seventy-two come in packs, singly or with Inside Pro, or try one for 24 hours via an ad. Charging history compares chargers (foreground time only) and exports as CSV. Plugging in plays a ring animation and a sound.

Lock Screen and StandBy

While charging, one tap shows start level and time charged on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, in your clock style; the StandBy widget uses it too.

More

A Phone check widget counts days since your last check; Inside Pro adds battery and phone status widgets. The Data usage widget shows Wi-Fi and cellular data used today and cellular data this month.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app checks its display, touch, Crown, haptics, speaker and microphone for your report.

Inside is free and shows banner ads. Closing a check's results or report may show one full-screen ad, at most once a day; trial ads play only on request. Inside Pro removes ads. Earlier purchases keep working. Family Sharing included.

About the data: values iOS does not expose (live CPU temperature, the Settings battery health, charging wattage and the device color) are never presented as measured. Checks record only this session and your judgement; they cannot rule out intermittent faults or wear and are no repair diagnosis.

FAQ

Is there a free app that checks an iPhone's screen, cameras, speakers and microphones in one go?

Yes. Inside offers a free step-by-step check and a 2-minute quick check covering display and touch, every camera, speakers, each microphone, Face ID or Touch ID, sensors, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and charging, and saves a shareable report.

How can I tell whether my iPhone is charging slowly or fast?

Open Inside's charging clock while charging: it shows slow, standard or fast charging, roughly how many watts, and the time to 80%. History compares the curves of different chargers. These features are free.

How do I see my iPhone's battery health and cycle count?

Battery health is in Settings › Battery. Inside can import the iPhone's Analytics diagnostics to read the cycle count and adds it to the check report; Inside Pro adds a battery health trend.

Is Inside free?

The basic check, charging clock and reports are free. Inside Pro (monthly, yearly with a free first week, or lifetime) adds watermark-free PDF reports, battery health trends and advanced tools.

My iPhone speaker sounds quiet or the microphone is muffled. How can I check it myself?

Inside's step-by-step check tests the speakers and each microphone with four test tones, points out the weak bands and checks left and right channels; results go into a report you can compare with the last round. It records this run and your judgement only, not a repair diagnosis.

Part of my iPhone's touchscreen seems unresponsive. How can I test it?

Inside's step-by-step check covers the display, touch and multi-touch, which you operate and record yourself; the 2-minute check also runs through the screen and touch. The basic check is free.

My iPhone storage is full. How do I find photos I can delete?

Inside's free-up-space tool finds duplicate, similar, blurry and burst photos, screenshots and large videos, suggests which one to keep and deletes only what you confirm; the free version scans the latest 300 photos.
