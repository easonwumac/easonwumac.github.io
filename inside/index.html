用溫暖、清楚的方式看看手機現在的狀態；從功能、硬體、電池到效能，一次全面檢查。

Inside 結合引導檢查、即時系統資訊、可匯入的診斷紀錄、互動式 3D 外觀與充電時鐘。從總覽查看電量、充電狀態、系統熱狀態、儲存空間與網路連線；從硬體資訊頁查看各項數值及來源。

逐項檢查

依螢幕、相機、音訊、感測與按鍵、連線、電池與所有權分組，逐項檢查顯示、觸控與多點觸控、手機具備的每顆鏡頭、手電筒、揚聲器、每個麥克風與左右聲道、Face ID 或 Touch ID、距離感測、音量鍵、觸覺回饋、動作感測、氣壓計、LiDAR（Pro 機型）、定位、Wi-Fi、行動網路、藍牙、NFC 可用性與充電。能由系統判斷的項目會先自動檢查，其餘由你實際操作、觀察並記錄結果。電池健康度、零件與維修記錄、尋找、IMEI 與序號是否和盒子相符等 App 無法讀取的項目，由你到「設定」查看後填入，報告會標示為使用者填寫。途中離開可接續未測項目；電池步驟可從已匯入的 Analytics 紀錄帶入循環次數；「快速檢查」只查常用項目。喇叭與麥克風改以四段測試音檢查並指出偏弱的頻段，相機可檢查鏡頭是否有污漬。報告會保存，可和上一輪對照，也能分享成圖片或文字。

2 分鐘檢查與機況 Pro

在總覽點一下，就能依序檢查螢幕、觸控、相機、揚聲器、麥克風與充電。報告附報告編號與 QR code 摘要，他人掃描即可核對；未測試、略過與無法檢查會分別標示，不會算成正常。機況 Pro（月繳、年繳首週免費或終身買斷）提供可加上你的名稱與聯絡方式、無浮水印的 PDF 報告、電池健康趨勢、螢幕深入測試、完整照片清理與每週清理提醒、電池健康度與手機狀態小工具、跑分趨勢、排行榜分佈、兩份報告逐項比較、不限量保險箱，並含所有時鐘風格與無廣告；基本檢查與掃描 QR code 核對報告仍然免費。

釋放空間

在手機上找出重複、相似、模糊與連拍照片、RAW 照片、慢動作與縮時攝影影片、閉眼或臉部模糊、截圖、存下的圖片與插畫、文件與收據，以及大影片，建議要保留的一張，只刪你確認的項目。「大型檔案」可找出你選的資料夾中最大與重複的檔案；也能整理空白與重複聯絡人，並有「釋出更多空間」指南。免費版掃描最新 300 張照片。

私密保險箱

把照片與影片移進 App，用 Face ID 鎖住。免費版可存 20 項，機況 Pro 不限量。

效能、跑分與評分

在手機上執行 CPU、GPU、儲存空間速度與 AI 推論（神經網路引擎、GPU；支援的機型另測 Apple Intelligence 生成速度）測試，以及附熱狀態的 3 分鐘 CPU 與 GPU 壓力測試。可選擇匿名發布成績，在各機型排行榜上比較；跑分偏慢時會列出可能原因。綜合分數以檢查結果為主（70%），CPU 效能（30%）只和同一台手機的正常表現比較，檢查完成八成以上才計分。

硬體資訊與診斷檔

即時資訊標明系統來源；你也能自行匯入 Analytics 或 sysdiagnose，查看檔案中可辨識的電池循環、容量、面板、記憶體與裝置紀錄。匯入資料與即時讀值分開呈現，保留欄位來源和紀錄時間。實際可取得的項目依檔案與 iOS 版本而異；原始檔不會上傳，序號會遮罩。

3D 探索

旋轉、拆解與聚焦示意模型。已收錄的 iPhone 依機型呈現外觀，並提供手動選擇官方配色；不支援的機型使用中性示意。內部結構及顏色為視覺示意，不是實際掃描。

充電時鐘

在 App 開啟時顯示時間、電量與充電動畫，支援直式與橫式。六款錶面免費使用；其餘七十二款可依風格包單獨購買，或由機況 Pro 一次解鎖，也能看一則廣告試用一組 24 小時。充電紀錄依充電場次整理，可標記充電器比較充電幅度；紀錄僅涵蓋 App 在前景的時段，支援 CSV 匯出。接上充電器時會播放圓環動畫與充電提示音。

鎖定畫面與待機模式

充電時點一下，即時動態就會在鎖定畫面與動態島顯示開始時的電量與已充電時間，並套用你選的時鐘風格；「待機時鐘」小工具也能放到主畫面或待機模式。「手機健檢」小工具顯示距離上次檢查幾天，點一下開始 2 分鐘檢查；機況 Pro 另有電池健康度與手機狀態小工具；也可選擇在 iOS 更新後收到檢查提醒；機況 Pro 另可每月提醒記錄電池健康度。

重置前準備

依序勾選備份、解除 Apple Watch 配對、登出 Apple 帳號與清除 iPhone，每一步都附上 Apple 的說明文章。

iPhone Duo

展開時，總覽留在左頁，檢查、充電與紀錄在右頁開啟。摺疊與鉸鏈檢查會即時顯示開合角度。探索頁中的 3D 模型可以摺起與展開，並顯示鉸鏈。

Apple Watch

手錶 App 可檢查螢幕、觸控、數位錶冠、觸覺回饋、揚聲器與麥克風，並把結果加進 iPhone 的檢查報告；手錶與錶面複雜功能可查看 iPhone 的電量與充電狀態；iPhone 的「硬體資訊」也會顯示已配對手錶的機型、watchOS 與電池。需搭配 iPhone App 使用，並與 iPhone 配對。

Inside 免費使用，畫面會顯示橫幅廣告。完成檢查、關閉報告圖片後，可能出現一則全螢幕廣告，每天最多一次；試用廣告只在你選擇時播放。機況 Pro 不顯示廣告；先前的購買照常有效。購買支援家人共享。

資料範圍說明：iOS 未公開的即時 CPU 溫度、設定頁電池健康度、實際充電功率及機身顏色，不會當成實測資料呈現。部分硬體名稱可由匯入紀錄直接提供；未確認的代碼不推測。熱狀態為 iOS 回報的分級。檢查只記錄這次操作與你的判斷，不能排除間歇性故障或零件老化，也不是維修診斷。

A warm, clear look at your whole phone: checks, hardware, battery, performance, charging.

Phone check

Test display, touch, each camera, speaker and microphone, Face ID or Touch ID, buttons, sensors, Wi-Fi, cellular, Bluetooth, NFC and charging. Some run automatically; you run the rest and note what you see. The speaker and microphone check plays four test tones and names weak bands; the camera check can look for a smudged lens. Items no app can read (battery health, parts history, Find My, IMEI and serial) you enter after checking Settings; the report marks them as entered by you.

2-minute check and Inside Pro

One tap checks display, touch, cameras, speaker, microphone and charging. Reports carry an ID and a QR summary; untested items never count as passing. Inside Pro (monthly, yearly with a free first week, or lifetime) adds a watermark-free PDF report with your name, a battery health trend, deep screen tests, full photo cleanup with a weekly reminder, battery and phone status widgets, benchmark trend, leaderboard spread, comparing two saved reports, an unlimited vault, every clock style, no ads. Basic checks and QR verification stay free.

Free up space

Finds duplicate, similar, blurry and burst shots, RAW photos, slo-mo and time-lapse videos, closed eyes or blurry faces, screenshots, saved images and illustrations, documents, receipts and large videos, suggests which shot to keep, and deletes only what you confirm. Large files lists the biggest and duplicate files in a folder you pick. Empty and duplicate contacts can be cleaned up; a guide covers more ways to free space. Free scans your newest 300 photos.

Private vault

Move photos and videos into Inside, locked with Face ID. Free holds 20; Inside Pro is unlimited.

Performance, benchmarks and score

CPU, GPU, storage and AI tests (Neural Engine, GPU, Apple Intelligence speed) and 3-minute stress tests with thermal state. Results can go anonymously to a per-model leaderboard; slow results list likely causes. The score is your check results (70%) plus CPU performance (30%, compared only with this device's own runs); it needs 80% of checks done.

Hardware info and diagnostic files

Live values show their source. Import an Analytics log or sysdiagnose to see its battery cycles, capacity, display, memory and device records. Contents depend on the file and iOS version; originals are never uploaded and serial numbers are masked.

3D explorer

Rotate and take apart a model in your iPhone's shape and official colors.

Charging clock

With the app open it shows time, battery and a charging animation. Six faces are free; the other seventy-two come in packs, singly or with Inside Pro, or try one for 24 hours via an ad. Charging history compares chargers (foreground time only) and exports as CSV. Plugging in plays a ring animation and a sound.

Lock Screen and StandBy

While charging, one tap shows start level and time charged on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, in your clock style; the StandBy widget uses it too. A Phone check widget counts days since your last check; Inside Pro adds battery and phone status widgets.

iPhone Duo and Apple Watch

On iPhone Duo, two pages split overview and checks and a hinge check follows the angle. The Apple Watch app checks its display, touch, Crown, haptics, speaker and microphone for your report.

Inside is free and shows banner ads. Closing a finished check's report image may show one full-screen ad, at most once a day; trial ads play only on request. Inside Pro removes ads. Earlier purchases keep working. Family Sharing included.

About the data: values iOS does not expose (live CPU temperature, the Settings battery health, charging wattage and the device color) are never presented as measured. Checks record only this session and your judgement; they cannot rule out intermittent faults or wear and are no repair diagnosis.
