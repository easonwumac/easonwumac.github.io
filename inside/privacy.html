摘要

系統數值與感測器資料用於本機呈現，不傳送至我們的伺服器，也不作為廣告自訂參數。

資料存放

健檢結果、手動配色、偏好與前景觀察紀錄可儲存在 App 資料中，可能包含於系統備份。

匯入診斷檔時，僅讀取你主動選擇的檔案，在本機解析，不上傳、不保存原始診斷檔副本，也不將其提供給廣告 SDK。解析後的硬體報告會保存在本機並排除系統備份；序號會遮罩處理。原始檔仍由「檔案」App 管理。

只有你啟動相關檢查時才要求相機與麥克風權限。相機僅預覽，不錄製或上傳。麥克風錄音最多五秒，用於本機回聽；正常離開檢查或進入背景會刪除暫存錄音。

廣告與追蹤

Inside 免費使用橫幅廣告，透過 Google Mobile Ads 與 User Messaging Platform（UMP）管理適用的廣告同意。非個人化廣告仍可能涉及 IP 位址、裝置資訊、廣告互動與診斷資料處理。本 App 不要求跨 App 追蹤權限。適用時可以從設定重新開啟廣告隱私選項。

購買

首版免費使用，可能顯示橫幅廣告；沒有去廣告購買或訂閱。

第三方服務

廣告資料處理請參閱 Google 廣告資料說明。接收你主動匯出內容的服務由你選擇，其處理方式依該服務政策。

資料刪除

你可主動分享健檢文字報告、匯入分析摘要或觀察 CSV，也可從健檢重新一輪、紀錄頁清除觀察資料，或在硬體分析報告頁清除報告與匯入摘要。你也可以在硬體分析報告頁主動分享摘要。

聯絡我們

客服與隱私問題：easonwumac@gmail.com。回報問題時，請提供 App 版本、iOS 版本、機型與重現步驟；不要附上整份診斷檔、私人錄音或敏感備註。

Summary

System values and sensor data are used for display on your device. They are not sent to our servers or used as custom advertising parameters.

Data stored

Checkup results, manually chosen colors, preferences, and foreground observation records may be stored in app data and included in system backups.

When importing a diagnostic file, Inside reads only the file you select and parses it locally. It does not upload it, retain a copy of the original diagnostic file, or provide it to the advertising SDK. The parsed hardware report is stored locally and excluded from system backups; serial numbers are masked. The original file remains managed by the Files app.

Camera and microphone permissions are requested only when you start the relevant check. The camera provides a preview only, with no recording or upload. Microphone recordings last no more than five seconds and are used for local playback. Temporary recordings are deleted when you leave the check normally or the app enters the background.

Ads and tracking

Inside is free with banner ads. Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) manage applicable advertising consent. Non-personalized ads may still involve processing IP addresses, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. The app does not request cross-app tracking permission. When applicable, you can reopen ad privacy options from Settings.

Purchases

The first version is free and may show banner ads. It has no remove-ads purchase or subscription.

Third-party services

See Google’s advertising data information for advertising data practices. You choose the services that receive content you export; their handling of it follows their own policies.

Deleting your data

You can choose to share a checkup text report, imported analysis summary, or observation CSV. You can start a new checkup round, clear observation data on the records page, or clear the report and imported summary on the hardware analysis report page. You can also choose to share a summary from the hardware analysis report page.

Contact

For support and privacy questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. For an issue report, include the app version, iOS version, device model, and steps to reproduce. Do not attach complete diagnostic files, private recordings, or sensitive notes.
