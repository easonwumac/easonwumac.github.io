常見問題

怎麼玩？

點閘門（或水閥輪）讓相鄰閘室的水位拉平，再點或拖曳船穿過打開的閘門，駛到同色旗子旁。水量有限，每次開閘都會永久拉平水位，所以開閘的順序就是謎題。水太淺船會擱淺，高桅桿也過不了低橋。開閘次數越少星星越多，卡關時可以用提示看下一步。

提示和重試用完了怎麼辦？

提示與重試每 5 分鐘各回復 1 次，最多 10 次。也可以在提示商店觀看獎勵廣告取得 6 次提示，或購買提示包。

可以離線遊玩嗎？

關卡可離線遊玩，廣告不可用時不會阻擋遊戲進度保存。廣告服務的資料處理並非完全離線。

換手機或重新安裝後，購買還在嗎？

「移除廣告」可在「設定 → 恢復購買」取回。提示包屬於消耗性項目，無法恢復。遊戲進度存在裝置上，刪除 App 會一併刪除。

如何查看廣告隱私選項？

SDK 要求提供隱私選項時，可在「設定 → 隱私權選項」重新開啟表單。選擇可能因地區與服務狀態而不同。目前遊戲版本未要求 Apple 跨 App 追蹤授權。

回報問題

遊戲問題：請提供關卡編號、App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本及重現步驟。如附截圖，請先遮蔽個人資料。

購買問題：請提供購買時間與項目，並先試「恢復購買」。退款請透過 Apple 的 回報問題 頁面申請。

不適當廣告：請提供廣告出現的時間與時區、廣告內容和當時關卡。如廣告提供回報功能，也可直接使用。請勿為回報而點擊廣告。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼、付款資料或私鑰。

FAQ

How do I play?

Tap gates (and valve wheels) to let the water even out between chambers, then tap or drag boats through open gates to the flag of their colour. Water is finite and every opening evens the levels for good, so the order of openings is the puzzle. Boats run aground in shallow water, and tall masts do not fit under low bridges. Fewer taps earn more stars; when stuck, a hint shows the next move.

What if I run out of hints or retries?

Hints and retries each refill one every 5 minutes, up to 10. You can also watch a rewarded ad in the hint shop for 6 hints, or buy a hint pack.

Can I play offline?

Levels work offline, and unavailable ads do not prevent game progress from being saved. Advertising data processing is not fully offline.

Do my purchases carry over to a new phone or a reinstall?

Remove Ads comes back with Settings → Restore Purchases. Hint packs are consumable and cannot be restored. Game progress is stored on the device and is deleted with the app.

How do I review ad privacy choices?

When required by the SDK, open Settings → Privacy Choices. Choices vary by region and service status. The current game version does not request Apple’s cross-app tracking authorization.

Report a problem

Game issues: include the level number, app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Hide personal information in screenshots.

Purchase issues: include the purchase time and item, and try Restore Purchases first. Request refunds through Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Inappropriate ads: include the time and time zone, ad content, and level. You can also use the ad provider’s reporting control if available. Do not click an ad just to report it. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or private keys.
