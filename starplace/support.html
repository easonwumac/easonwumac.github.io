常見問題

安星教什麼？

安星教你親手排出一張紫微斗數命盤：從生月、命宮、身宮到五行局，再安紫微、天府兩組主星、輔星與煞星，最後是生年四化。點錯時，命盤上會畫出數宮的路徑並說明規則。完成後可在「星曜」查看每顆星與十二宮的介紹。

這是算命 App 嗎？

不是。安星介紹紫微斗數這項傳統文化的排盤方法與術語，不提供預測，也不應作為健康、財務、感情或其他決定的依據。

出生時間在晚上 11 點後，或是閏月出生怎麼算？

23:00 之後算隔天的子時。閏月出生時，十五日以前按本月、十六日以後按下個月安生月，課程中會標示採用的月份。出生年份支援 1900 至今。

可以練習別人的命盤嗎？

可以。在「命盤」新增其他人的生辰，或點「練習新的命盤」用隨機生辰練習，並可選擇從哪一章開始。

哪些功能免費？可以購買什麼？

所有課程、命盤與星曜介紹都可免費使用（含廣告）。一次性內購「移除廣告」永久移除廣告；沒有訂閱，價格以 App Store 購買畫面為準。

如何恢復購買或申請退款？

在「設定」點「恢復購買」，並使用原購買 Apple 帳號。購買由 Apple App Store 處理，退款依 Apple 的政策處理。

如何刪除資料？

在「命盤」清單向左滑動或長按命盤即可刪除；刪除 App 會移除所有命盤與進度。資料只存在裝置上。

回報問題

請附上裝置型號、iOS 版本與問題描述；排盤結果有疑問時，請附上出生日期、時間與性別，或改用不屬於任何人的範例生辰。

FAQ

What does Starplace teach?

Starplace teaches you to build a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart by hand: the birth-month palace, the Life and Body palaces and the bureau, then the Zi Wei and Tian Fu star groups, the lucky and harsh stars, and finally the four transformations. When you tap the wrong palace, the board draws the counting path and explains the rule. Afterwards, Stars describes every star and the twelve palaces.

Is this a fortune-telling app?

No. Starplace explains how charts are built in this traditional practice and what its terms mean. It makes no predictions and should not be used for health, financial, relationship or any other decisions.

How are births after 11 p.m. or in a leap month handled?

Births from 23:00 count as the Zi hour of the next day. For a leap month, days 1–15 count as that month and days 16 onward as the next month; the lesson shows which month is used. Birth years from 1900 to today are supported.

Can I practice on other charts?

Yes. Add another birth in Charts, or tap Practice a new chart to use a random birth and choose which chapter to start from.

Which features are free, and what can I buy?

All lessons, charts and star notes are free, with ads. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes ads permanently. There are no subscriptions; the App Store purchase screen shows local prices.

How do I restore a purchase or request a refund?

In Settings, tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Apple App Store processes purchases and handles refunds under its policies.

How do I delete data?

Swipe left on a chart or touch and hold it in the Charts list to delete it; deleting the app removes all charts and progress. Data stays on your device.
