常見問題

怎麼開始？

第一次打開時點「使用我的位置」，或用「選擇城市」搜尋你所在的城市，然後把手機舉向天空。畫面會跟著手機轉動；也可以用手指拖曳、雙指縮放瀏覽星空，點下方的指向按鈕回到跟隨手機。

畫面的方向和實際天空對不上怎麼辦？

指南針容易受附近金屬、磁鐵或手機殼影響。請遠離金屬物品，拿著手機畫幾次 8 字形；仍有偏差時，到「設定」點「校正指南針」，把月亮或一顆亮星對到畫面中央即可。

為什麼看不到國際太空站等人造衛星？

人造衛星位置需要從網路下載最新的軌道資料（最多每 2 小時一次）。請確認裝置已連上網路，稍後再開啟 App。

夜視模式是什麼？

夜視模式把整個畫面改成暗紅色，讓眼睛在戶外維持對黑暗的適應。可在「設定」或「圖層」中開關。

可以購買什麼？如何還原購買？

所有功能都可免費使用（含廣告）。一次性內購「移除廣告」可永久移除廣告；沒有訂閱，價格以 App Store 購買畫面為準。要還原購買，請到「設定」點「還原購買」，並使用原購買的 Apple 帳號。

如何申請退款？

購買由 Apple App Store 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊。退款依 Apple 的政策處理。

星空資料來自哪裡？

恆星與深空天體資料來自 Wikidata（CC0），星座連線來自 XEphem（MIT 授權），星名來自國際天文學聯合會 IAU 的 Catalog of Star Names（CC BY 4.0），銀河影像為 ESO/S. Brunier 的〈The Milky Way panorama〉（CC BY 4.0，經修改），月球貼圖來自 NASA Scientific Visualization Studio，人造衛星軌道來自 CelesTrak。完整標示見 App 的「設定 › 致謝」。

FAQ

How do I start?

On first launch, tap Use My Location, or Choose a City to search for where you are, then hold your phone up to the sky. The view follows the phone; you can also drag and pinch to browse, and tap the pointing button at the bottom to follow the phone again.

The view does not line up with the real sky. What can I do?

The compass is easily thrown off by nearby metal, magnets or phone cases. Move away from metal objects and wave the phone in a figure eight a few times. If it is still off, open Settings, tap Calibrate Compass and put the Moon or a bright star in the middle of the screen.

Why can’t I see the International Space Station or other satellites?

Satellite positions need fresh orbital data from the internet (downloaded at most once every two hours). Make sure the device is online and open the app again later.

What is Night Vision?

Night Vision turns the whole screen dark red so your eyes stay adapted to the dark outdoors. Turn it on or off in Settings or Layers.

What can I buy, and how do I restore a purchase?

Every feature is free, with ads. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes ads permanently; there are no subscriptions, and the App Store purchase screen shows local prices. To restore a purchase, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made it.

How do I request a refund?

Apple App Store processes purchases, and we never receive your payment details. Apple handles refunds under its policies.

Where does the sky data come from?

Stars and deep-sky objects come from Wikidata (CC0), constellation figures from XEphem (MIT License), star names from the IAU Catalog of Star Names (CC BY 4.0), the Milky Way image is “The Milky Way panorama” by ESO/S. Brunier (CC BY 4.0, modified), the Moon map comes from the NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, and satellite orbits from CelesTrak. Full credits are in the app under Settings › Acknowledgements.
