摘要

尋星 Nightvane 是星空導覽 App：把手機舉向天空，就會依你的位置、時間與手機朝向畫出眼前的星星、行星、星座、月亮與人造衛星。App 不需要帳號，我們沒有自己的伺服器接收你的資料。

位置

位置只用來決定要畫哪一片天空。你可以選擇「使用我的位置」（僅在使用 App 時取得，並四捨五入到約 1 公里），或用「選擇城市」手動搜尋城市而不開啟定位。取得位置時，App 會請 Apple 地圖服務把位置轉成城市名稱；搜尋城市時，你輸入的文字會交由 Apple 地圖服務提供建議。四捨五入後的座標與城市名稱只存在裝置上。

動作感測器

陀螺儀、加速度計與磁力計（指南針）的資料只在裝置上即時使用，用來判斷手機指向的方向，不會儲存或傳送。

儲存在裝置上的資料

所選地點、圖層與夜視模式設定、指南針校正值，以及人造衛星軌道資料的快取都儲存在裝置上。刪除 App 即會一併刪除。

人造衛星軌道資料

為顯示國際太空站等人造衛星的位置，App 會向 CelesTrak（celestrak.org）下載公開的軌道根數，最多每 2 小時一次。請求不包含你的位置或任何個人資料，但與任何網路連線一樣，對方伺服器會看到你的 IP 位址。

廣告與追蹤

尋星使用 Google AdMob 在「今晚」與「搜尋」面板底部顯示橫幅廣告，並在關閉這些面板時偶爾顯示插頁廣告；星空畫面上不放廣告，第一次使用時也不顯示廣告。App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。在適用地區，App 會在載入廣告前透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）詢問廣告同意，之後可在「設定」中的「隱私選項」變更。你的位置不會傳給廣告服務。

AdMob 可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。購買「移除廣告」後不再顯示廣告。

購買

所有功能都可免費使用（含廣告）。一次性內購「移除廣告」可永久移除廣告；沒有訂閱，價格以 App Store 購買畫面為準。購買由 Apple App Store 處理，App 使用 StoreKit 確認購買權益，我們不會取得你的付款資訊。退款依 Apple 的政策處理。要還原購買，請到「設定」點「還原購買」，並使用原購買的 Apple 帳號。

第三方服務

Apple 地圖服務處理城市名稱與搜尋，Apple App Store 處理購買，Google AdMob 與 UMP 處理廣告與廣告同意，CelesTrak 提供人造衛星軌道資料。各服務受其自身的條款與隱私權政策約束。

兒童

本 App 並非專為兒童設計，我們不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

資料刪除

刪除 App 即會移除裝置上所有 App 資料。我們沒有保存你的任何資料；如曾寄送客服郵件，可寄信要求刪除往來郵件。

Summary

Nightvane is a night-sky guide: hold your phone up to the sky and it draws the stars, planets, constellations, the Moon and satellites in front of you from your location, the time and where the phone points. The app needs no account, and we run no server that receives your data.

Location

Location only decides which sky to draw. Choose Use My Location (read only while you use the app and rounded to about 1 km), or Choose a City to search for a city without turning on location. When it reads your location, the app asks Apple’s map service for the city name; when you search for a city, what you type goes to Apple’s map service for suggestions. The rounded coordinates and the city name stay on your device.

Motion sensors

Gyroscope, accelerometer and magnetometer (compass) readings are used live on the device to tell where the phone points. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The chosen place, layer and night-vision settings, the compass correction and a cache of satellite orbital data are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Satellite orbital data

To show satellites such as the International Space Station, the app downloads public orbital elements from CelesTrak (celestrak.org), at most once every two hours. The request carries no location or personal data, but like any internet connection it shows your IP address to that server.

Ads and tracking

Nightvane uses Google AdMob to show a banner at the bottom of the Tonight and Search panels and, now and then, an interstitial ad when you close those panels. There are no ads over the sky and none in your first session. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. Where required, the app asks for advertising consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before loading ads; you can change it later under Settings → Privacy Choices. Your location is never sent to the ad service.

AdMob may process IP address, device and ad-interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure. Ads stop after you buy Remove Ads.

Purchases

Every feature is free, with ads. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes ads permanently; there are no subscriptions, and the App Store purchase screen shows local prices. Apple App Store processes purchases; the app uses StoreKit to verify them and never receives your payment details. Apple handles refunds under its policies. To restore a purchase, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made it.

Third-party services

Apple’s map service handles city names and search, Apple App Store handles purchases, Google AdMob and UMP handle ads and ad consent, and CelesTrak provides satellite orbital data. Each service is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. We keep no data about you; if you emailed support, you may ask us to delete that correspondence.
