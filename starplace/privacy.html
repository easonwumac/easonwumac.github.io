摘要

安星 Starplace 是學習紫微斗數排盤的 App：輸入生辰後，一步步把十二宮、主星、輔星、煞星與生年四化安到命盤上。App 不需要帳號，你輸入的資料不會傳到我們的伺服器。

資料存放

你輸入的名字、出生日期與時間、性別及學習進度只儲存在裝置上，用於計算命盤與保存進度；裝置備份（iCloud 或電腦）可能依 Apple 設定包含這些資料。App 不使用 iCloud 同步，也不讀取你的聯絡人、照片或位置。

廣告與追蹤

安星使用 Google AdMob 在畫面底部顯示橫幅廣告，並在完成章節時偶爾顯示插頁廣告。App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。在適用地區，App 會在載入廣告前透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）詢問廣告同意，之後可在「設定」中的「隱私選項」變更。你的名字、生辰與命盤不會傳給廣告服務。

AdMob 可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。購買「移除廣告」後不再顯示廣告。

購買

所有課程、命盤與星曜介紹都可免費使用（含廣告）。一次性內購「移除廣告」永久移除廣告；沒有訂閱，價格以 App Store 購買畫面為準。購買由 Apple App Store 處理，App 使用 StoreKit 確認購買權益；我們不在 App 中取得付款卡號。退款依 Apple 的政策處理。

在「設定」點「恢復購買」，並使用原購買 Apple 帳號。

第三方服務

Apple App Store 處理購買，StoreKit 用於確認購買權益；Google AdMob 與 UMP 處理廣告與廣告同意。

資料刪除

在「命盤」清單向左滑動或長按命盤即可刪除；刪除 App 會移除所有命盤與進度。裝置備份可能另依 Apple 設定保留。客服郵件可寄信要求刪除。

聯絡我們

隱私或服務問題請寄信至 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你寄送客服電子郵件，我們會使用你提供的信箱及內容回覆與處理問題。

Summary

Starplace teaches how a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart is built: enter a birth date and time, then place the twelve palaces, the major stars, the lucky and harsh stars and the four transformations step by step. The app needs no account, and what you enter is never sent to our servers.

Data stored

Names, birth dates and times, gender and lesson progress you enter are stored only on your device, to build charts and keep your progress. Device backups (iCloud or a computer) may include them under your Apple settings. The app does not use iCloud sync and does not read your contacts, photos or location.

Ads and tracking

Starplace uses Google AdMob to show a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and an occasional interstitial ad when you finish a chapter. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. Where required, the app asks for advertising consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before loading ads; you can change it later under Settings → Privacy choices. Names, birth details and charts are never sent to the ad service.

AdMob may process IP address, device and ad-interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure. Ads stop after you buy Remove Ads.

Purchases

All lessons, charts and star notes are free, with ads. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes ads permanently. There are no subscriptions; the App Store purchase screen shows local prices. Apple App Store processes purchases. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlements and does not receive your payment card number. Apple handles refunds under its policies.

In Settings, tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Third-party services

Apple App Store handles purchases, and StoreKit verifies entitlements; Google AdMob and UMP handle ads and ad consent.

Deleting your data

Swipe left on a chart or touch and hold it in the Charts list to delete it; deleting the app removes all charts and progress. Device backups may remain under your Apple settings. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Contact

For privacy or service questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you email support, we use the email address and content you provide to reply and resolve the issue.
