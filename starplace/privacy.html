摘要

尋星 Nightvane 是星空導覽 App：把手機舉向天空，就會依你的位置、時間與手機朝向畫出眼前的星星、行星、星座、月亮與人造衛星。App 不需要帳號；除了你在「傳送意見」中主動送出的內容，我們沒有自己的伺服器接收你的資料。

位置

位置只用來決定要畫哪一片天空。你可以選擇「使用我的位置」（僅在使用 App 時取得，並四捨五入到約 1 公里），或用「選擇城市」手動搜尋城市而不開啟定位。取得位置時，App 會請 Apple 地圖服務把位置轉成城市名稱；搜尋城市時，你輸入的文字會交由 Apple 地圖服務提供建議。四捨五入後的座標與城市名稱只存在裝置上。

動作感測器

陀螺儀、加速度計與磁力計（指南針）的資料只在裝置上即時使用，用來判斷手機指向的方向，不會儲存或傳送。

相機

只有在你點星空畫面上方的相機按鈕時，App 才會請求相機權限，並把後鏡頭的即時畫面放在星星後面。畫面只在裝置上顯示，不會拍照、錄影、儲存或傳送。

雲量預報

「今晚」的雲量來自 Apple 天氣（WeatherKit）：App 會把四捨五入到約 1 公里的座標傳給 Apple 天氣以取得逐時預報，同一地點最多每小時一次。我們不會收到這些資料。

觀測紀錄與 Game Center

觀測紀錄（看過、找到的天體與日期）只儲存在裝置上。排行榜使用 Apple Game Center：只有在你點「排行榜」後才會登入 Game Center，之後 App 會把「對準實際天空找到的天體數量」上傳到 Game Center 排行榜，並依 Game Center 的設定顯示你的暱稱。分享卡片只有在你點「分享」並選擇對象時才會送出。

鎖定畫面倒數與天空提醒

鎖定畫面倒數（即時動態）與天空提醒都在裝置上排程，不經過我們的伺服器。

儲存在裝置上的資料

所選地點、圖層與夜視模式設定、指南針校正值、觀測紀錄，以及人造衛星軌道資料、雲量預報與「更多 App」清單的快取都儲存在裝置上。刪除 App 即會一併刪除。

設定中的「傳送意見」會打開意見表單，可附上最多 3 張你選的圖片。只有你按下「送出」時，才會把你的文字、圖片、選填的 Email、App 版本、裝置型號與 iOS 版本傳到開發者的伺服器 agentmesh.uk（由 Cloudflare 託管），轉成開發者私人 GitHub 儲存庫中的一則待辦；文字轉成待辦後即從伺服器刪除，圖片存放在只有該待辦才有連結的網址，90 天後刪除。每日送出次數以當天更換的 IP 雜湊計算，隔日刪除。內容只用於回覆與改善 App，請勿附上敏感資料，可來信要求提早刪除。廣告位置沒有廣告可顯示時，會改顯示一款我們自己的 App，不另外傳送資料。

人造衛星軌道資料

為顯示國際太空站等人造衛星的位置，App 會向 CelesTrak（celestrak.org）下載公開的軌道根數，最多每 2 小時一次。請求不包含你的位置或任何個人資料，但與任何網路連線一樣，對方伺服器會看到你的 IP 位址。

廣告與追蹤

尋星使用 Google AdMob 在「今晚」與「搜尋」面板底部顯示橫幅廣告，並在關閉這些面板時偶爾顯示插頁廣告；星空畫面上不放廣告，第一次使用時也不顯示廣告。App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。App 只請求非個人化廣告，因此不顯示 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）的廣告同意表單。你的位置不會傳給廣告服務。

AdMob 可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。購買「移除廣告」後不再顯示廣告。

購買

所有功能都可免費使用（含廣告）。一次性內購「移除廣告」可永久移除廣告；沒有訂閱，價格以 App Store 購買畫面為準。購買由 Apple App Store 處理，App 使用 StoreKit 確認購買權益，我們不會取得你的付款資訊。退款依 Apple 的政策處理。要還原購買，請到「設定」點「還原購買」，並使用原購買的 Apple 帳號。

第三方服務

Apple 地圖服務處理城市名稱與搜尋，Apple 天氣提供雲量預報，Apple Game Center 處理排行榜，Apple App Store 處理購買；設定中的「更多 App」會從我們的網站（agentmesh.uk）與 Apple 的 iTunes 查詢服務讀取我們其他 App 的公開清單（不含個人資料，對方會看到你的 IP 位址）。Google AdMob 處理廣告，CelesTrak 提供人造衛星軌道資料。各服務受其自身的條款與隱私權政策約束。

兒童

本 App 並非專為兒童設計，我們不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

資料刪除

刪除 App 即會移除裝置上所有 App 資料。我們沒有保存你的任何資料；如曾寄送客服郵件，可寄信要求刪除往來郵件。

聯絡我們

隱私或服務問題請寄信至 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你寄送客服電子郵件，我們會使用你提供的信箱及內容回覆與處理問題。

Summary

Nightvane is a night-sky guide: hold your phone up to the sky and it draws the stars, planets, constellations, the Moon and satellites in front of you from your location, the time and where the phone points. The app needs no account, and apart from what you choose to send with Send feedback, we run no server that receives your data.

Location

Location only decides which sky to draw. Choose Use My Location (read only while you use the app and rounded to about 1 km), or Choose a City to search for a city without turning on location. When it reads your location, the app asks Apple’s map service for the city name; when you search for a city, what you type goes to Apple’s map service for suggestions. The rounded coordinates and the city name stay on your device.

Motion sensors

Gyroscope, accelerometer and magnetometer (compass) readings are used live on the device to tell where the phone points. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

Camera

The app asks for the camera only when you tap the camera button above the sky, and then shows the back camera’s live picture behind the stars. The picture is only shown on your device; nothing is photographed, recorded, stored or sent.

Cloud forecast

Tonight’s cloud cover comes from Apple Weather (WeatherKit): the app sends coordinates rounded to about 1 km to Apple Weather for an hourly forecast, at most once an hour per place. We do not receive this data.

Observation log and Game Center

The observation log (objects you have seen or found, with dates) stays on your device. The leaderboard uses Apple Game Center: the app signs in to Game Center only after you tap Leaderboard, then reports the number of objects you found by pointing at the real sky to the Game Center leaderboard, where your nickname appears as your Game Center settings allow. A share card leaves the app only when you tap Share and pick where it goes.

Lock-screen countdown and sky alerts

The lock-screen countdown (Live Activity) and sky alerts are scheduled on your device, without our servers.

Data stored on your device

The chosen place, layer and night-vision settings, the compass correction, the observation log, and caches of satellite orbital data, the cloud forecast and the More Apps list are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Satellite orbital data

To show satellites such as the International Space Station, the app downloads public orbital elements from CelesTrak (celestrak.org), at most once every two hours. The request carries no location or personal data, but like any internet connection it shows your IP address to that server.

Ads and tracking

Nightvane uses Google AdMob to show a banner at the bottom of the Tonight and Search panels and, now and then, an interstitial ad when you close those panels. There are no ads over the sky and none in your first session. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. The app requests non-personalized ads only, so it shows no Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form. Your location is never sent to the ad service.

AdMob may process IP address, device and ad-interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure. Ads stop after you buy Remove Ads.

Purchases

Every feature is free, with ads. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes ads permanently; there are no subscriptions, and the App Store purchase screen shows local prices. Apple App Store processes purchases; the app uses StoreKit to verify them and never receives your payment details. Apple handles refunds under its policies. To restore a purchase, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made it.

Third-party services

Apple’s map service handles city names and search, Apple Weather provides the cloud forecast, Apple Game Center runs the leaderboard, Apple App Store handles purchases; More Apps in Settings reads the public list of our other apps from our site (agentmesh.uk) and Apple’s iTunes lookup service (no personal data; they see your IP address); Google AdMob handles ads, and CelesTrak provides satellite orbital data. Each service is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

Send feedback in Settings opens a form where you can attach up to 3 pictures you choose. Only when you tap Send do your text, pictures, the email you may add, the app version, device model and iOS version go to the developer’s server agentmesh.uk (hosted on Cloudflare), which files them as an item in the developer’s private GitHub repository. The text is deleted from the server once filed; pictures stay at an address only that item links to and are deleted after 90 days. The daily send limit counts a hash of your IP address that changes every day and is deleted the next day. It is used only to reply and improve the app; please leave out sensitive information, and email us to have it deleted sooner. When the ad space has no ad to show, it shows one of our own apps instead, with nothing more sent.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. We keep no data about you; if you emailed support, you may ask us to delete that correspondence.
