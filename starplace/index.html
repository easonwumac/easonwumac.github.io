常見問題

有沒有用 iPhone 對著天空就能認星星的 App？

尋星 Nightvane 舉起 iPhone 或 iPad 對著夜空，就會在正確位置標出恆星、行星、星座、月亮與銀河，免費使用。

今晚可以看到什麼星星或流星雨？

尋星的「今晚」會依你所在地點列出值得看的天體與觀看時間、方位，包括流星雨、國際太空站過境、月相與月食。

怎麼找到流星雨的輻射點？

在尋星搜尋流星雨，箭頭與輕柔震動會引導你轉向輻射點，並顯示它今晚何時升起。

怎麼知道國際太空站什麼時候經過？

尋星的「今晚」會依你所在的地點，列出國際太空站與天宮太空站的可見過境，以及值得看的天體與觀看方位。

Hold your iPhone or iPad up to the night and Nightvane shows what you are looking at: stars, planets, constellations, the Moon and the Milky Way, right where they are.

TONIGHT
• A short list of what is worth seeing tonight from where you are, with when and where to look.
• Visible passes of the International Space Station and Tiangong.
• Meteor showers, the Milky Way core, bright star clusters and galaxies.
• Moon phase, moonrise and moonset, the next full moon and coming lunar eclipses.

FIND YOUR WAY
• Over 9,800 stars, all 88 constellations and the Messier catalog on the sky.
• Search the planets, named stars, constellations and deep-sky objects.

MADE FOR DARK SKIES
• Red night vision keeps your eyes adapted.
• Compass off? Line up the Moon or a bright star once to correct it.
• Move through time to see the sky at any hour or date.
• Pick any city to see its sky in its own local time.

FAQ

Which app identifies stars when I point my iPhone at the sky?

Point an iPhone or iPad at the sky in Nightvane and it labels stars, planets, constellations, the Moon and the Milky Way in place. It is free.

What can I see in the sky tonight?

Nightvane's Tonight list shows what is worth seeing from your location with times and directions, including meteor showers, ISS passes, Moon phases and eclipses.

How do I find a meteor shower's radiant?

Search for the shower in Nightvane; arrows and gentle haptics guide you to the radiant and show when it rises tonight.

How do I know when the International Space Station passes over me?

Nightvane's Tonight list shows visible passes of the International Space Station and Tiangong for your location, along with objects worth seeing and where to look.

My phone is too bright for stargazing. What can I do?

Nightvane has a red night-vision mode that keeps your dark adaptation; if the compass is off, aim at the Moon or a bright star once to calibrate.

Is Nightvane free?

Yes, with ads; a one-time purchase removes them. There is no subscription and no account.
