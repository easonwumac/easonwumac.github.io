摘要

鄉民是 PTT 論壇的閱讀器，不是 PTT 官方 App。閱讀不需要帳號；我們不建立帳號、不收集姓名，除非你在許願池自己留下 Email，也不販售你的資料。

閱讀與 PTT 帳號

文章、看板與推文直接從 PTT 公開網頁（www.ptt.cc）讀取。登入時，帳號密碼只存在裝置的鑰匙圈，並透過 PTT 官方的 WebSocket 終端連線直接送到 PTT，不經過我們的伺服器。推文、發文、站內信、水球、好友與投票都在你的裝置與 PTT 之間進行。

存在你裝置與 iCloud 的資料

我的最愛、封鎖名單、追蹤、設定、使用者備註、閱讀紀錄與收藏，保存在 App 資料中。若 iCloud 開啟，這些資料會透過 Apple 的 iCloud 鍵值儲存與 CloudKit 私人資料庫，在你的裝置間同步；我們無法讀取你的 iCloud 資料。

我們的伺服器

部分功能使用我們架在 Cloudflare 的伺服器（folkline-api.easonwumac.workers.dev）：熱門排行、鄉民日報、即時文章與鄉民 AI 分析；ptt.cc 連不上時，也會改由伺服器代為讀取同一個公開頁面。每次寫入都附一個只存在此次安裝的匿名 App Attest 金鑰簽章，由 Apple 確認來自真實裝置上的本 App；伺服器只保存金鑰公開部分與使用次數，用來計算 AI 額度。

App 每天最多一次，把 Apple MetricKit 統計的啟動時間、卡頓、記憶體用量與當機次數，以 App 版本為單位傳到同一台伺服器，用來改善效能；這些只是數字，不含你的帳號、閱讀內容或任何可辨識你的資訊。鄉民沒有安裝任何第三方分析 SDK。

鄉民 AI 分析：你按下分析或提問時，文章內容、推文與你輸入的問題會送到伺服器，再交給 Anthropic 的 Claude 模型產生結果；不含你的 PTT 帳號。結果快取最多一天，供同篇文章重複使用；Anthropic 依其商業條款處理資料，不用於訓練模型。裝置上的重點整理使用 Apple Intelligence，在裝置上完成。

追蹤通知：你開啟通知後，推播權杖、你追蹤的作者、文章、關鍵字，以及你封鎖的 PTT ID 會存在伺服器，用來偵測新推文並透過 Apple 推播服務通知你，且不通知或引用封鎖對象。關閉通知或刪除全部追蹤會一併刪除這些資料。

檢舉：你檢舉文章時，伺服器記錄看板、文章代號、作者 ID 與理由，並在開發者的私人 GitHub 儲存庫建立一則只有開發者看得到的 issue，供我們在 24 小時內審查；紀錄保存 180 天。檢舉不含你的帳號或聯絡方式。

許願池：你送出願望時，伺服器保存願望內容、你選填的聯絡方式與 App 版本，並在開發者的私人 GitHub 儲存庫建立一則只有開發者看得到的 issue，用來規劃功能與回覆你。需要刪除請來信。

圖片上傳：你在設定填入自己的 Imgur 或 ImgBB 金鑰後，從照片選的圖片會由裝置直接傳到該圖床、算在你的帳號下，不經過我們的伺服器；金鑰只存在裝置的鑰匙圈。

Pro 訂閱：伺服器以 App Store 交易代號確認訂閱狀態與 AI 額度，不含付款資訊。

廣告

免費版在看板與文章列表顯示 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告。鄉民不要求 App 追蹤透明度權限，廣告請求為非個人化；Google 仍可能使用 App 內容、大致位置、IP 位址、裝置資訊、廣告互動與診斷資料。因為只請求非個人化廣告，App 不顯示廣告同意表單。訂閱鄉民 Pro 後不再顯示廣告。

橫幅無法載入時，版位可能顯示我們其他 App 的 App Store 連結，不依個人資料挑選。「更多 App」清單由 agentmesh.uk 提供，不傳送個人資料。

文章與推文裡的圖片、影片縮圖（例如 Imgur、YouTube）由你的裝置直接向該網站載入，和瀏覽器開啟這些連結一樣，該網站會看到你的 IP 位址；設定可關閉圖片或改為點了才載入。

購買

鄉民 Pro 為自動續訂訂閱（月或年），包含移除廣告。價格以 App 內 App Store 資訊為準，可在「我的 › 鄉民 Pro」回復購買，並在 App Store 帳號設定取消訂閱。使用條款：Apple 標準 EULA。

資料刪除

刪除 App 會刪除裝置上的資料；iCloud 中的資料可在 iOS 設定 › Apple 帳號 › iCloud 管理。伺服器上的追蹤與推播權杖會在關閉通知時刪除；其他刪除需求請來信。

Summary

Folkline is a reader for the PTT forum and is not an official PTT app. Reading needs no account; we create no accounts, collect no names, no emails unless you leave one in a wish, and never sell your data.

Reading and your PTT account

Posts, boards and pushes are read from PTT's public web pages (www.ptt.cc). When you log in, your ID and password are kept only in the device keychain and sent straight to PTT over its official WebSocket terminal, never through our servers. Pushing, posting, mail, waterballs, friends and polls happen between your device and PTT.

Data on your device and in iCloud

Favourites, blocked users, follows, settings, user notes, reading history and saved posts are kept in the app's data. With iCloud on, they sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage and a private CloudKit database; we cannot read your iCloud data.

Our server

Some features use our server on Cloudflare (folkline-api.easonwumac.workers.dev): hot rankings, the daily paper, live posts and Folkline AI Analysis; when ptt.cc can’t be reached, the server reads the same public page instead. Every write is signed with an anonymous App Attest key that exists only in this installation, which Apple confirms comes from this app on a real device; the server keeps only the key's public part and a use counter, to count AI quota.

At most once a day the app sends Apple MetricKit figures (launch time, hangs, memory use and crash counts) per app version to the same server, to improve performance. They are numbers only, without your account, what you read or anything that identifies you. Folkline includes no third-party analytics SDK.

Folkline AI Analysis: when you ask for an analysis or ask a question, the post, its pushes and your question go to our server and on to Anthropic's Claude model; your PTT account is not included. Results are cached for up to a day for the same post; Anthropic processes the data under its commercial terms and does not train on it. On-device key points use Apple Intelligence on the device.

Follow notifications: when you turn notifications on, the push token, the authors, posts and keywords you follow, and the PTT IDs you blocked are stored on the server to detect new pushes and notify you through Apple Push Notification service. Turning notifications off or removing every follow deletes them.

Reports: when you report a post, the server records the board, post ID, author ID and reason, and files them as an issue in the developer's private GitHub repository so we review it within 24 hours; records are kept for 180 days. A report carries no account or contact of yours.

Wishing well: when you send a wish, the server keeps its text, the contact you chose to leave and the app version, and files it as an issue in the developer's private GitHub repository that only the developer can see, to plan features and reply to you. Email us to have it deleted.

Picture upload: once you enter your own Imgur or ImgBB key in Settings, a picture you pick goes from the device straight to that host under your account, never through our server; the key stays in the device keychain.

Pro subscription: the server checks the subscription and AI quota by the App Store transaction ID, never payment details.

Ads

The free version shows Google AdMob banners in board and post lists. Folkline does not ask for App Tracking Transparency and requests non-personalized ads; Google may still use app content, approximate location, IP address, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics. Because it requests non-personalized ads only, the app shows no ad consent form. Folkline Pro turns ads off.

When a banner cannot load, the slot may show App Store links to our other apps, never chosen from personal data. The More apps list comes from agentmesh.uk and sends no personal data.

Pictures and video thumbnails linked in posts and pushes (Imgur, YouTube and the like) load from those sites directly to your device, as they would in a browser, so those sites see your IP address; Settings can turn pictures off or load them only on tap.

Purchases

Folkline Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) that also removes ads. Prices are shown in the app from the App Store. Restore purchases in Me › Folkline Pro and cancel in your App Store account settings. Terms of Use: Apple standard EULA.

Deleting data

Deleting the app deletes its data on the device; manage iCloud data in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud. Follows and the push token on our server are deleted when you turn notifications off; email us for anything else.
