Folkline

Folkline is a forum reader with PTT support: AI reads the mood of a thread for you, and posts, pushes and the editor never show ads.

Free on the App StoreSupportFeedback

Features

Hot and boards

  • Hot posts and hot boards grouped by topic: news, sports, money, tech, games…
  • Post categories shown as tags, push counts and new pushes at a glance
  • Favourites, recent boards, categories and forgiving board search

Reading

  • Pushes read like a chat, with the author's own pushes marked
  • Filter by up, down, note, author, images or one ID; push chart and floor scrubber
  • ASCII art and tables keep their layout, or show a whole post on the BBS's own 80-column grid
  • Busy threads follow new pushes live; posts in other languages translate on the device
  • Previous and next post; saved posts keep snapshots even after the original is deleted

AI analysis

  • On-device key points on devices with Apple Intelligence and iOS 26 or later
  • Folkline AI Analysis: each side's stance, main arguments and how the mood shifted

With a PTT account

  • Push, post and reply, each read back to confirm it went through
  • Mail, friends online, waterballs and board polls
  • Switch between accounts in one tap
  • Upload pictures from Photos with your own Imgur or ImgBB key

Also

  • Banner ads only in lists and settings: never inside a post, its pushes, a letter or the editor, and no full-screen ads
  • Follow authors, posts and keywords and get notified of new posts and pushes
  • iPad board wall: boards and live threads side by side as columns
  • Daily paper, widgets and Apple Watch
  • 28 colour styles or your own page colours, title weight and light or dark as you like
  • Wishing well: tell the developer what you'd like next
  • Settings, history and saved posts sync between iPhone and iPad through iCloud

FAQ

Is there a good PTT reader app?

Folkline is a forum reader with PTT support: hot posts and boards grouped by topic, pushes that read like a chat and ASCII art kept in its layout; posts, pushes and the editor never show ads, and with a PTT account you can push, post and read mail.

What does Folkline's AI analysis do?

It sums up the sides, main arguments and shifts in mood of a thread. Devices with Apple Intelligence get free on-device key points; cloud analysis is 20 a month free or 50 a week with Folkline Pro, and runs only after you allow it.
