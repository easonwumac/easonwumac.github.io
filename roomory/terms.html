Purpose and professional review

Roomory helps measure, plan, and produce interior drawings. Scans can be inaccurate. Verify measurements on site before construction and have qualified professionals review structure, electrical loads, plumbing, codes, and safety. App drawings are not professional approval or authority permits.

Purchases

Apple App Store processes purchases. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlements and does not receive your payment card number. Apple handles refunds under its policies.

Roomory Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly). Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and the subscription renews and is charged within 24 hours before the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings; no refund is given for the unused part of a period that has started. The earlier one-time purchases are no longer sold; people who bought one keep what it unlocked.

AI assistant

Each subscription has 150 AI requests a month. The answers are generated by Anthropic's Claude and may be wrong or unsuitable for your space; layouts, quote items and defect notes are drafts for you to check before use, and construction matters are for qualified professionals to judge. Do not send content you have no right to provide.

Data and responsibility

You must have suitable rights to enter and share property, client, and design information. Keep backups of important projects. Check drawings for completeness and suitability before construction or delivery to others.
