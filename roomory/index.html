從重新擺放家具，到準備整戶裝潢，居圖陪你把現有的空間，整理成心中的配置。掃描房間或手動開始，在平面圖上調整，再用 3D 預覽看看想法。

從一間房，規劃到整個家
使用配備 LiDAR 的 iPhone 或 iPad 掃描房間，記錄牆面、門窗與家具。也可以手動建立多房間、多樓層專案，在整戶總圖上調整房間位置。不支援 LiDAR 的裝置，仍可手動建立與編修平面圖。

把想法放進平面圖
分開查看建築、家具與設備圖層，調整物件的尺寸、位置與角度，安排燈具、插座與給排水路線。切換 3D 配置，換上不同材質，看看家具擺放與日照時段帶來的變化，讓屋主與設計、裝潢工作者更方便討論。

留下設計的每一步
重新掃描時，先核對新舊牆面位置，再確認合併，保留既有編修並建立版本備份。你也可以保存設計版本、備份與匯入專案，並透過 iCloud 同步專案。iCloud 同步需登入 iCloud 並允許居圖使用 iCloud。

免費使用，需要更多時訂閱居圖 Pro
掃描、建模、平面編修、多專案、專案備份、iCloud 同步、基本 3D 傢具與材質都可免費使用（含廣告）。居圖 Pro 是自動續訂的訂閱（月繳或年繳），包含：
• 專業匯出：A3 PDF 圖組、可編修 DXF、SVG、USDZ 與 GLB 模型、高解析渲染圖、報價單與驗收報告 PDF、3D 環繞影片
• 全部傢具與材質：再多 36 件 3D 傢具與 29 種實拍材質
• AI 助理：一句話排出家具配置、草擬報價項目、描述驗收照片，每月 150 次（中國大陸不提供）
• 無廣告
訂閱會自動續訂，除非在當期結束前至少 24 小時取消；可在 App Store 帳號設定中管理或取消。先前的一次性購買仍保有原本解鎖的內容。使用 AI 時，你輸入的文字、房間尺寸與物件，以及要描述的照片會傳到我們的伺服器並交由 Anthropic 的 Claude 處理。

房間掃描需要配備 LiDAR 的 iPhone 或 iPad。掃描尺寸需現場複測；施工前，圖面仍須由合格專業人員審核，居圖不提供專業簽核。

搬家或重新擺家具前，怎麼先在手機上試擺？

在居圖 Roomory 的平面圖上調整家具尺寸、位置與角度，切到 3D 看擺放、材質與日照時段的效果，也能保存多個設計版本比較。

From moving a sofa to planning a renovation, Roomory helps you turn the space you have into the layout you want. Scan a room or start by hand, then edit your floor plans and preview your ideas in 3D.

Plan one room or a whole home
Use a LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad to scan walls, doors, windows, and furniture. You can also create projects by hand, add rooms and floors, and adjust room positions in the whole-home plan. Devices without LiDAR can still create and edit plans manually.

Give your ideas a place
View architecture, furniture, and services in separate layers. Adjust sizes, positions, and angles, then place lights, outlets, and plumbing routes. Switch to the 3D layout to try materials and different daylight settings. Keep your ideas together for conversations with homeowners, designers, and renovation professionals.

Keep each step of your design
When you scan again, check the old and new wall positions before merging. Existing edits are preserved and a version backup is created. Save design versions, export and import project backups, and sync projects with iCloud. iCloud sync requires an iCloud account with access enabled for Roomory.

Free to use, with Roomory Pro when you need more
Scanning, modeling, plan editing, multiple projects, project backups, iCloud sync, starter 3D furniture and materials are free, with ads. Roomory Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, that adds:
• Pro Export: A3 PDF drawing sets, editable DXF, SVG, USDZ and GLB models, full-resolution renders, quote and inspection PDFs, and orbit videos
• All furniture and materials: 36 more 3D pieces and 29 photographed materials
• AI assistant: lay out a room from one sentence, draft quote items and describe inspection photos, 150 requests a month (not in China mainland)
• No ads
The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. Earlier one-time purchases keep what they unlocked. AI requests send the text you enter, the room's dimensions and objects, and the photo you ask to describe to our server and Anthropic's Claude.

Room scanning requires a LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad. Scan dimensions need on-site checks. A qualified professional must review plans before construction; Roomory does not provide professional sign-off.

FAQ

Which app scans a room with an iPhone to make a floor plan?

Roomory scans rooms with a LiDAR iPhone or iPad, capturing walls, doors, windows and furniture into an editable floor plan with a 3D preview; devices without LiDAR can draw plans by hand.

How can I try a furniture layout on my phone before moving things?

In Roomory, adjust furniture size, position and angle on the plan, switch to 3D to see layout, materials and daylight, and save design versions to compare.

Is Roomory free?

Scanning, modelling, plan editing, multiple projects, backups, iCloud sync and basic 3D furniture are free with ads. Roomory Pro (monthly or yearly) adds PDF, DXF, SVG and USDZ export, all furniture and materials, the AI assistant and no ads.

Can I draw a floor plan on an iPhone without LiDAR?

Yes. Roomory lets you create and edit floor plans by hand on devices without LiDAR, including multi-room and multi-floor projects; only room scanning needs an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR.

How can I show my renovation layout to a designer or contractor?

In Roomory you adjust building, furniture and fixture layers, then switch to 3D to try materials and daylight; Roomory Pro exports A3 PDF sets, editable DXF and 3D models.

Are room scan measurements accurate enough to build from?

Roomory's scanned dimensions should be re-measured on site, and drawings still need review by a qualified professional before work starts; Roomory does not provide professional sign-off.
