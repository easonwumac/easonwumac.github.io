摘要

居圖 Roomory 提供室內空間掃描、居家規劃、量測、配置與設計圖組製作工具。App 使用相機與 LiDAR 建立室內空間資料；相機畫面用於掃描，不會由我們的伺服器接收即時影像。使用家具掃描時，拍攝的照片與重建中的暫存檔只存在裝置上，用於建立 3D 模型，完成或取消後即刪除；建立的家具模型隨專案儲存。

資料存放

你輸入的專案名稱、客戶資訊、地址、設計備註、尺寸、配置與掃描模型會儲存在裝置上。使用者登入 iCloud 並同步時，專案和掃描檔會儲存在其 Apple iCloud 私有資料庫，供其裝置同步。

你主動匯出 PDF、DXF、SVG、USDZ、GLB、PNG 渲染圖或專案備份並分享時，資料會交給你選擇的接收方或 App。你應確保對輸入及分享的房屋、客戶與設計資料有適當權利，並自行保存重要備份。

廣告與追蹤

居圖使用 Google AdMob 顯示插頁廣告（例如從專案返回列表時）。App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。在適用地區，App 會在載入廣告前透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）詢問廣告同意，之後可在「購買項目」中的「廣告隱私設定」變更。你的專案、掃描、照片與客戶資料不會傳給廣告服務。

AdMob 可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。購買「移除廣告」後不再顯示廣告。

購買

掃描、建模、多專案及個人 iCloud 同步可免費使用（含廣告）。一次性內購：「專業匯出」解鎖成品匯出（PDF 圖組、DXF、SVG、USDZ、GLB 與高解析 3D 渲染圖），「精選傢具包」與「實拍材質包」解鎖更多 3D 傢具與材質，「移除廣告」永久移除廣告。沒有訂閱，價格以 App Store 購買畫面為準。

購買由 Apple App Store 處理，App 使用 StoreKit 確認購買權益；我們不在 App 中取得付款卡號。退款依 Apple 的政策處理。

在專案列表右上角選單點「購買項目」，再點「恢復購買」，並使用原購買 Apple 帳號。

第三方服務

iCloud 資料由 Apple 的 iCloud 服務處理，受 Apple 的條款與隱私政策約束。Apple App Store 處理購買，StoreKit 用於確認購買權益；Google AdMob 與 UMP 處理廣告與廣告同意。主動分享的資料交由你選擇的接收方或 App。

資料刪除

本機專案由你在 App 的專案列表刪除。同步專案刪除後，App 會將刪除狀態同步至 iCloud；裝置離線時需待重新連線。也可在 Apple 裝置的 iCloud 儲存空間管理中移除本 App 資料。Apple 帳號或裝置上的備份可能另依 Apple 設定保留。客服郵件可寄信要求刪除。

Summary

Roomory provides tools for room scanning, home planning, measurement, layouts, and interior drawing sets. The app uses the camera and LiDAR to create indoor space data. Camera frames are used for scanning and are not streamed to our servers. When you scan furniture, the photos and reconstruction files stay on your device, are used only to build the 3D model, and are deleted when capture finishes or is cancelled; the resulting furniture model is saved with your project.

Data stored

Project names, client information, addresses, design notes, dimensions, layouts, and scan models you enter are stored on your device. When you sign in to iCloud and sync, projects and scan files are stored in your Apple iCloud private database for use across your devices.

When you export and share a PDF, DXF, SVG, USDZ, GLB, PNG render, or project backup, data goes to the recipient or app you select. You must have suitable rights to enter and share property, client, and design information, and keep backups of important projects.

Ads and tracking

Roomory uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads (for example, when you return from a project to the list). The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. Where required, the app asks for advertising consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before loading ads; you can change it later under Purchases → Ad privacy choices. Your projects, scans, photos, and client information are never sent to the ad service.

AdMob may process IP address, device and ad-interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure. Ads stop after you buy Remove Ads.

Purchases

Scanning, modeling, multiple projects, and sync through your personal iCloud account are free, with ads. One-time purchases: Pro Export unlocks finished exports (PDF drawing set, DXF, SVG, USDZ, GLB, and high-resolution 3D renders), the Furniture Pack and Material Pack add more 3D furniture and materials, and Remove Ads removes ads permanently. There are no subscriptions; the App Store purchase screen shows local prices.

Apple App Store processes purchases. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlements and does not receive your payment card number. Apple handles refunds under its policies.

On the project list, open the top-right menu, choose Purchases, then tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Third-party services

Apple processes iCloud data under its terms and privacy policy. Apple App Store handles purchases, and StoreKit verifies entitlements; Google AdMob and UMP handle ads and ad consent. Data you choose to share goes to your selected recipient or app.

Deleting your data

Delete local projects from the app’s project list. For synced projects, the app sends the deletion to iCloud; an offline device must reconnect first. You can also remove this app’s data in your Apple device’s iCloud storage settings. Device or Apple Account backups may remain under your Apple settings. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.
