常見問題

如何開始？沒有 LiDAR 也能使用嗎？

在支援 RoomPlan 的 LiDAR iPhone 或 iPad 建立專案，逐間掃描，並在平面圖上校正尺寸與位置。沒有 LiDAR 的裝置仍可手動規劃。居圖提供室內空間掃描、居家規劃與設計圖組製作工具。

哪些功能免費？可以購買什麼？

掃描、建模、多專案及個人 iCloud 同步可免費使用（含廣告）。一次性內購：「專業匯出」解鎖成品匯出（PDF 圖組、DXF、SVG、USDZ、GLB 與高解析 3D 渲染圖），「精選傢具包」與「實拍材質包」解鎖更多 3D 傢具與材質，「移除廣告」永久移除廣告。沒有訂閱，價格以 App Store 購買畫面為準。

掃描尺寸與圖面能直接用於施工嗎？

掃描可能有誤差，請在施工前現場複測，並由具資格的專業人員確認結構、電力負載、給排水、法規及施工安全。App 產生的圖面不等同專業簽證或主管機關核准。使用圖面施工或向第三方交付前，請自行確認圖面完整性與適用性。

如何申請退款？

購買由 Apple App Store 處理，App 使用 StoreKit 確認購買權益；我們不在 App 中取得付款卡號。退款依 Apple 的政策處理。

如何恢復購買？

在專案列表右上角選單點「購買項目」，再點「恢復購買」，並使用原購買 Apple 帳號。

如何刪除資料？

在專案列表刪除專案。若已啟用 iCloud 同步，重新連線後會同步刪除；也可在 Apple 裝置的 iCloud 儲存空間管理中移除本 App 資料。Apple 帳號或裝置上的備份可能另依 Apple 設定保留。客服郵件可寄信要求刪除。

輸入、分享資料與備份有哪些責任？

你應確保對輸入及分享的房屋、客戶與設計資料有適當權利，並自行保存重要備份。主動匯出 PDF、DXF、SVG、USDZ、GLB、PNG 渲染圖或專案備份並分享時，資料會交給你選擇的接收方或 App。

回報問題

請附上裝置型號、iOS 版本與問題描述。請勿寄送客戶地址或完整掃描檔，除非支援人員明確需要且你同意提供。

FAQ

How do I get started? Can I use a device without LiDAR?

Create a project on a LiDAR iPhone or iPad that supports RoomPlan. Scan each room, then correct dimensions and placement on the floor plan. Devices without LiDAR can still be used for manual planning. Roomory provides room scanning, home planning, and drawing-set tools.

Which features are free, and what can I buy?

Scanning, modeling, multiple projects, and sync through your personal iCloud account are free, with ads. One-time purchases: Pro Export unlocks finished exports (PDF drawing set, DXF, SVG, USDZ, GLB, and high-resolution 3D renders), the Furniture Pack and Material Pack add more 3D furniture and materials, and Remove Ads removes ads permanently. There are no subscriptions; the App Store purchase screen shows local prices.

Can I build directly from scan dimensions and drawings?

Scans can be inaccurate. Verify measurements on site before construction and have qualified professionals review structure, electrical loads, plumbing, codes, and construction safety. App drawings are not professional approval or authority permits. Check drawings for completeness and suitability before construction or delivery to others.

How do I request a refund?

Apple App Store processes purchases. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlements and does not receive your payment card number. Apple handles refunds under its policies.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the project list, open the top-right menu, choose Purchases, then tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

How do I delete data?

Delete the project from the project list. If iCloud sync is active, the deletion syncs when you reconnect. You can also remove this app’s data in your Apple device’s iCloud storage settings. Device or Apple Account backups may remain under your Apple settings. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

What are my responsibilities for data, sharing, and backups?

You must have suitable rights to enter and share property, client, and design information. Keep backups of important projects. When you export and share a PDF, DXF, SVG, USDZ, GLB, PNG render, or project backup, data goes to the recipient or app you select.
