熊推蘿蔔是冰面上的北極熊一推就滑到底，把白蘿蔔推進雪洞種好的解謎遊戲。

常見問題

熊為什麼停不下來？

冰面上的熊和蘿蔔都會一路滑到碰到石頭、雪地或其他東西才停。善用石頭和雪地當煞車。每一關都保證有解，星星依最少步數計算。

每日池塘什麼時候換？

每天依你裝置的當地日期換一題，所有玩家同一天拿到同一題。

進度會不見嗎？需要帳號嗎？

不需要帳號。進度保存在裝置上；刪除 App 會一併刪除進度。若開啟 iCloud 備份，從備份還原裝置時進度也會一起還原。

廣告多久出現一次？

只在過關之後出現，關卡進行中不會打斷你。前 5 關沒有廣告，之後至少間隔 3 關且 3 分鐘才可能出現一次。廣告相關的資料處理請參閱 隱私權政策。

可以關掉音效嗎？

可以，在 App 的「設定」關閉音效；手機切到靜音時也不會播放。

回報問題

請附上 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、關卡編號與重現步驟，寄到 easonwumac@gmail.com。

FAQ

Why won't the bear stop?

On ice, the bear and the radishes slide until they hit a rock, snow or something else. Use rocks and snow as brakes. Every level is solvable, and stars are based on the fewest moves.

When does the Daily pond change?

At midnight in your device's local time. Everyone gets the same pond on the same day.

Will I lose my progress? Do I need an account?

No account is needed. Progress is stored on your device; deleting the app also deletes it. With iCloud backup enabled, progress is restored along with your device backup.

How often do ads appear?

Only after you finish a level, never during one. There are no ads in the first five levels, and afterwards at most one every three levels and three minutes. See the Privacy Policy for ad data practices.

Can I turn off sound?

Yes, in the app's Settings. Sounds also stay silent when your phone is muted.
