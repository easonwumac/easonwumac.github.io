隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

摘要

本政策適用於巡站 Siteward（com.easonwu.autotap）iPhone 與 iPad App 及本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護。巡站不需要帳號，也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。只有你使用雲端「巡站 AI」（「交給巡站 AI」步驟，或用一句話建立步驟）時，App 才會連線到開發者營運的伺服器（見下方「巡站 AI」）。Google 廣告服務有其自身的資料處理方式。

資料存放

你的流程、步驟、排程設定、執行歷程、讀取的網頁內容、變更比較與截圖，以及內建瀏覽器的 Cookie、快取與登入狀態，都保存在你的裝置上，除下方「巡站 AI」所述外不會上傳給開發者。你在「設定 → 已存的登入資料」儲存的網站帳號與密碼：帳號存在 App 內，密碼只存在這台裝置的鑰匙圈（不經 iCloud 同步）；登入過期時 App 在裝置上直接填入該網站的登入表單，不會傳給開發者、巡站 AI 或 Anthropic，你可以隨時在同一頁刪除。巡站不提供雲端執行或 iCloud 同步。

裝置端 AI 功能使用 Apple 的裝置端模型，在你的裝置上判斷條件、讀取資訊與摘要變化，不會將網頁內容送出裝置。AI 功能需要支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置，並啟用 Apple Intelligence 且備妥模型；不支援時仍可手動建立與執行流程。在支援巡站 AI 的裝置上，「用一句話建立」改由巡站 AI 產生步驟（見下方）；其他裝置上仍由裝置端模型產生。

巡站 AI

「交給巡站 AI」步驟是選用的雲端功能，第一次使用前 App 會說明並徵求你的同意，你可以隨時在「設定」關閉。執行這個步驟時，App 會把目前網頁的網址、標題、可操作元素與可見文字、你寫下的目標、這一步已做過的動作與讀到的值，以及需要時的畫面截圖，傳到開發者在 Cloudflare 上營運的伺服器，再轉給 Anthropic 的 Claude 模型決定下一個動作。用一句話建立步驟時，App 會把你寫的句子、目前網頁的網址、標題、可操作元素與可見文字，以及這個流程已讀取的值名稱，以同樣方式傳給 Claude 產生步驟，產生的步驟會先讓你確認再加入。密碼欄位的內容不會送出，但網頁上看得到的其他文字可能包含個人資料，請只在你願意分享的網頁使用。

開發者的伺服器不儲存網頁內容、截圖、目標或句子；只保存一組隨機產生的帳號代碼（存在你的 iCloud 鑰匙圈）、Apple App Attest 裝置金鑰、每日與每週用量次數及訂閱交易紀錄，用於計算額度與防止濫用。Cloudflare 與 Anthropic 會依其政策處理連線紀錄與請求內容；Anthropic 不會用 API 傳送的內容訓練模型，並可能為安全與防濫用目的短期保存。詳見 Anthropic 隱私權政策 與 Cloudflare 隱私權政策。

通知

流程設定的提醒，以及偵測到變更或執行受阻的通知，均由裝置發送本機通知，不使用開發者的推播伺服器。你可調整流程與排程，或在 iOS 設定中關閉巡站的通知權限。背景檢查何時執行由 iOS 決定，不保證準時或每次執行。

廣告

巡站使用 Google AdMob 在首頁底部顯示橫幅廣告，並在關閉流程編輯器等自然停頓處顯示插頁式廣告；廣告不會在流程執行中或瀏覽網頁時出現。一次購買「移除廣告」即可關閉所有廣告。App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。載入廣告前，App 透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）處理適用的廣告同意流程，並依你的選擇與適用要求載入廣告。你的流程、網頁內容、瀏覽資料、執行歷程與截圖不會提供給廣告服務。

AdMob 可能蒐集裝置識別碼、IP 位址（可用於推估大致位置）、使用與廣告互動資料，以及效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、分析、防詐欺與服務運作。未同意個人化廣告不代表廣告服務完全不處理資料。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

購買

你可以在「設定 → 移除廣告」選擇一次性購買，之後 App 不再顯示廣告，沒有訂閱。購買與還原由 Apple App Store 處理；巡站不會取得你的付款資料。重新安裝或換裝置後，可在「設定 → 還原購買」恢復已購買的移除廣告功能。

網站與第三方服務

當你瀏覽網站或執行流程時，App 會直接連線至你選擇的網站。網站可能接收你的 IP 位址、Cookie、登入資訊與你在網頁上輸入或提交的內容，並依其自身的隱私權政策處理；本機保存不代表瀏覽網站時不會傳送資料。網站帳號由你自行管理，巡站不會建立或代管網站帳號。

你有責任遵守各網站的使用條款。巡站不會解答或繞過 CAPTCHA、人機驗證或網站的自動化檢查；遇到驗證或需要重新登入時，流程會停止並通知你開啟網頁手動處理。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

資料刪除

在「設定 → 清除瀏覽資料」可移除內建瀏覽器的 Cookie、快取與網站登入狀態，之後可能需要重新登入網站。清除瀏覽資料不會刪除流程或執行歷程。要移除全部本機 App 資料，包含流程、執行歷程與截圖，請刪除 App。要刪除巡站 AI 的帳號代碼與用量紀錄，請在「設定 → 巡站 AI」點「刪除巡站 AI 資料」：伺服器上的帳號與用量會刪除，iCloud 鑰匙圈裡的帳號代碼也會移除；無法使用 App 時，也可以寄信並附上帳號代碼。這不會刪除網站或廣告服務商已持有的資料；你可以寄信要求刪除支援來信。

聯絡我們

若有隱私、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與附件，用於回覆與排查問題。請先遮蔽截圖中的個人資料，不要寄送密碼、Cookie、私人網頁內容或付款資料。

Summary

This policy covers the Siteward (巡站, com.easonwu.autotap) iPhone and iPad app and this support website. The app is maintained by an independent developer. Siteward requires no account and does not collect usage analytics itself. The app connects to a developer-operated server only when you use cloud Siteward AI (the “Do with Siteward AI” step, or writing steps from one sentence; see “Siteward AI” below). Google’s advertising service has its own data practices.

Data stored

Your flows, steps, schedules, run history, text read from pages, change comparisons, and screenshots, along with the built-in browser’s cookies, cache, and sign-in sessions, stay on your device and are not uploaded to the developer, except as described under “Siteward AI”. Sign-ins you save in Settings → Saved sign-ins keep the username in the app and the password only in this device’s Keychain (not synced through iCloud); when a session expires the app fills that site’s sign-in form on the device. They are never sent to the developer, Siteward AI or Anthropic, and you can delete them on the same screen. Siteward offers no cloud execution or iCloud sync.

On-device AI features use Apple’s on-device model to evaluate conditions, read information, and summarize changes locally; that page content never leaves the device. AI features require an Apple Intelligence device with Apple Intelligence enabled and the model ready. You can still build and run flows manually on unsupported devices. On devices that support Siteward AI, Describe in words writes steps with Siteward AI (see below); elsewhere the on-device model writes them.

Siteward AI

The “Do with Siteward AI” step is an optional cloud feature. Before its first use the app explains it and asks for your consent, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time. When the step runs, the app sends the current page’s URL, title, interactive elements and visible text, the goal you wrote, the actions taken and values read so far in that step, and a screenshot when needed, to a developer-operated server on Cloudflare, which passes them to Anthropic’s Claude model to choose the next action. When you write steps from one sentence, the app sends your sentence, the current page’s URL, title, interactive elements and visible text, and the names of values the flow already reads to Claude the same way, and shows you the steps to confirm before adding them. Password field contents are never sent, but other visible text on a page may contain personal information, so use it only on pages you are willing to share.

The developer’s server does not store page content, screenshots, goals or sentences. It keeps only a randomly generated account code (stored in your iCloud Keychain), an Apple App Attest device key, daily and weekly usage counts and subscription transaction records, to count your allowance and prevent abuse. Cloudflare and Anthropic process connection records and request content under their own policies; Anthropic does not train models on content sent through its API and may retain it briefly for safety and abuse prevention. See the Anthropic Privacy Policy and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders configured in your flows and alerts for changes or blocked runs are local notifications sent by your device, with no developer-operated push server. You can adjust flows and schedules or turn off Siteward’s notification permission in iOS Settings. iOS decides when background checks run; they are not guaranteed to run on time or on every occasion.

Ads

Siteward uses Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the home screen and interstitial ads at natural breaks, such as after closing the flow editor. Ads never appear during a run or while browsing, and a one-time Remove ads purchase turns them all off. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. Before loading ads, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising consent and loads ads according to your choices and applicable requirements. Your flows, page content, browsing data, run history, and screenshots are not shared with the advertising service.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses (which can indicate an approximate location), usage and ad interaction data, and performance and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Declining personalized ads does not mean the advertising service processes no data. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure.

Purchases

You can make an optional one-time Remove ads purchase in Settings → Remove ads, after which the app stops displaying ads. There is no subscription. Apple App Store handles purchases and restoration; Siteward does not receive your payment details. After reinstalling or changing devices, use Settings → Restore purchases to restore your Remove ads purchase.

Websites and third-party services

When you browse or run a flow, the app connects directly to the websites you choose. Those sites may receive your IP address, cookies, sign-in information, and content you enter or submit, and handle it under their own privacy policies. Local storage does not mean browsing sends no data to websites. You manage your website accounts; Siteward does not create or manage them for you.

You are responsible for following each website’s terms. Siteward does not solve or bypass CAPTCHAs, human verification, or website automation checks. If verification or a fresh sign-in is needed, the flow stops and notifies you to open the page and handle it manually.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear browsing data to remove the built-in browser’s cookies, cache, and website sign-in sessions. You may need to sign in to websites again. Clearing browsing data does not delete flows or run history. To remove all local app data, including flows, run history, and screenshots, delete the app. To delete your Siteward AI account code and usage records, tap Delete Siteward AI data in Settings → Siteward AI: the account and its usage are deleted from the server and the code is removed from your iCloud Keychain. If you cannot use the app, email us with the account code instead. This does not delete data already held by websites or advertising providers. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Contact

For privacy, data, or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments to reply and troubleshoot. Hide personal details in screenshots and do not send passwords, cookies, private page content, or payment information.
