摘要

本政策適用於自動點 AutoTap（com.easonwu.autotap）iPhone 與 iPad App 及本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護。AutoTap 不需要帳號，沒有開發者營運的伺服器，也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。Google 廣告服務有其自身的資料處理方式。

資料存放

你的流程、步驟、排程設定、執行歷程、讀取的網頁內容、變更比較與截圖，以及內建瀏覽器的 Cookie、快取與登入狀態，都保存在你的裝置上，不會上傳給開發者。AutoTap 不提供雲端執行或 iCloud 同步。

AI 功能使用 Apple 的裝置端模型，在你的裝置上產生流程、判斷條件、讀取資訊與摘要變化；不會將網頁內容送到開發者伺服器或雲端 AI 處理。AI 功能需要支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置，並啟用 Apple Intelligence 且備妥模型；不支援時仍可手動建立與執行流程。

通知

流程設定的提醒，以及偵測到變更或執行受阻的通知，均由裝置發送本機通知，不使用開發者的推播伺服器。你可調整流程與排程，或在 iOS 設定中關閉 AutoTap 的通知權限。背景檢查何時執行由 iOS 決定，不保證準時或每次執行。

廣告

AutoTap 使用 Google AdMob 在首頁底部顯示橫幅廣告，並在關閉流程編輯器等自然停頓處顯示插頁式廣告；廣告不會在流程執行中或瀏覽網頁時出現。一次購買「移除廣告」即可關閉所有廣告。載入廣告前，App 透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）處理適用的廣告同意流程，並依你的選擇與適用要求載入廣告。你的流程、網頁內容、瀏覽資料、執行歷程與截圖不會提供給廣告服務。

AdMob 可能蒐集裝置識別碼、IP 位址（可用於推估大致位置）、使用與廣告互動資料，以及效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、分析、防詐欺與服務運作。未同意個人化廣告不代表廣告服務完全不處理資料。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

購買

你可以在「設定 → 移除廣告」選擇一次性購買，之後 App 不再顯示廣告，沒有訂閱。購買與還原由 Apple App Store 處理；AutoTap 不會取得你的付款資料。重新安裝或換裝置後，可在「設定 → 還原購買」恢復已購買的移除廣告功能。

網站與第三方服務

當你瀏覽網站或執行流程時，App 會直接連線至你選擇的網站。網站可能接收你的 IP 位址、Cookie、登入資訊與你在網頁上輸入或提交的內容，並依其自身的隱私權政策處理；本機保存不代表瀏覽網站時不會傳送資料。網站帳號由你自行管理，AutoTap 不會建立或代管網站帳號。

你有責任遵守各網站的使用條款。AutoTap 不會解答或繞過 CAPTCHA、人機驗證或網站的自動化檢查；遇到驗證或需要重新登入時，流程會停止並通知你開啟網頁手動處理。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

資料刪除

在「設定 → 清除瀏覽資料」可移除內建瀏覽器的 Cookie、快取與網站登入狀態，之後可能需要重新登入網站。清除瀏覽資料不會刪除流程或執行歷程。要移除全部本機 App 資料，包含流程、執行歷程與截圖，請刪除 App。這不會刪除網站或廣告服務商已持有的資料；你可以寄信要求刪除支援來信。

聯絡我們

若有隱私、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與附件，用於回覆與排查問題。請先遮蔽截圖中的個人資料，不要寄送密碼、Cookie、私人網頁內容或付款資料。

Summary

This policy covers the AutoTap (自動點, com.easonwu.autotap) iPhone and iPad app and this support website. The app is maintained by an independent developer. AutoTap requires no account, has no developer-operated server, and does not collect usage analytics itself. Google’s advertising service has its own data practices.

Data stored

Your flows, steps, schedules, run history, text read from pages, change comparisons, and screenshots, along with the built-in browser’s cookies, cache, and sign-in sessions, stay on your device and are not uploaded to the developer. AutoTap offers no cloud execution or iCloud sync.

AI features use Apple’s on-device model to create flows, evaluate conditions, read information, and summarize changes locally. Page content is not sent to a developer server or cloud AI for processing. AI features require an Apple Intelligence device with Apple Intelligence enabled and the model ready. You can still build and run flows manually on unsupported devices.

Notifications

Reminders configured in your flows and alerts for changes or blocked runs are local notifications sent by your device, with no developer-operated push server. You can adjust flows and schedules or turn off AutoTap’s notification permission in iOS Settings. iOS decides when background checks run; they are not guaranteed to run on time or on every occasion.

Ads

AutoTap uses Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the home screen and interstitial ads at natural breaks, such as after closing the flow editor. Ads never appear during a run or while browsing, and a one-time Remove ads purchase turns them all off. Before loading ads, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising consent and loads ads according to your choices and applicable requirements. Your flows, page content, browsing data, run history, and screenshots are not shared with the advertising service.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses (which can indicate an approximate location), usage and ad interaction data, and performance and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Declining personalized ads does not mean the advertising service processes no data. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure.

Purchases

You can make an optional one-time Remove ads purchase in Settings → Remove ads, after which the app stops displaying ads. There is no subscription. Apple App Store handles purchases and restoration; AutoTap does not receive your payment details. After reinstalling or changing devices, use Settings → Restore purchases to restore your Remove ads purchase.

Websites and third-party services

When you browse or run a flow, the app connects directly to the websites you choose. Those sites may receive your IP address, cookies, sign-in information, and content you enter or submit, and handle it under their own privacy policies. Local storage does not mean browsing sends no data to websites. You manage your website accounts; AutoTap does not create or manage them for you.

You are responsible for following each website’s terms. AutoTap does not solve or bypass CAPTCHAs, human verification, or website automation checks. If verification or a fresh sign-in is needed, the flow stops and notifies you to open the page and handle it manually.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear browsing data to remove the built-in browser’s cookies, cache, and website sign-in sessions. You may need to sign in to websites again. Clearing browsing data does not delete flows or run history. To remove all local app data, including flows, run history, and screenshots, delete the app. This does not delete data already held by websites or advertising providers. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Contact

For privacy, data, or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments to reply and troubleshoot. Hide personal details in screenshots and do not send passwords, cookies, private page content, or payment information.
