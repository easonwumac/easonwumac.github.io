摘要

本政策適用於 Glowbloom（引光）iOS App 與本支援網站。引光是轉動鏡子，把陽光引到每個花盆，讓花開滿花園的解謎遊戲。此 App 由個人開發者維護，不需要帳號，沒有開發者營運的伺服器，也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。Google 廣告服務有其自身的資料處理方式。

資料存放

遊戲進度、最佳步數、收藏與每日連續天數只保存在你的裝置上。若你啟用 iCloud 備份，這些資料也可能包含在你自己的 iCloud 備份中。App 不會上傳你的遊戲資料。

廣告

引光使用 Google AdMob，在關卡之間顯示插頁式廣告，關卡進行中不會顯示。載入廣告前，App 透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）處理適用的廣告同意流程。App 不會請求 Apple App Tracking Transparency（ATT）追蹤授權，也不會跨 App 追蹤你。

AdMob 可能蒐集裝置識別碼、IP 位址所推得的大略位置、廣告互動資料，以及效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、防範詐欺與服務運作。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

購買

若 App 提供一次性的「移除廣告」購買，購買與還原由 Apple App Store 處理，引光不會取得你的付款資料。

本網站

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

資料刪除

刪除 App 即會移除裝置上的所有遊戲資料。已存在的 iCloud 備份須透過 Apple 的備份設定另外管理；刪除本機資料不會刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。你可以寄信要求刪除支援來信。

兒童

引光適合一般大眾，並非專為兒童設計，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

聯絡我們

若有隱私或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱與訊息內容，僅用於回覆與排查問題。

Summary

This policy covers the Glowbloom (引光) iOS app and this support website. Glowbloom is a calm puzzle game: turn mirrors to guide sunlight onto every flower pot and grow a garden. The app is maintained by an independent developer, needs no account, has no developer-operated server, and does not collect usage analytics itself. Google's advertising service has its own data practices.

Data stored

Game progress, best moves, collections and daily streaks stay on your device. If you enable iCloud backup, this data may also be included in your own iCloud backup. The app does not upload your game data.

Ads

Glowbloom uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads between levels, never during a level. Before loading ads, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising consent. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not track you across apps.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, and performance and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure.

Purchases

If the app offers a one-time Remove ads purchase, Apple's App Store handles purchases and restoration; Glowbloom never receives your payment details.

This website

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data from your device. Existing iCloud backups are managed in Apple's backup settings; local deletion does not erase data already held by advertising providers. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Children

Glowbloom is for a general audience, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Contact

For privacy or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply and troubleshoot.
