常見問題

可以離線遊玩嗎？

核心關卡可離線遊玩，廣告不可用時不會阻擋遊戲進度保存。廣告服務的資料處理並非完全離線。

重設遊戲進度會刪除什麼？

會清除本地進度、星星與成就，並保留個人設定。重設不會撤回廣告同意或刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。刪除 App 可移除本機 App 資料，Apple 備份須另外管理。

如何查看廣告隱私選項？

SDK 要求提供隱私選項時，可在「設定 → 一般 → 隱私選項」重新開啟表單。選擇可能因地區與服務狀態而不同。目前遊戲版本未要求 Apple 跨 App 追蹤授權。

回報問題

遊戲問題：請提供關卡編號、App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本及重現步驟。如附截圖，請先遮蔽個人資料。

不適當廣告：請提供廣告出現的時間與時區、廣告內容和當時關卡。如廣告提供回報功能，也可直接使用。請勿為回報而點擊廣告。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼、付款資料或私鑰。

FAQ

Can I play offline?

Core levels work offline, and unavailable ads do not prevent game progress from being saved. Advertising data processing is not fully offline.

What does resetting game progress delete?

It clears local progress, stars, and achievements while keeping preferences. Resetting does not withdraw ad consent or erase data already held by ad providers. Deleting the app removes local app data; Apple backups must be managed separately.

How do I review ad privacy choices?

When required by the SDK, open Settings → General → Privacy options. Choices vary by region and service status. The current game version does not request Apple’s cross-app tracking authorization.

Report a problem

Game issues: include the level number, app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Hide personal information in screenshots.

Inappropriate ads: include the time and time zone, ad content, and level. You can also use the ad provider’s reporting control if available. Do not click an ad just to report it. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or private keys.
