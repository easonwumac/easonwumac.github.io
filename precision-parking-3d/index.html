Slow down. Find your angle. Make every space count.

Take on 40 fixed parking challenges across five chapters, starting with three introductory levels to learn steering, pedals and gear changes. From level four, tighter lots and more parked cars demand careful control. Hitting another car ends the attempt.

Each level has three objectives: park successfully, finish without a collision and complete an extra challenge. Replay to earn missing objectives across separate attempts, with 120 stars to collect. Completing a level unlocks the next.

Choose left/right buttons, sliding steering or a rotating wheel. Switch between third-person and overhead views. Play in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese or Korean.

All core levels work offline. Progress and achievements are stored on your device. Includes interstitial ads at natural transitions. Advertising and privacy services require a connection and depend on applicable consent.
