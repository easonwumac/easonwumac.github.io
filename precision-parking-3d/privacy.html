摘要

本政策適用於「極限停車場：都市挑戰 / Precision Parking 3D」iOS 遊戲與本支援網站。本遊戲由個人開發者維護。

資料存放

關卡進度、星星、最佳時間、成就、教學狀態、設定及廣告顯示頻率計數儲存在你的裝置。本遊戲沒有玩家登入、自建帳號系統或開發者營運的遊戲後端，也不自行上傳這些遊戲存檔。裝置備份可能依你的 Apple 系統設定保留 App 資料。本地遊戲資料在你重設或刪除之前保留。

匿名使用統計（提供此功能的更新版本）

更新版本可回報首次量測啟動、每日使用、首次功能開始／完成，以及次日與第七日回訪的日期與彙總次數；升級用戶也會納入首次量測，不代表新增下載。Inside 的功能完成指引可能包含使用者略過的項目；停車遊戲以第一關開始及完成計。這些統計不含檢查結果、備註、錄音、遊戲存檔、廣告識別碼或固定裝置／安裝識別碼。

資料透過 agentmesh.uk 傳送，由 Cloudflare 提供網路與儲存服務。每個事件的獨立隨機收據僅用於避免重複計數，七日後清除；彙總次數保留 180 日。我們的統計資料庫不保存 IP 位址、來源網址或瀏覽器指紋；Cloudflare 會為提供服務處理網路請求。可在 App 設定關閉匿名使用統計，停止後續傳送並清空尚未送出的事件；已彙總的資料無法對應個人刪除。舊版未包含此回報功能。詳見 使用統計與隱私。

廣告與追蹤

遊戲整合 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）及 User Messaging Platform（UMP），用於提供插頁廣告與管理適用的隱私選擇。Google 及參與廣告供應的服務可能處理 IP 位址及推估的大致位置、裝置或 App 範圍識別碼、廣告及互動資料、效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、分析、服務運作與改善。本遊戲不要求精確位置權限。

廣告服務的資料處理並非完全離線；「非個人化廣告」也不代表完全不蒐集資料。可請求的廣告依適用地區、SDK 狀態及有效隱私選擇而定。核心關卡可離線遊玩，廣告不可用時不會阻擋遊戲進度保存。

SDK 要求提供隱私選項時，可在遊戲「設定 → 一般 → 隱私選項」重新開啟表單，調整適用的選擇。選擇可能因地區與服務狀態而不同。目前遊戲版本未要求 Apple 跨 App 追蹤授權；你也可以在 iOS「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」管理 App 的系統追蹤權限。

第三方服務

請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或 App 的資訊，了解其處理方式、資料保留與控制選項。第三方資料依各服務商政策保留。

本網站使用 GitHub Pages 託管，沒有自行加入追蹤分析程式。GitHub 可能為提供網站服務處理連線及技術紀錄，詳見 GitHub 隱私聲明。Google、GitHub 與郵件服務可能在你所在地以外處理資料，依各自政策及適用保障措施運作。廣告或外部連結開啟的網站另適用其自身政策。

資料刪除

「重設遊戲進度」會清除本地進度、星星與成就，並保留個人設定；此操作不會撤回廣告同意，也不會刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。刪除 App 可移除本機 App 資料，Apple 備份須另依系統設定管理。

可寄信要求存取、更正或刪除你提供給開發者的支援資料，或提出其他適用隱私權請求。對 Google 持有的資料，請使用其隱私政策所列控制與聯絡方式。

Summary

This policy covers the Precision Parking 3D (極限停車場：都市挑戰) iOS game and this support website. The game is maintained by an individual developer.

Data stored

Level progress, stars, best times, achievements, tutorial status, preferences, and ad-frequency counters are stored on your device. The game has no player login, developer-operated account system, or game backend, and does not itself upload these save files. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your system settings. Local game data remains until you reset or delete it.

Anonymous usage counts (updates that include this feature)

Updated versions can report dates and counts for first measured opens, daily activity, first measured task starts and completions, and returns on day 1 and day 7. Existing users who upgrade are included in first measured opens; these are not new-download counts. Inside counts a finished guided check, which may include skipped steps; the parking game counts starting and completing level one. No check results, notes, recordings, game saves, advertising IDs, or persistent device or installation identifiers are included.

Reports are sent to agentmesh.uk using Cloudflare for networking and storage. Independent random event receipts prevent duplicate delivery and are removed after seven days; aggregate counts are retained for 180 days. Our metrics database does not store IP addresses, referrers, or browser fingerprints; Cloudflare processes network requests to provide the service. Turn off anonymous usage counts in app settings to stop future reporting and clear unsent events. Aggregate data cannot be linked to a person for individual deletion. Older versions do not include this reporting. See usage counts and privacy.

Ads and tracking

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for interstitial ads and applicable privacy choices. Google and participating ad services may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device or app-scoped identifiers, advertising and interaction data, performance information, and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, analysis, and service operation or improvement. The game does not request precise-location permission.

Advertising data processing is not fully offline. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is collected. Available ad requests depend on region, SDK status, and valid privacy choices. Core levels work offline, and unavailable ads do not prevent game progress from being saved.

When required by the SDK, open Settings → General → Privacy options to review applicable choices. Availability varies by region and service status. The current game version does not request Apple’s cross-app tracking authorization. You can also manage system tracking permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Third-party services

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps for processing, retention, and controls. Third-party information follows each provider’s retention policy.

This website uses GitHub Pages without developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records to serve the website; see its Privacy Statement. Google, GitHub, and email services may process information outside your country under their policies and applicable safeguards. External websites and ad destinations have their own policies.

Deleting your data

Reset game progress clears local progress, stars, and achievements while keeping preferences. It does not withdraw advertising consent or delete information already held by ad providers. Deleting the app removes local app data; manage Apple backups separately through system settings.

Email us to request access, correction, or deletion of support information you provided, or to exercise other applicable privacy rights. For information held by Google, use the controls and contact methods in Google’s policy.
