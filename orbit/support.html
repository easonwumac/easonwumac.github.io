常見問題

Orbit 的系統需求是什麼？如何開始掃描？

Orbit 需要 macOS 26 以上。選擇資料夾後才會掃描。

為什麼有些項目沒有計入？符號連結如何處理？

若部分項目無法讀取，請確認檔案權限；尚未下載的雲端檔案不會計入。符號連結會保留，但不跟隨其目標。

空間圖的大小代表可釋出的空間嗎？

空間圖顯示邏輯檔案大小，不代表實際佔用或可回收容量。移到垃圾桶後，需清空垃圾桶才會釋出空間。平面容量圖以矩形面積呈現邏輯大小，選取區塊會與檔案表格同步；可深入資料夾並回到上一層。

如何比較空間時間軸？

時間軸從實際掃描開始累積，重新掃描相同位置後即可比較；掃描不完整時不顯示可靠的增減比較。

專案卡與重複檔案要如何檢查？

專案卡依檔名線索提出候選群組，同名不代表一定相關。重複檔案請先指定保留副本，再預覽其他副本的整理操作。

可以跨磁碟區搬移或放回原處嗎？

首版僅允許同一磁碟區內搬移、移至垃圾桶及條件式放回原處；跨磁碟區操作會在改動原始項目前阻擋，請改用 Finder。

為什麼本機分析無法使用？

可選的本機分析需符合 Apple Intelligence 裝置、語言、地區、啟用與模型可用條件；不可用時仍可使用基本掃描與整理。

回報問題

來信請提供 macOS 與 Orbit 版本、操作步驟及錯誤訊息；寄送截圖或掃描報告前，請先移除私人檔名與路徑。

FAQ

What does Orbit require, and how do I start scanning?

Orbit requires macOS 26 or later. Select a folder to begin scanning.

Why are some items skipped, and how are symbolic links handled?

If some items cannot be read, check file permissions. Cloud files that have not downloaded are skipped. Symbolic links are preserved without following their targets.

Does the space map show how much space I can recover?

The space map shows logical file sizes, not physical usage or guaranteed recoverable space. Moving items to Trash releases space only after Trash is emptied. The flat map represents logical sizes with rectangular areas; selecting a block also selects its file-table row. You can explore folders and go back.

How do I compare scans in the space timeline?

The timeline begins with actual scans. Rescan the same location to compare; incomplete scans cannot provide reliable change comparisons.

How should I review project cards and duplicate files?

Project cards suggest candidate groups from filename clues; matching names do not prove a relationship. For duplicates, choose a copy to keep before reviewing organization actions for the other copies.

Can I move items across volumes or put them back?

Version 1 allows moves, Trash, and guarded Put Back only within the same volume. Cross-volume operations are blocked before changing the original; use Finder for those moves.

Why is local analysis unavailable?

Optional local analysis needs eligible Apple Intelligence hardware, language, region, enablement, and an available model. Basic scanning and organization work when it is unavailable.

Report a problem

Include your macOS and Orbit versions, steps, and any error message. Remove private filenames and paths before sending screenshots or scan reports.
