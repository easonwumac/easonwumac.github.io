常見問題

怎麼玩？

水火箭車每次都會全速衝出去，你要做的是改裝：把零件拖到車頭、車頂、車尾、兩側或底盤，同一個零件放在不同位置效果不同。按 GO 看它能不能跑完軌道、衝過終點旗再停下來。

失敗了怎麼辦？

失敗畫面會說明原因，例如太快衝出彎道、速度不夠繞不過迴圈，還會亮起值得改的位置。同一關失敗兩次後，左上的提示燈泡會一步一步給出答案。

提示燈泡為什麼要看廣告？

每關提示的第一步免費；之後每一步前會播放一則廣告。分享過關可以得到提示卡，提示卡會先用掉，不用看廣告。

飛行大賽是什麼？

過完第 1 章後開放。零件任你裝，看誰飛得最遠又平安落地。每週換一次跳台高度與風向，成績會上 Game Center 的每週排行榜。

怪招和秘密星是什麼？

用意想不到的方式過關會蓋上「怪招」章，收進怪招圖鑑；有些關卡藏著紫色秘密星，收集 5 顆可以打開秘密關。

進度會不見嗎？

進度自動存在裝置上，刪除 App 會清除進度。

FAQ

How do I play?

Your water-rocket car always launches at full power; your job is the build. Drag parts onto the nose, roof, tail, sides or belly, and the same part does different things in different places. Tap GO to see whether it finishes the track, passing the finish flag and stopping.

What if I fail?

The failure screen says why, for example too fast into a bend or too slow for a loop, and lights the spot worth changing. After two failures on a level, the hint bulb at the top left gives the answer one step at a time.

Why do hints show ads?

The first hint step of each level is free; each later step plays one ad first. Sharing a win earns hint cards, which are used before any ad.

What is the flight contest?

It opens after chapter 1. Build anything you like and see who flies farthest and still lands safely. The ramp height and wind change every week, and your best goes to the weekly Game Center leaderboard.

What are odd wins and secret stars?

Winning in an unexpected way stamps an odd win into your book; some levels hide purple secret stars, and five of them open the secret level.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device automatically. Deleting the app clears it.
