Splash Car

Your water-rocket car always launches at full power. Whether it finishes the track is down to your build.

Free on the App StoreSupportFeedback

Features

Every crash teaches something

  • The failure screen says why: too fast into a bend, too slow for the loop, nose too high on landing
  • The spot worth changing lights up; after two tries the hint bulb walks you to a three-star build, one step at a time
  • After each win, one line explains the idea behind it: center of mass, lift, grip, air drag, thrust and weight

50 toy-track levels

  • 9 chapters: loops, ramps, ice, conveyor belts, head and tail winds, low bars and towers to ram
  • Parts: wings, weights, guide rollers, a drag chute, balloons, magnets, fins, springs and a spare tank, plus nozzle, tires and water
  • Three stars per level: fly through the stars in the air
  • Purple secret stars: collect 5 to open the secret level

More to play

  • Flight contest: build within the week’s rules, fly the furthest and land safely; the ramp and wind change every week, and some weeks add a bend, a short landing strip or a banned part, with a weekly Game Center leaderboard
  • Odd wins book: win in a way nobody planned and get it stamped
  • 15 achievements and a record of every winning build
  • Turn a win into a card and a short video to challenge friends

Drag parts onto the nose, roof, tail, sides, belly or tow hitch, and the same part does different things in different places: a wing on the tail presses the car down for steadier bends, on the roof it lifts the car so it flies farther. Tap GO and see whether it passes the finish flag and stops.

No sign-up, plays offline; only the leaderboard uses Game Center. iPhone and iPad.

FAQ

Is there a game that teaches physics through play?

Splash Car has you build a water-rocket car for 50 toy-track levels: the same part works differently in different places, a crash screen says why it failed, and each win explains the idea behind it, such as center of mass, lift, grip or drag. No sign-up, plays offline.

How do you play Splash Car?

Your water-rocket car always launches at full power; whether it finishes the track is down to your build. Drag parts onto the nose, roof, tail, sides, belly or tow hitch, where the same part works differently, then press GO and see if it crosses the finish flag.

How many levels does Splash Car have, and does it need internet?

Splash Car has 50 toy-track levels in 9 chapters, each with three stars. No sign-up is needed and it plays offline; only the leaderboards use Game Center. It supports iPhone and iPad.
