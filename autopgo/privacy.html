摘要

本政策適用於噴噴車 Splash Car iOS App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護，不需要帳號，不要求任何系統權限，也不自行蒐集使用分析資料；只有你主動送出意見時，才會把內容傳給開發者。

存放在裝置上的資料

關卡進度、星星、過關組合、怪招、成就、提示卡、飛行大賽的每週最佳成績與設定都只保存在你的裝置上，不會上傳到我們這裡。刪除 App 會一併刪除。

Game Center

飛行大賽的每週排行榜使用 Apple 的 Game Center。若你已登入 Game Center，你的最佳飛行距離會傳給 Apple，與你的 Game Center 名稱一起顯示在排行榜上；這些資料由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理，我們看不到你的帳號資料。未登入時不會傳送任何成績。

分享

過關後按「分享」，App 會在裝置上做出一張圖卡與一段短片，用 iOS 分享選單送出，送到哪裡由你決定；App 不會自動傳送。

廣告

噴噴車使用 Google AdMob，只在你看提示的第二步起播放插頁廣告（第一步免費，分享過關可得提示卡抵用），且只請求非個人化廣告。App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限；App 內含 Google 的 User Messaging Platform（UMP）SDK，但因只請求非個人化廣告，不會顯示其同意表單。

即使是非個人化廣告，AdMob 仍可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

意見回饋與其他作品

「關於」頁的「傳送意見」與關卡中的「!」會打開意見表單；從關卡打開時會先附上當下的畫面截圖、關卡編號與車子配置，送出前可看到並移除。只有你按下「送出」時，才會把你的文字、圖片、選填的 Email、App 版本、裝置型號與 iOS 版本傳到開發者的伺服器 agentmesh.uk（由 Cloudflare 託管），轉成開發者私人 GitHub 儲存庫中的一則待辦；文字轉成待辦後即從伺服器刪除，圖片存放在只有該待辦才有連結的網址，90 天後刪除。每日送出次數以當天更換的 IP 雜湊計算，隔日刪除。內容只用於回覆與改善遊戲，請勿附上敏感資料，可來信要求提早刪除。

「關於」頁的其他作品清單每天最多向 agentmesh.uk 讀取一次，不附帶任何關於你的資料；點選後在 App Store 開啟該作品頁面。

兒童

App 不要求任何個人資料。廣告請求設定為適合所有年齡的內容分級（G）。

聯絡我們

若有隱私、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與附件，只用於回覆。

Summary

This policy covers the Splash Car (噴噴車) iOS app and this support website. The app is maintained by an independent developer, needs no account, asks for no system permissions, and does not collect usage analytics itself; only feedback you choose to send reaches the developer.

Data on your device

Your level progress, stars, winning builds, odd wins, achievements, hint cards, weekly flight-contest bests and settings stay on your device and are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Game Center

The weekly flight-contest leaderboard uses Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best flight distance is sent to Apple and shown on the leaderboard with your Game Center name; Apple handles this under its own privacy policy, and we never see your account details. Nothing is sent when you are not signed in.

Sharing

Tapping Share after a win makes a card image and a short video on your device and opens the iOS share sheet; you choose where they go. The app never sends them on its own.

Ads

Splash Car uses Google AdMob to show an interstitial ad only before the second and later steps of a hint (the first step is free, and hint cards earned by sharing a win are used first), and requests non-personalized ads only. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. The app includes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK but shows no consent form, because it requests non-personalized ads only.

Even for non-personalized ads, AdMob may process IP addresses, device and ad interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Feedback and our other apps

Send Feedback on the About page and the “!” button in a level open a feedback form; from a level it starts with a screenshot of the moment, the level number and the car’s parts, which you see and can remove before sending. Only when you tap Send do your text, pictures, the email you may add, the app version, device model and iOS version go to the developer’s server agentmesh.uk (hosted on Cloudflare), which files them as an item in the developer’s private GitHub repository. The text is deleted from the server once filed; pictures stay at an address only that item links to and are deleted after 90 days. The daily send limit counts a hash of your IP address that changes every day and is deleted the next day. It is used only to reply and improve the game; please leave out sensitive information, and email us to have it deleted sooner.

The list of our other apps on the About page is read from agentmesh.uk at most once a day and carries no data about you; tapping one opens its App Store page.

Children

The app asks for no personal information. Ad requests are limited to content rated for all ages (G).

Contact

For privacy, data, or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments, used only to reply.
