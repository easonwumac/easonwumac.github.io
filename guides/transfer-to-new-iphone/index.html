Moving to a new iPhone: Quick Start step by step

用「快速開始」把舊手機的資料直接搬到新手機，不需要電腦。開始前先準備好這幾件事。

1. 準備舊手機

舊手機需要 iOS 11 以上，建議兩台都更新到最新版本。挑一段幾分鐘內不需要用舊手機的時間。

2. 靠近並依畫面操作

舊手機連上 Wi-Fi、開啟藍牙，解鎖停在主畫面；把新手機開機放在旁邊，照畫面指示操作，設定 Face ID 或 Touch ID。

3. 選擇移轉方式

可以從 iCloud 下載（App 與資料在背景下載，可以馬上開始用），或直接從舊手機移轉（要等兩台都完成）。移轉時兩台放在一起並接上電源；提示消失就重新啟動兩台。

換完手機後，可以用機況 Inside 的 2 分鐘檢查，把新手機的螢幕、觸控、相機、揚聲器、麥克風與充電都測一輪；賣舊機前也能先出一份報告。基本檢查免費。 App Store

Moving to a new iPhone: Quick Start step by step

Quick Start moves your data from the old phone to the new one without a computer. Get these ready first.

1. Prepare the old phone

The old phone needs iOS 11 or later; update both phones to the latest version. Pick a time when you won't need the old phone for several minutes.

2. Bring them together and follow the screen

Put the old phone on Wi-Fi with Bluetooth on, unlocked at the Home Screen; turn on the new phone next to it, follow the onscreen steps and set up Face ID or Touch ID.

3. Choose how to transfer

Download from iCloud (apps and data download in the background so you can start right away) or transfer directly (wait until both finish). Keep both phones together and plugged in; if the prompt disappears, restart both.

After switching, Inside's 2-minute check tests the new phone's screen, touch, cameras, speakers, microphones and charging, and it can make a report for the old phone before you sell it. Basic checks are free. App Store

Sources: support.apple.com/en-us/102659
