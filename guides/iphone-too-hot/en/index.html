Is it normal for an iPhone to get hot?

Warmth after an update, while wireless charging or gaming is common. Here is what Apple calls normal and what to do when it overheats.

1. When warmth is normal

Warmth during setup, restore, major updates, wireless charging, demanding apps or high-quality video streaming is normal. iPhone is designed for ambient temperatures of 0 to 35 °C.

2. What happens when it's too hot

When too hot, charging may slow or pause, the display may dim, the signal may weaken, the flash may be disabled or performance may drop. “Charging On Hold” means charging resumes once the temperature is normal.

3. If you see a temperature warning

Turn the iPhone off and move it somewhere cooler, out of direct sunlight, to cool down. Don't leave it in a car.
