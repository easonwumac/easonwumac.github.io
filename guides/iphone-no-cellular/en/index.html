iPhone shows No Service or SOS? Fix cellular problems

When there's no signal, start with Airplane Mode, then check carrier settings and Dual SIM.

1. Toggle Airplane Mode and restart

In Settings, turn on Airplane Mode for at least 15 seconds, then turn it off; if that doesn't help, restart the iPhone.

2. Update iOS and carrier settings

Update to the latest iOS. On Wi-Fi, go to Settings > General > About and install any carrier settings update offered.

3. Check the line and roaming

With Dual SIM, check the line is on in Settings > Cellular; when traveling, turn on Data Roaming. If problems remain, contact your carrier about the account, coverage and data plan.
