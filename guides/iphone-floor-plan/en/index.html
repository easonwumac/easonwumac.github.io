Measure a room and draw a floor plan with iPhone: LiDAR scan or by hand

Before moving, buying furniture or renovating, a floor plan makes every conversation easier.

1. Check whether your iPhone has LiDAR

According to Apple, iPhone 12 Pro, iPhone 12 Pro Max and later Pro models have a LiDAR Scanner, as do some iPad Pro models. Without LiDAR you can still draw a plan by hand.

2. Scan or draw

When scanning, walk slowly around the room so walls, doors, windows and furniture are captured; to draw by hand, measure each wall first.

3. Re-measure on site

Phone measurements are approximate. Before ordering made-to-measure furniture or starting work, re-check key dimensions with a tape measure and have a qualified professional review the plans.

Roomory scans rooms with a LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad, capturing walls, doors, windows and furniture; without LiDAR you can draw multi-room, multi-floor plans by hand. Then switch to 3D to try materials and daylight, which helps when talking to designers and contractors. Scanning, modelling and plan editing are free.
