Is there enough light for your plant? Compare spots at home with your phone camera

Leggy growth and pale leaves often mean too little light. Eyes are bad at telling how much two spots differ; numbers make it clear.

1. Compare spots at the same time of day

Measure the window sill, the middle of the room and a corner at the same time of day to see the relative difference.

2. Measure a few times a day

Morning, noon and afternoon light differ a lot, and so do windows facing different ways.

3. Treat the numbers as a guide

A phone camera gives approximate brightness, good for comparing spots but not a professional light meter; judge it together with how the plant is actually doing.
