想知道下次月經大概哪天來，先要算對週期長度，而且至少要有幾次紀錄。

1. 從這次第一天算到下次第一天

週期長度是從月經來的第一天，算到下一次月經來的第一天，不是月經持續的天數。

2. 週期本來就會變動

依英國 NHS 說明，週期平均約 28 天，21 到 35 天都算常見；間隔少於 21 天或超過 35 天屬於不規律，可以和醫師討論。

3. 多記幾次，預測才有意義

只有一次紀錄時無法判斷規律，連續記錄幾個週期，預測才比較接近你的節奏。預測只是參考，不能用來避孕。

When is my next period? How to count your cycle length

To estimate when your next period will start, you first need to count your cycle length correctly, from more than one cycle.

1. Count from the first day of one period to the first day of the next

Cycle length runs from the first day of one period to the first day of the next, not the number of days you bleed.

2. Cycles naturally vary

According to the NHS, the average cycle is about 28 days and 21 to 35 days is common; gaps under 21 or over 35 days count as irregular and are worth discussing with a doctor.

3. Several cycles make the estimate meaningful

One record shows no pattern; logging several cycles in a row brings the estimate closer to your rhythm. A prediction is only a guide and cannot be used for contraception.
