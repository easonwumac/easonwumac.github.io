Track your period in the iPhone Health app

The built-in Health app can log your period, show predictions and export your records.

1. Set up Cycle Tracking

Open the Health app and tap the Browse tab > Cycle Tracking > Get Started. To log, swipe to the right day and tap its oval, scroll down to add other details, then tap Done.

2. Predictions, notifications and export

In Options, turn on Period Predictions and Period Notifications; Cycle History exports the last 12 months as a PDF.

Daikon marks period days on a colorful cycle ring with a diary page for every day; it can import past records from Apple Health, sync periods back to Health and export a PDF.

Free on the App Store
