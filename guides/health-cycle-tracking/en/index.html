Track your period in the iPhone Health app

The built-in Health app can log your period, show predictions and export a record for your doctor.

1. Set up Cycle Tracking

Open Health, tap the Browse tab > Cycle Tracking > Get Started. To log, swipe to the day and tap the oval, scroll down to add details, then tap Done.

2. Predictions, notifications and export

In Options, turn on Period Predictions, Period Notifications, Fertile Window Estimates and Fertility Notifications; Cycle History exports the last 12 months as a PDF. Apple notes it isn't birth control and shouldn't be used to diagnose conditions.

Daikon marks period days and the estimated fertile window on a colorful cycle ring with a diary page for every day; it can import past records from Apple Health, sync periods back to Health and export a PDF for your doctor.

Free on the App Store
