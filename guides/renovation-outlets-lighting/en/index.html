Planning outlets, lights and plumbing before a renovation: put them on the floor plan first

Too few outlets or a switch on the wrong side usually shows up only after moving in. Drawing furniture and utilities on one plan before work starts makes every discussion clearer.

1. Place furniture first, then outlets

The appliances by the bed, desk, TV and kitchen counter decide where outlets go and how many you need.

2. Lights and switches follow how you move

Think about the path when you come in, go to bed or get up at night, and put the switches along it.

3. Confirm water and drain routes early

Kitchen, bathroom and washer positions drive the pipework, so settle them early; every electrical and plumbing layout must be confirmed by qualified tradespeople.
