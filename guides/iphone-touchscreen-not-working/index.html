螢幕對觸控太敏感，或時好時壞沒反應時，先照 Apple 的順序排除，再決定要不要送修。

1. 重新啟動並清潔螢幕

先重新啟動 iPhone，並確認螢幕乾淨，沒有碎屑或水漬。

2. 拔掉配件

拔除所有 Lightning 或 USB-C 配件。如果拔掉後螢幕恢復正常，改用其他插座、連接線或充電器試試。

3. 取下保護殼與保護貼

取下保護殼與螢幕保護貼再測一次。仍無法正常運作，就透過 Apple 的「取得維修服務」安排維修。

iPhone touchscreen not responding or too sensitive?

If the screen is too sensitive or responds only some of the time, work through Apple's steps before booking a repair.

1. Restart and clean the screen

Restart your iPhone and make sure the screen is clean and free of debris or water.

2. Disconnect accessories

Disconnect any Lightning or USB-C accessories. If the screen works after removing one, try a different outlet, cable or charger.

3. Remove cases and screen protectors

Remove any case or screen protector and test again. If it still doesn't work, use Apple's Get service link to arrange a repair.
