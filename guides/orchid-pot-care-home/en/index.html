Potted orchid care: how to check when a moth orchid needs water

For moth orchids grown in sphagnum moss, use moss color to assess moisture and consider light and ventilation. Both too much and too little water can cause problems.

1. Identify the orchid and medium

This guidance concerns moth orchids in sphagnum moss. The Taoyuan research station notes that moss holds substantial water and overwatering can rot roots.

2. Check moss color

Wet moss is darker brown and dry moss lighter brown. The station suggests watering when about two-thirds of the moss in the pot is light brown.

3. Do not assume soft leaves need more water

Too little water can cause drooping, soft, wrinkled leaves, but frequent watering can damage roots. Check the medium before adding water.

4. Review light and temperature

Consider a brighter window position or supplemental light if indoors is dim. Avoid sustained summer heat and winter cold, and pay attention to ventilation.

5. Check the moss after flowering

After flowering, the station recommends cutting the flower stalk at its base and replacing moss as needed before deteriorating aeration harms the roots.
