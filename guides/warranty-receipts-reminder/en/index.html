How to keep warranties and receipts: snap them, note the expiry, get reminded

People remember the warranty only when something breaks, then cannot find the receipt or find it has expired. One extra step at purchase saves the trouble.

1. Photograph or screenshot it right away

Paper receipts fade and warranty cards get lost; photograph the receipt, warranty card and order page, and keep the originals too.

2. Note the expiry date

Write down the actual expiry date from the purchase date rather than working it out later.

3. Remind yourself before it ends

Check the item a month before expiry, while repairs are still covered.

Tuck reads the receipts and warranties you save on your phone, shows the warranty expiry and reminds you a month before; receipts group by month, Taiwan e-invoice QR codes fill in the number, date and total, and a month of receipts exports as CSV and PDF. No sign-in.
