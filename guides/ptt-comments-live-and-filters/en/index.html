How to follow PTT comments with live updates, filters, and notifications

Folkline follows new comments in active PTT threads and offers filters for reactions, the author, or a specific ID. Follow a post for notifications when you want to return later.

1. Read comments alongside the post

Comments appear as chat-style messages, with the post author's comments highlighted. Read the post and author additions before interpreting other responses; a comment count alone does not explain the discussion.

2. Narrow the view with filters

Filter by positive, negative, or neutral comments, the author, images, or a particular ID. Choose the author filter for updates or an ID filter to follow one participant's contributions.

3. Navigate a long thread

The store description lists a comment trend chart and floor scrubber. Use the scrubber to move through a long thread and filters to narrow what you read, then follow live updates for new comments.

4. Use live reading and notifications for different needs

Active threads can follow new comments live. Following authors, posts, and keywords can notify you about new posts or comments; use live reading while engaged and notifications to return later.

5. Use a PTT account to comment

Folkline's account features include comments, posts, and replies and require a PTT account. Submitted content is read back for confirmation; distinguish reading a thread from participating in it.

Folkline offers comment filters, live threads, and follow notifications. Posts and comment views have no ads.
