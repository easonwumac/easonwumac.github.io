How to start Prawnlight: stock, cash flow, and expansion

In Prawnlight you manage ordering, stocking prawns, layout and staff; you are the owner, not an angler. You start with a loan, so take care of supplies, rent and the Sunday repayment first, then think about more land and branches, so expanding too fast does not drain your cash.

1. Compare location costs and customers

The Taiwan map offers eight locations with different rent, foot traffic, and customer spending power. Compare those factors together when choosing where to open.

2. Plan around the loan and operating expenses

The description warns that stock costs, rent, and Sunday loan payments arrive before revenue. Consider those expenses before expansion rather than spending all available cash on new facilities.

3. Manage stock and the hall layout

Order stock, release prawns, and arrange facilities such as pools, stools, grills, and drink refrigerators. Start with these operating tasks before incorporating fishing competitions and hourly releases.

4. Staff the register and floor

Hire cashiers and floor staff, each with different abilities and personalities. Consider staffing alongside facilities when growing the hall rather than focusing only on more pools or decorations.

5. Expand after covering existing commitments

You can lease or buy up to ten neighboring lots for the hall, parking, a stir-fry restaurant, a tackle shop, or a pavilion, and open branches at the required level. The description warns that growing too fast can empty the till.

Prawnlight is a Taiwanese indoor prawn-fishing hall management game with location selection, supplies, layout, hiring, and expansion.
