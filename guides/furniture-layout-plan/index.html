Before buying furniture, lay it out on a floor plan: will the sofa, bed and cabinets fit?

Finding out on delivery day that the sofa blocks a door or a cabinet cannot open is the most common and costly mistake. Laying it out on a plan first avoids most of it.

1. Measure the room, and the doors and hallways

Besides wall lengths, door widths, hallways and stair turns decide whether furniture can get in at all; mark windows, outlets and the air conditioner too.

2. Place pieces at their real size

Use the length, width and depth from the product page, then check how much room cabinet doors, drawers and pulled-out chairs need.

3. Look again from another angle

A plan shows position; 3D shows how crowded a room feels and how you move through it. Before ordering, re-check key dimensions on site with a tape measure.
