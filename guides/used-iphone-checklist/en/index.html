What to check when buying a used iPhone: an in-person checklist

You usually get a few minutes at the meetup. Check in this order to avoid a locked phone, replaced parts and broken features.

1. Make sure Activation Lock is off

If the phone shows “iPhone Locked to Owner” at startup, or setup asks for the previous owner's Apple Account, it is still linked to their account. Apple advises not taking ownership until the device has been removed from the previous owner's account. Ask the seller to erase it in front of you and check that it returns to the Hello setup screen.

2. Check parts and service history

Go to Settings > General > About and look at Parts and Service History. An “Unknown Part” label can mean a non-genuine or unverified replacement part, and it can affect trade-in value.

3. Check battery health

Go to Settings > Battery > Battery Health to see Maximum Capacity, measured relative to when the battery was new. A low number means shorter battery life, which is worth factoring into the price.

4. Test every function

  • Display: look for bright or dead pixels and burn-in on solid colours.
  • Touch: dragging should work across the whole screen, corners included.
  • Try every camera, the speakers and each microphone by recording and playing back.
  • Face ID or Touch ID, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and charging.
