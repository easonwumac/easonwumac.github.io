Free floor plan app: what can you draw and export?

A free drawing tool may charge for professional exports. In Roomory, check the editing tools, included 3D assets, and output formats separately.

1. Check what is included

Roomory includes scanning, modeling, plan editing, multiple projects, and starter 3D furniture and materials for free, with ads.

2. Check your device

Room scanning requires a LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad. Devices without LiDAR can still create and edit plans manually.

3. Match exports to your needs

A3 PDF drawing sets, editable DXF, SVG, and USDZ and GLB models are Pro features. Check the required format before subscribing.

4. Check backup and sync

Project backups and iCloud sync are included for free. Sync requires an iCloud account and access enabled for Roomory.

5. Have plans checked before construction

The listing requires on-site checks of scanned dimensions and qualified professional review before construction. An exported plan has no professional sign-off.

Roomory includes free plan editing and starter 3D assets; Pro adds professional exports through a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription.
