How to start playing Precision Parking 3D

In Precision Parking 3D, start with the three tutorial levels to practise steering, pedals and gears, then take on the fixed levels. Parking, zero collisions and the extra challenge are separate goals you can complete over replays, so you do not need full stars the first time.

1. Begin with the three tutorials

The first three tutorials cover steering, pedals, and gear changes. Begin there before progressing; denser parking lots start at level four.

2. Choose a steering method

Choose left/right buttons, swipe steering, or a rotating steering wheel. Start with whichever you find manageable, then compare the alternatives after learning the controls.

3. Compare the available views

The game offers third-person and overhead views. Compare them while learning and use the one that makes the parking space clearest to you.

4. Read the three objectives

Each level has parking, zero-collision, and extra-challenge objectives that can be completed across replays. From level four, hitting another vehicle ends the attempt; use the next attempt to work on unfinished goals.

5. Progress after completing a level

The game has 40 fixed levels across five chapters. Completing a level unlocks the next, with up to 120 stars to collect. Core levels work offline, and progress and achievements are stored on the device.

Precision Parking 3D offers three steering methods and two views on iPhone and iPad.
