Too many iPhone screenshots? Sort bookings, receipts and recipes

The trouble with screenshots isn't saving them; it's finding them later. The key is making every screenshot searchable by its text.

1. Separate what you need to keep

Bookings, tickets, receipts, warranties and documents will be needed on a specific date; restaurants, recipes and things to buy are for later. Most of the rest can go once seen.

2. Make screenshots searchable by text

Later you usually remember only a shop name or a few words, so the text in each screenshot needs to be searchable.

3. Set reminders for important dates

Bookings, return windows and warranty expiry are easiest to forget, so decide on a reminder when you save them.

Tuck's AI reads your screenshots and files on the device, recognises bookings, tickets, receipts, orders, warranties and documents, and files them automatically, with restaurants, recipes and things to buy in their own folders. Find them again with a few words and set reminders for important dates. Once saved, screenshots can be deleted from Photos in one tap.
