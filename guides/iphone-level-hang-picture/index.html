Hanging a picture straight with the iPhone level

不用另外買水平尺，iPhone 內建的「測距儀」就有水平儀，掛畫、裝層板都用得到。

1. 打開水平儀

打開「工具程式」資料夾裡的「測距儀」，點「水平儀」，把 iPhone 貼著要檢查的物品，例如相框。

2. 轉到變綠色

轉動物品或 iPhone，直到畫面變成綠色就是水平。要讓兩個物品同一個斜度，先點一下螢幕記下第一個的角度，再貼著第二個轉到變綠。數值是約略值，再點一下螢幕可以重置。

Hanging a picture straight with the iPhone level

No spirit level needed: the built-in Measure app has one for hanging pictures and shelves.

1. Open the level

Open Measure in the Utilities folder, select Level, and hold the iPhone against the object, such as a picture frame.

2. Turn until it's green

Turn the object and iPhone until the display shows green. To match a slope, touch the screen to record the first angle, then turn the second object until green. Readings are approximate; touch the screen again to reset.
