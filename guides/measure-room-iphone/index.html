Measure rooms and furniture with iPhone: how to use the Measure app

看房、買家具前想先知道大概的尺寸，可以用 iPhone 內建的「測距儀」量，再把數字記到平面圖上。

1. 打開「測距儀」並掃描

「測距儀」在「工具程式」資料夾裡。打開後慢慢移動手機掃描周圍，讓要量的物體出現在畫面中。

2. 量長度

把畫面中央的點對準起點，點「加入」，移到終點再點「加入」就會顯示長度；點「清除」可以重來。量到方形物體時出現白框，點白框就能直接看到長寬。

3. 量得準一點

Apple 建議量 0.5 到 3 公尺外、輪廓清楚的物體，而且結果是近似值。要下訂或施工前，還是用捲尺複量一次。

Measure rooms and furniture with iPhone: how to use the Measure app

Before viewing a flat or buying furniture, get rough sizes with the built-in Measure app, then put the numbers on a floor plan.

1. Open Measure and scan

Measure is in the Utilities folder. Open it and slowly move the phone around so the object you want to measure is on screen.

2. Measure a length

Line up the dot at the center of the screen with the start point and tap Add, move to the end point and tap Add to see the length; tap Clear to start over. When a white box frames a rectangular object, tap it to see its dimensions.

3. Get closer to the real size

Apple recommends measuring well-defined objects 0.5 to 3 meters away, and measurements are approximate. Check with a tape measure before ordering or building.
