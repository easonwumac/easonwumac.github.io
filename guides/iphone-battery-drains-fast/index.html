耗電變快時，先看系統的電池解析，再調整網路與耗電模式；電池老化則看電池健康度。

1. 看建議與每日用量

到「設定」>「電池」，建議與解析會顯示在「每日用量」上方。常見的耗電類型有背景活動、沒有收訊與低訊號、通知，以及曾連接充電器。

2. 調整網路與耗電模式

能用 Wi-Fi 就用 Wi-Fi，沒訊號時可以開飛航模式。「設定」>「電池」>「耗電模式」可以開低耗電模式，電量超過 80% 時會自動關閉。剛更新完的幾天，背景工作也可能較耗電。

3. 看電池健康度

iPhone 15 以後在「設定」>「電池」>「電池健康度」，iPhone 14 以前在「電池健康度與充電」。旁邊顯示「服務」時，可以考慮更換電池。

機況 Inside 可以從匯入的 Analytics 紀錄帶入電池循環次數；機況 Pro 另有電池健康趨勢與每月記錄提醒。 App Store

iPhone battery draining fast? Check these in Battery settings

When the battery drains faster, read the system's battery insights, then adjust network and power settings; for an aging battery, check Battery Health.

1. Read suggestions and Daily Usage

Go to Settings > Battery; suggestions and insights appear above Daily Usage. Common usage types include Background Activity, No Coverage and Low Signal, Notifications and Connected to Charger.

2. Adjust network and Low Power Mode

Use Wi-Fi when you can, and Airplane Mode when there's no coverage. Turn on Low Power Mode in Settings > Battery > Power Mode; it turns off above 80%. For a few days after an update, background tasks can use more battery.

3. Check Battery Health

On iPhone 15 and later it's Settings > Battery > Battery Health; on iPhone 14 and earlier, Battery Health & Charging. If Service appears, consider a battery replacement.

Inside can bring in battery cycle counts from an imported Analytics log; Inside Pro adds a battery health trend and a monthly reminder to log it. App Store
