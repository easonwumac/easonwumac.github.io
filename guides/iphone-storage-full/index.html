跳出「儲存空間即將用完」時，先找出佔最多空間的項目，再決定要卸載 App、刪影片還是整理照片。

1. 看儲存空間清單

到「設定」>「一般」>「iPhone 儲存空間」，可以看到系統的建議，以及每個 App 用了多少空間。點 App 名稱可以看詳細資料；快取與暫存資料不一定算在用量裡。

2. 卸載或刪除 App

在 App 的詳細畫面可以「卸載 App」，釋出 App 本身的空間但保留文件與資料；也可以「刪除 App」，連同相關資料一起移除。

3. 用系統建議清理

在建議區點「全部顯示」，可以直接「啟用」某項建議，或點進去檢查有哪些內容可以刪除。Apple 也建議移除影片與用不到的 App。

注意：系統不會自動刪除 iCloud 內容或快取檔案；手機需要空間時才會自己清掉快取。

iPhone storage almost full? First see what is using it

When the “Storage Almost Full” alert appears, find what takes the most space first, then decide whether to offload apps, delete videos or clean up photos.

1. Check the storage list

Go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage to see recommendations and how much space each app uses. Tap an app's name for details; cached and temporary data might not count as usage.

2. Offload or delete apps

In an app's detail screen you can offload the app, which frees its space but keeps its documents and data, or delete the app together with its data.

3. Use the recommendations

Tap Show All in the recommendations, then tap Enable or open a recommendation to review what you can delete. Apple also suggests removing videos and apps you no longer need.

Note: the device cannot automatically delete iCloud content or cached files; it removes cached files itself when it needs space.
