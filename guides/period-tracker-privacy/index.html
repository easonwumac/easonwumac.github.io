經期紀錄是很私人的健康資料。挑 App 時，除了好不好用，也要看資料存在哪裡、會不會拿去投放廣告。

1. 資料存在哪裡

看 App 是否需要帳號、資料是否只存在裝置或你自己的 iCloud，以及健康資料會不會交給廣告。

2. 預測的依據

預測只是參考，週期可能變化；漏記時預測是否還會繼續，也很重要。

3. 能不能匯出

看醫生時，能匯出一份完整的週期紀錄會很有幫助。

Choosing a period tracker: privacy, predictions and export

Period data is personal health data. Beyond ease of use, check where it is stored and whether it is used for advertising.

1. Where your data lives

Check whether the app needs an account, whether data stays on the device or in your own iCloud, and whether health data is shared with advertising.

2. What predictions are based on

Predictions are only estimates and cycles can vary; it also matters whether predictions keep going when you miss an entry.

3. Can you export it

Being able to export a full cycle history helps when you see a doctor.

Daikon needs no account and has no social features or subscription. Records stay on the device and sync only through your private iCloud when available; health data is never sent to ad SDKs. It uses up to your last six cycles, keeps predicting if you miss an entry, exports a PDF for your doctor, and can optionally sync with Apple Health. Predictions are for reference only, not medical advice, and not for contraception. App Store
