Seeing a gynecologist? Bring your period records in one place

Doctors often ask when your last period started, how long your cycle is and what symptoms you have. Having it ready means not answering from memory.

1. Start and end dates of each period

Bring at least the last few periods so the doctor can see your cycle length and how it changes.

2. Symptoms and what keeps recurring

Which days cramps, headaches, mood or sleep changes happen is more useful than a single description.

3. One record you can hand over

Print it or keep a PDF to show the doctor, which is faster than scrolling on your phone.

Daikon records each cycle and daily symptoms, spots what keeps recurring after a few cycles, and exports a PDF for your doctor; past records can be imported from CSV or Apple Health. Predictions are a guide only, not medical advice. App Store
