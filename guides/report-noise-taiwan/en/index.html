How to report construction noise in Taiwan: phone and online options

Report construction noise to the local environmental protection bureau or the pollution complaint system. For urgent or ongoing pollution, call 0800-066-666 with the time, location, and description.

1. Choose the appropriate authority

Environmental authorities receive pollution complaints. Police handle noncontinuous or difficult-to-measure disturbances to daily life; neighbor commotion and construction equipment may need different reporting routes.

2. Call about urgent or ongoing pollution

Use the national pollution complaint line, 0800-066-666, or contact the local environmental protection bureau. Give the source's precise address and a concrete description so officials can arrange an investigation.

3. Use the official online complaint system

Submit an online case through the Environmental Management Administration's pollution complaint system. The Ministry also says urgent or ongoing pollution can be reported directly on the national complaint line, 0800-066-666.

4. Prepare the required incident details

Government guidance asks for your real name and contact details, a description, dates and times, a precise location, and supporting photos or video. Explain the site and affected location rather than submitting only a sound level.

5. Separate reporting hours from violation criteria

The Ministry says environmental bureaus receive pollution complaints around the clock. Reporting availability is separate from the rules used to assess a violation; reporting is not limited to nighttime.
