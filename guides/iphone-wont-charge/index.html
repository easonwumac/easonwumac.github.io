充電沒反應時，多半是線材、充電埠或溫度的問題。照 Apple 的步驟逐一排除。

1. 檢查線材與充電埠

檢查充電線和 USB 轉接器有沒有破損或插頭彎曲，有損壞就不要用。清掉充電埠的碎屑，把線牢牢插好，接上牆壁插座後重新啟動 iPhone。

2. 先充半小時

充電約半小時。如果還是無法開機或只顯示電池圖像，強制重新啟動後再充半小時；仍然不行就可能需要維修。

3. 停在 80% 不一定是壞掉

「最佳化電池充電」、電池過熱，或 iPhone 15 以後的「充電上限」設定，都會讓電量停在 80%。出現「配件不支援」時，換一條線或充電器並更新 iOS。

iPhone won't charge, or stops at 80%?

When charging doesn't start, the cable, the port or the temperature is usually the cause. Rule them out one by one following Apple.

1. Check the cable and port

Inspect the cable and USB adapter for damage or bent prongs and don't use damaged ones. Clear debris from the charging port, plug in firmly, connect to a wall outlet and restart the iPhone.

2. Charge for half an hour

Charge for about half an hour. If it still won't turn on or shows only a battery icon, force restart and charge another half hour; if that fails, it may need service.

3. Stopping at 80% isn't always a fault

Optimized Battery Charging, a warm battery, or the Charge Limit on iPhone 15 and later can all stop charging at 80%. For an “accessory not supported” alert, try another cable or charger and update iOS.
