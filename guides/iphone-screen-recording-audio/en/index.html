How to screen record on iPhone, and what to do when there is no sound

Start in Control Center. To include your voice, hold the recording control and enable Microphone. Some apps restrict recording, and screen mirroring cannot run at the same time.

1. Add the recording control

Open Control Center. If you do not see the Screen Recording control, add it to Control Center; if it is already there, use it.

2. Start recording

Tap Screen Recording, wait for the three-second countdown, then leave Control Center and perform the actions you want to capture.

3. Include narration

Touch and hold Screen Recording, enable Microphone, then tap Start Recording to add your voice.

4. Check recording restrictions

Some apps do not permit recording their content. Screen recording also cannot run alongside screen mirroring. Check these restrictions first.

5. Stop and find the video

On iPhone with the Dynamic Island, tap the red circle, then the stop button. On other models, tap the red timer at the top left, then tap Stop. Find the video in Photos > Collections > Media Types > Screen Recordings.

Inside also offers microphone checks to record how the microphone responds during a check.
