What are Taiwan’s noise limits in decibels? Businesses, construction and loudspeakers

Taiwan’s noise limits depend on the source, the control zone and the time of day. For example, entertainment and business premises in a Class 2 zone are limited to 57 dB(A) by day, 52 in the evening and 47 at night (20 Hz to 20 kHz).

1. Start with the source category

The Noise Control Standards list separate limits for factories, entertainment and business premises, construction and loudspeaker equipment. Air-conditioning units, building sites and publicity vans cannot be compared against the same row just because they are all loud.

2. Identify the control zone

The tables distinguish four noise-control zones. Ask the local environmental protection bureau which zone applies to the measurement address instead of selecting a zone from the neighborhood's appearance.

3. Use the zone's time periods

Daytime is 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Evening ends at 10 p.m. in zones 1 and 2, and 11 p.m. in zones 3 and 4. Nighttime then runs until 7 a.m.; record the time before selecting a column.

4. Match the frequency range and metric

The standards distinguish frequency ranges, including 20 Hz–20 kHz and 20–200 Hz. Leq is an energy average over time; Lmax is the maximum during a measurement. A peak alone is not interchangeable with every assessment metric.

5. Check whether a numerical limit applies

The Ministry of Environment directs noncontinuous or difficult-to-measure disturbances to the police. Describe what happened and when rather than treating a phone reading as a prerequisite for reporting.

6. Main limits (20 Hz to 20 kHz, dB(A))

Under the Noise Control Standards, these are the day / evening / night limits for control zones 1 to 4. In a designated special control zone, each limit is 5 dB(A) lower.

  • Entertainment and business premises: Class 1 55 / 50 / 40, Class 2 57 / 52 / 47, Class 3 67 / 57 / 52, Class 4 80 / 70 / 65.
  • Factories: Class 1 50 / 45 / 40, Class 2 57 / 52 / 47, Class 3 67 / 57 / 52, Class 4 80 / 70 / 65.
  • Construction, equivalent level (Leq): Class 1 67 / 47 / 47, Class 2 67 / 57 / 47, Class 3 72 / 67 / 62, Class 4 80 / 70 / 65; maximum level (Lmax): Classes 1–2 100 / 80 / 70, Classes 3–4 100 / 85 / 75.
  • Loudspeaker equipment: Class 1 57 / 47 / 40, Class 2 72 / 57 / 47, Class 3 77 / 62 / 52, Class 4 82 / 72 / 62.

Factories, business premises and construction also have separate low-frequency limits (20 Hz to 200 Hz), which are lower and are measured indoors with windows closed using a Class 1 sound level meter that meets the standard.
