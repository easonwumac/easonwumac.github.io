合約、收據、保固卡不用掃描器，用內建的「備忘錄」或「檔案」就能掃成清楚的文件。

1. 在「備忘錄」掃描

打開「備忘錄」，選取或新增一則備忘錄，點「附件」按鈕再點「掃描文件」。「自動」模式會自己拍；也可以點快門或按音量鍵，調整範圍後點「保留掃描檔」，最後點「完成」。

2. 在「檔案」掃描

在「檔案」App 點「更多」>「掃描文件」，掃完點「完成」，選擇儲存位置再點「儲存」。

收好 Tuck 也能用掃描加入文件，認出收據、保固與訂位，整理出日期、金額與地址，之後搜幾個字就找回；保固到期前一個月會提醒你。 App Store

Scan documents with iPhone: in Notes or Files

Contracts, receipts and warranty cards don't need a scanner: the built-in Notes or Files app scans them cleanly.

1. Scan in Notes

Open Notes, select or create a note, tap the Attachment button, then Scan Documents. Auto mode captures by itself; or tap the Shutter or press a Volume button, adjust the corners, tap Keep Scan, then Done.

2. Scan in Files

In the Files app, tap More > Scan Documents, tap Done when finished, choose a location and tap Save.

Tuck can add documents by scanning too, recognizes receipts, warranties and bookings, pulls out dates, amounts and addresses so a few words find them later, and reminds you a month before a warranty ends. App Store
