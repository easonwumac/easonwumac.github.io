Scan documents with iPhone: in Notes or Files

Contracts, receipts and warranty cards don't need a scanner: the built-in Notes or Files app scans them cleanly.

1. Scan in Notes

Open Notes, select or create a note, tap the Attachment button, then Scan Documents. Auto mode captures by itself; or tap the Shutter or press a Volume button, adjust the corners, tap Keep Scan, then Done.

2. Scan in Files

In the Files app, tap More > Scan Documents, tap Done when finished, choose a location and tap Save.

Tuck can add documents by scanning too, recognizes receipts, warranties and bookings, pulls out dates, amounts and addresses so a few words find them later, and reminds you a month before a warranty ends.

Free on the App Store

Sources: support.apple.com/en-us/108963
