How to take and save a full-page screenshot on iPhone

In Safari, take a screenshot, tap the thumbnail and select Full Page to capture beyond the screen. Preview the image on the right to confirm everything you need is included.

1. Open the webpage

Open the page in Safari, Apple’s example of content suitable for a full-page capture.

2. Capture and select Full Page

Quickly press and release the Side and Volume Up buttons together on a Face ID model; on iPhone SE, press and release the Home and Side buttons together. Tap the screenshot thumbnail in the lower left, then tap Full Page.

3. Review and crop

Drag along the preview on the right to inspect the capture. Use Crop and drag the handles to keep only the portion you need.

4. Save to Photos

To save a full-screen screenshot to your photo library, swipe up, or tap the Selected button and choose an option. Then find it in Photos > Collections > Media Types > Screenshots.

5. If Full Page is missing

Try a webpage in Safari. Apple documents full-page capture for content extending beyond the display; it does not promise scrolling capture in every app.
