How to study orb-puzzle routes with an iPhone screenshot

To study an orb-puzzle route, save the board first and compare the image with a route analysis. iPhone screenshots can be cropped and marked up, giving you a fixed board to review outside live play.

1. Capture the board you want to review

On a Face ID iPhone, press and quickly release the side and volume-up buttons together. On a Touch ID model with a side button, use the Home and side buttons.

2. Open the screenshot preview

Apple describes a screenshot screen with quick tools in iOS 26. In iOS 18 and earlier, tap the temporary lower-left thumbnail to open it. These are system screenshot controls, separate from route analysis.

3. Prepare a board reference

You can crop, save, or share the screenshot. Apple's Markup tool lets you draw on an image to note positions you want to examine; distinguish the board itself from your annotations.

4. Distinguish image capture from route analysis

Apple's screenshot tools save and edit the screen. AutoCombo's store description lists recognizing the board image and planning the route; the two cover getting the image and analyzing the route, so do not treat a screenshot as an already calculated route.

5. Keep your notes with the image

Use screenshot drawing and saving tools to keep the board and your notes for later review. AutoCombo's listing describes image analysis and route planning; it does not establish additional unlisted control features.

AutoCombo's store description lists board-image recognition and orb-puzzle route planning.
