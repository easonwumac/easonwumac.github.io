Identify stars with your phone: four steps for beginners

No need to learn star charts: hold your phone up to the sky to see which star or constellation you're looking at.

1. Find somewhere darker

Get away from street lights and let your eyes adjust; when the Moon is bright, faint stars are harder to see.

2. See what's up tonight

Start with easy targets such as planets, bright stars or the Moon, then work outward to constellations.

3. Calibrate the compass

Phone compasses can drift, which makes the sky on screen line up wrongly.

4. Use red light

Red light disturbs dark adaptation far less than white light.

Nightvane covers more than 9,800 stars, all 88 constellations and the Messier objects, and labels stars, planets, constellations, the Moon and the Milky Way in place when you raise an iPhone or iPad.
