How to see the International Space Station with your own eyes

The International Space Station is one of the brightest objects in the night sky and needs no telescope. The trick is knowing when and where it will pass.

1. Look up passes for your location

The station is visible only when it passes over your location at the right time and direction, and the times differ from place to place.

2. Find the direction early

A pass usually lasts only a few minutes, so face the direction it will appear from in advance. It looks like a steady, bright, moving point that doesn't blink.

3. Protect your night vision

Looking at a bright phone ruins your dark adaptation, so use red light or dim the screen.
