How to search for PTT boards: categories and adding favorites

In Folkline, find PTT boards through topic groups, board categories, or fuzzy name search. Use favorites for regular reading and recent boards to return to something you visited.

1. Browse by topic if you do not know the name

Folkline groups popular posts and boards into topics such as news, sports, investing, technology, and games. Start with the relevant topic when you do not yet know a board's name.

2. Search when you know part of a name

The store description lists fuzzy board-name search. Use it when you have a name clue; its stated scope is board names, so distinguish it from searching post contents.

3. Explore the board categories

Besides hot boards, Folkline also offers board categories. Hot sections suit entering by topic area, while board categories are another way to browse; pick whichever fits the topic you are looking for.

4. Keep regular boards in favorites

Folkline lists favorites as a feature. Use that collection for boards you read regularly, keeping your everyday reading separate from boards you are still exploring.

5. Return through recent boards

Use recent boards when you forget the name of a board you just visited. On iPad, the board wall also displays multiple boards and live threads side by side.

Folkline offers topic groups, fuzzy board-name search, favorites, and recent boards.
