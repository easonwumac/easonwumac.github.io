How often should you water a plant? Check the soil and keep a log instead of a fixed schedule

A fixed rule like "once a week" is a common reason plants rot or dry out. How much water a plant needs changes with the season, the pot and the light.

1. Touch the soil before watering

Pushing a finger into the soil or lifting the pot to feel its weight is more reliable than the calendar. Needs vary widely between plants, so follow the care advice for that species.

2. Log each watering

With dates logged you can see how many days this pot takes to dry out in this season, and adjust reminders to match.

3. Watch again when the season changes

After the weather cools or the plant moves, it dries at a different pace; observe a few times before setting a new interval.
