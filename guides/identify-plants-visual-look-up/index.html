Identify a plant from a photo with iPhone Visual Look Up

不知道家裡的盆栽叫什麼？iPhone 的「圖像查詢」可以從照片認出植物和花。

1. 選取照片

在「備忘錄」、「郵件」或「訊息」等 App 中按住照片，點「資訊」按鈕（在 Safari 中點「查詢」）。iPhone 需要 iOS 15 以上。

2. 點葉子圖像

點照片上或下方出現的葉子圖像，就會看到 Siri Knowledge 和網路上的結果。如果「資訊」按鈕上沒有星形符號，表示這張照片不能用這個功能。

栽日 ZaiRi 是植物日誌：拍照記錄生長、從照片辨識植物、快速記下澆水施肥，並提醒到期的照護任務。所有植物日誌功能都免費。 App Store

Identify a plant from a photo with iPhone Visual Look Up

Not sure what a houseplant is called? iPhone's Visual Look Up can identify plants and flowers in a photo.

1. Select the photo

In an app such as Notes, Mail or Messages, touch and hold the image and tap the Info button (in Safari, tap Look Up). iPhone needs iOS 15 or later.

2. Tap the leaf icon

Tap the leaf icon on or below the photo to see results from Siri Knowledge and the web. If the Info button has no star, Visual Look Up isn't available for that photo.

ZaiRi is a plant journal: photograph growth, identify plants from photos, log watering and fertilizing quickly, and get reminders for care tasks. Every journal feature is free. App Store
