iPhone screenshots missing? Check deleted and hidden photos

Check Recently Deleted and Hidden, then iCloud syncing and library views. Deleted photos can be recovered within 30 days; after that, they are permanently deleted.

1. Check Recently Deleted

In Photos, open Recently Deleted under Utilities, tap View Album, and unlock it. Deleted images remain there for 30 days.

2. Recover the image

Open the image, tap Recover, and confirm. For several images, select them and choose Recover from the more menu.

3. Look in Hidden

Open Photos > Collections > Utilities > Hidden and unlock it. Hidden items appear only in this album and can be unhidden from there.

4. Check iCloud and your account

Check Sync this iPhone in Settings > your name > iCloud > Photos. Devices need the same Apple Account. Connect to Wi-Fi and allow syncing time before looking again.

5. Review Shared Library views

If you use iCloud Shared Photo Library, check whether you are viewing only your Personal or Shared Library. Apple recommends checking the Shared Library for missing images too.

Tuck keeps the originals of saved items, which you can open from the item.
