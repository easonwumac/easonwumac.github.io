How to add tickets, boarding passes and rewards cards to Apple Wallet

Boarding passes, event tickets, coupons and rewards cards that support Apple Wallet can be added once and opened on the spot.

1. Add it from the app or email

Open the app, email or notification with the ticket, tap Add to Apple Wallet and follow the onscreen steps; tap Add in the upper-right corner if asked.

2. If there's no Add option

A pass added to iPhone also appears on a paired Apple Watch. If you don't see the option, the issuer may not support Wallet; contact them to check.

3. Open it fast at the gate

Turn on Double-Click Side Button in Settings > Wallet & Apple Pay, then double-click the side button to open Wallet even when locked. Passes with a time or place also appear on the Lock Screen when they become relevant.

4. Tickets that are only a PDF or screenshot

Wallet only accepts passes the issuer provides; a PDF, screenshot or QR code on a web page does not become a Wallet pass by itself. Keep them somewhere you can find quickly, and open the code before the gate with the screen bright enough to scan.

5. Share a ticket with someone

Open the Wallet app, tap the ticket and tap the Share button; you can also hold your iPhone near the top of the other person’s iPhone and tap Share when the pass appears. If there is no Share button, the issuer has not enabled sharing in Wallet, so check their app or website for other ways to share.
