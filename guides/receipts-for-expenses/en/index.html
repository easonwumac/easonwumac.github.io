How to keep receipts and e-invoices so month-end expenses are easy

The slow part of expenses is hunting for receipts at month end. Save them as you go, group them by month, and export when it's time.

1. Photograph or screenshot right away

Paper receipts fade, so photograph them straight away; for online orders, take a screenshot or save the PDF.

2. Group by month

Grouping by month with a monthly total is the quickest way to match card statements or file expenses.

3. Export at month end

Saved to Tuck, receipts are grouped by month with totals, and a month of receipts exports as CSV and PDF for expenses. E-invoice QR codes fill in the invoice number, date and total, and monthly spending can be broken down by category.
