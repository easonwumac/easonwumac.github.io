How to play Pow3: match gems, break rock, and meet the level goal

Swap gems into matches of three to break neighboring rock in Pow3. Clearing a rock row digs deeper. Check whether the level asks for depth, gold, or a core before spending its limited moves.

1. Check the goal and move limit

Levels ask you to dig a set distance, release gold nuggets from rock, or break a core before running out of moves. Identify the current goal before swapping gems.

2. Make matches beside rock

A line of three gems clears and damages adjacent rock. Clearing a full rock row digs one meter. Relate the match location to the rock you need to remove.

3. Learn the special-match shapes

Four in a row creates a jackhammer, an L or T creates a powder keg, and five creates a prism. Specials trigger each other; consider these shapes in relation to the current objective.

4. Watch the core's effects

Cores can freeze gems, turn gems into stone, and shake the board. Deeper levels also introduce frozen gems, amethyst, boulders, and lava, so reassess the board when conditions change.

5. Finish the objective and preserve moves

Finishing early converts unused moves into points and adds a finale with up to eight jackhammers. Complete the objective first, then use that rule to understand the value of saving moves.

Pow3 is a mine-themed gem-swapping game with limited-move digging, gold, and core objectives.
