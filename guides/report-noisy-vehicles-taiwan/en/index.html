How to report a noisy vehicle in Taiwan: plate, time, and road details

Use the Ministry of Environment's noisy-vehicle reporting website for suspected excessive vehicle noise. Report promptly within 30 days with the vehicle type, plate, time, road, and disturbance details or evidence.

1. Use the dedicated vehicle-noise service

The Ministry provides a dedicated reporting website for suspected excessive noise from vehicles in use. It is a vehicle-noise service, rather than a channel for construction or neighbor complaints.

2. Report within 30 days

The service asks for reports as soon as possible within 30 days of the observation. Keep the actual date and time instead of a vague description such as “one evening recently.”

3. Include the vehicle and location details

Required details include vehicle type, plate, observation time and road, and disturbance facts or violation evidence. Check each item so the noise report identifies a particular vehicle.

4. Provide the requested reporter information

The website requests a real name, daytime contact number, address, and national ID or new UI number for verification when needed. Provide these through the official form.

5. Distinguish noise from modification complaints

When reporting noise, describe the sound and what happened. The official website has a separate link for reporting illegally modified vehicles; use that link for a modification complaint.
