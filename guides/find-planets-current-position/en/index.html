How to find the planets’ current positions

Set your observing location and time, then check each planet’s rise and set times and direction. A fixed chart is not enough for every location and hour.

1. Select the observing city

Nightvane can display any city’s sky in its local time, including a destination you plan to visit.

2. Set the date and time

Move through time in Nightvane to see the sky at a chosen hour or date. Check whether you are viewing now or your planned session.

3. Search and check rise and set times

Search for a planet and tap it to view rise and set times and brightness, then choose a target for the session.

4. Follow the direction guide

Arrows and gentle haptics guide you toward the target. Correct compass alignment by lining up the Moon or a bright star.

5. Understand what the eye can see

NASA lists Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn as easily seen without a telescope. Planets move against the stars and tend to shine more steadily.

Nightvane searches planets, shows rise and set times and brightness, and lists worthwhile targets for your location; it is free with ads and has no subscription.
