Set up Back Tap screenshots—or stop accidental captures

Back Tap can take screenshots when you double- or triple-tap the phone’s back. If captures seem accidental, check both assignments and set unwanted ones to None.

1. Check compatibility

Apple documents Back Tap for iPhone 8 and later. It is available from iOS 14; Apple recommends keeping iOS up to date.

2. Open Back Tap

Open Settings > Accessibility > Touch > Back Tap.

3. Assign Screenshot

Choose Double Tap or Triple Tap, then Screenshot. Return to the content you want and tap the back the assigned number of times.

4. Remove an unwanted assignment

Open the assigned Double Tap or Triple Tap action and choose None. Check the other assignment separately.

5. Keep button capture available

With Face ID, quickly press and release Side and Volume Up. With a Home button, use Home and the Side or Top button.

Share screenshots you want to keep to Tuck and find them later by text.
