買賣二手機或找配件時，先確認機型。Apple 提供了一份依型號查詢的對照表。

1. 先找到型號

依 Apple 另一篇說明找到 iPhone 的型號；較舊的機型（例如 iPhone 7）型號印在背蓋上。

2. 在 Apple 的列表中對照

Apple 的「辨識 iPhone 機型」列表依機型列出年份、容量、顏色，以及不同國家或地區的型號，用型號就能對到正確機型。

機況 Inside 的硬體資訊頁會顯示即時讀到的裝置資訊並標明來源，3D 探索也會依機型呈現外觀。 App Store

Which iPhone do I have? Look it up by model number

Before buying or selling a used phone or picking accessories, confirm the model. Apple keeps a list you can look up by model number.

1. Find the model number

Find your iPhone's model number following Apple's separate article; on older models such as iPhone 7 it is printed on the back cover.

2. Match it in Apple's list

Apple's “Identify your iPhone model” list gives each model's year, storage, colors and model numbers by region, so the model number points to the exact model.

Inside's hardware page shows live device details with their sources, and its 3D explorer shows the look of your iPhone model. App Store

Sources: support.apple.com/en-us/108044 · support.apple.com/en-us/106343
