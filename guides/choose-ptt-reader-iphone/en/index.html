How to choose a PTT reader for iPhone

Choose an iPhone PTT reader by matching board discovery, formatting, comment tools, and account features to your reading habits. Folkline's store description gives you specific features to compare with your needs.

1. Match board discovery to your habits

For discovery, look at categories and search. Folkline lists topic groups, categories, fuzzy board-name search, favorites, and recent boards, covering both exploration and returning to familiar boards.

2. Check formatting for long posts

If you read ASCII art or tables, check layout support. Folkline describes preserving those layouts and offering a full-post BBS-style 80-column view.

3. Check comment navigation

For busy discussions, check author highlighting and reaction or ID filters. Folkline also lists a floor scrubber, live comments, and follow notifications; compare those with the way you follow threads.

4. Check the account features you need

Only reading posts is a different need from pushing, posting and replying. The PTT account features Folkline lists also include mail, online friends, waterballs, voting and switching between accounts; choose by the features you will use.

5. Review ad placement and saved posts

Folkline says banners appear only in lists and settings, while posts, comments, and the editor are ad-free. Saved posts retain snapshots; consider both reading interruptions and later access when choosing.

Folkline is a PTT-compatible forum reader with board discovery, comment filters, and saved snapshots. It is not PTT's official app.
