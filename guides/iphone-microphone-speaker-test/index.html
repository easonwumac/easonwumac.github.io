通話時對方聽不清楚你說話、錄影錄不到聲音，或喇叭有雜音，送修前可以先自己檢查，也方便跟維修人員說明。

1. 先排除簡單原因

依 Apple 說明，先移除螢幕保護貼、膜或保護殼，清除開孔的灰塵，並確認沒有連著有線或無線耳機。

2. 每個麥克風分開測

iPhone 有不只一個麥克風，用語音備忘錄、前鏡頭與後鏡頭錄影各錄一段再播放，比較哪一個聲音偏弱。

3. App 聽不到就檢查權限

只有某個 App 收不到聲音時，到「設定」>「隱私權與安全性」>「麥克風」確認它有權限。仍有問題就聯絡 Apple 支援。

機況 Inside 可以逐項檢查揚聲器、每個麥克風與左右聲道，用四段測試音指出偏弱的頻段，再存成報告分享給維修人員。基本檢查免費。 App Store

iPhone microphone or speaker acting up? Test them yourself first

If callers cannot hear you, videos record no sound or the speaker crackles, check it yourself before a repair, which also helps you explain the problem.

1. Rule out the simple causes

Following Apple, remove any screen protector, film or case, clear debris from the openings, and make sure no wired or wireless headset is connected.

2. Test each microphone separately

An iPhone has more than one microphone; record a voice memo, a front-camera video and a rear-camera video and play them back to see which sounds weak.

3. If one app cannot hear you, check its permission

If only one app cannot hear you, check Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. If problems remain, contact Apple Support.

Inside checks the speakers, each microphone and left and right channels one by one, uses four test tones to point out a weak frequency band, and saves a report you can share with a repair shop. Basic checks are free. App Store
