iPhone 喇叭沒聲音或有雜音？先檢查這幾件事

No sound or distorted sound from your iPhone speaker?

喇叭突然沒聲音，常常只是靜音或勿擾模式開著。照這個順序檢查，就知道是設定還是硬體問題。

1. 關閉靜音與勿擾模式

把響鈴/靜音開關撥到看不到橘色；有「動作」按鈕的 iPhone 可以在「控制中心」確認。再到「設定」>「專注模式」>「勿擾模式」確認已關閉。

2. 清理喇叭開口

取下保護貼、膜或保護殼，檢查喇叭開口與聽筒有沒有堵塞，用乾淨、乾燥的小型軟毛刷清理。

3. 用鈴聲滑桿測試

到「設定」>「聲音與觸覺回饋」，來回拖動「鈴聲和提示聲」滑桿。如果完全沒聲音，或揚聲器按鈕變暗，Apple 說揚聲器可能需要維修。

機況 Inside 用四段測試音分別檢查揚聲器、每個麥克風與左右聲道，指出偏弱的頻段，再存成報告給維修人員看。基本檢查免費。 App Store

No sound or distorted sound from your iPhone speaker?

A silent speaker is often just Silent mode or Do Not Disturb. Check in this order to tell a setting from a hardware fault.

1. Turn off Silent mode and Do Not Disturb

Move the Ring/Silent switch so no orange shows; on an iPhone with an Action button, check Control Center. Then make sure Settings > Focus > Do Not Disturb is off.

2. Clean the speaker openings

Remove screen protectors, films or cases, check the speaker opening and receiver for blockage, and clean them with a small, clean, dry soft-bristled brush.

3. Test with the ringtone slider

Go to Settings > Sounds & Haptics and drag the Ringtone and Alerts slider back and forth. If you hear nothing or the speaker button is dimmed, Apple says the speaker may need service.

Inside checks the speakers, each microphone and left and right channels with four test tones, points out a weak frequency band, and saves a report for a repair shop. Basic checks are free. App Store
