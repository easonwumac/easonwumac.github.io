截圖存太多找不到時，「照片」App 的搜尋可以用圖片裡的文字、地點或日期來找。

1. 打開搜尋

在「照片」App 點右下角的「搜尋」按鈕，會先看到建議。

2. 用文字、地點或日期找

可以輸入日期、地點、商家名稱、類別、事件、你命名過的人或寵物、圖片裡的文字或說明；加上媒體類型可以縮小範圍。

3. 排序與過濾

用「排序和過濾」按鈕依日期排序，或只看「喜好項目」、「已編輯」等項目。

收好 Tuck 會讀懂你存下的截圖：訂位、收據、保固、餐廳和食譜，自動分好類；之後用店名、金額、日期或訂位代號搜尋就找得到，也能收好後一鍵從相簿刪除。 App Store

Find old screenshots in iPhone Photos: search by text and date

When screenshots pile up, the Photos search can find them by the text in the image, a place or a date.

1. Open Search

In Photos, tap the Search button in the bottom-right corner to see suggestions.

2. Search by text, place or date

Search by date, place, business, category, event, a named person or pet, text in the image or a caption; add a media type to narrow the results.

3. Sort and filter

Use the Sort and Filter button to sort by date or show only Favorites, Edited and so on.

Tuck reads the screenshots you keep (bookings, receipts, warranties, restaurants, recipes) and sorts them; search later by shop name, amount, date or booking code, and remove them from Photos in one tap once saved. App Store
