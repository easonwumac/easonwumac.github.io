How to check iPhone battery health and maximum capacity, and when to replace the battery

Battery health is right in your iPhone's settings. Here is what each number and message means.

Where to find it

Open Settings > Battery > Battery Health. Maximum Capacity is the battery's capacity relative to when it was new.

How much wear is normal

Apple says that under ideal conditions, batteries in iPhone 14 models and earlier are designed to retain 80 percent of their original capacity at 500 complete charge cycles, and iPhone 15 models at 1,000 cycles.

What the Peak Performance messages mean

  • Normal: the battery supports normal peak performance.
  • Performance management applied: the phone shut down unexpectedly, so iOS limits peak performance to help prevent it happening again.
  • Significantly degraded: an Apple Authorized Service Provider can replace the battery; this is not a safety issue.
  • Unable to verify a genuine Apple battery: the health information shown may not be accurate.

iPhone 15 and later: check the cycle count

On iPhone 15 and later, Settings > General > About shows the battery's manufacture date, first use and cycle count. The closer the count gets to 1,000, the closer the battery is to the wear point Apple designs for.

Help the battery age more slowly

  • Turn on Optimized Battery Charging; on iPhone 15 and later you can also choose to stop at 80% in Settings > Battery > Charging.
  • Avoid heat: Apple says 16° to 22° C (62° to 72° F) is ideal, and ambient temperatures above 35° C (95° F) can permanently damage battery capacity.
  • If the phone gets hot while charging, take it out of its case first.
