How to measure a person’s height with iPhone

On supported iPhones, Measure can detect a person’s height. Fit the whole person in view, wait for the line above their head, and capture the reading.

1. Check feature support

Apple limits automatic height measurement to supported models. Having the Measure app alone does not establish support for this feature.

2. Fit the whole person on screen

Open Measure and position iPhone so the person appears from head to toe. Wait for automatic detection.

3. Read the endpoint correctly

The line appears at the top of the head, hair, or hat, with the reading below it. A hat is included in that reading.

4. Capture or repeat the reading

Tap the capture button to record the measurement. To repeat it, turn iPhone away briefly, then point back at the person.

5. Measure seated height too

Apple also supports measuring seated height. Check whether the upper line ends at the head, hair, or hat.
