摘要

本政策適用於 Spireling（登天勇者）iOS App 與本支援網站。登天勇者是一款放置型爬塔 RPG：四位紙偶勇者自動戰鬥、向上攀登。此 App 由個人開發者維護，不需要帳號，沒有開發者營運的伺服器，也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。Google 廣告服務與 Apple Game Center 有其自身的資料處理方式。

資料存放

遊戲進度、夥伴、裝備、天賦與任務只保存在你的裝置上。若你啟用 iCloud 備份，這些資料也可能包含在你自己的 iCloud 備份中。App 不會把遊戲存檔上傳到開發者的伺服器。

Game Center

Game Center 登入完全自選，不登入也能完整遊玩。登入後，你的最高樓層、賽季塔成績、最快登上 100 層的時間與成就會送到 Apple Game Center，顯示在排行榜上，並依你的 Game Center 設定讓好友看見。這些資料由 Apple 依其 隱私權政策處理，開發者不會取得你的 Apple 帳號資訊。

廣告

登天勇者使用 Google AdMob。獎勵影片完全自願，只有在你按下按鈕時才播放；插頁式廣告只可能在第二次登天重生之後出現，且至少間隔 10 分鐘；賽季塔中沒有任何廣告。載入廣告前，App 透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）處理適用的廣告同意流程。App 不會請求 Apple App Tracking Transparency（ATT）追蹤授權，也不會跨 App 追蹤你。

AdMob 可能蒐集裝置識別碼、IP 位址所推得的大略位置、廣告互動資料，以及效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、防範詐欺與服務運作。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

購買

遊戲貨幣無法購買。若 App 提供一次性的「移除廣告」購買，購買與還原由 Apple App Store 處理，登天勇者不會取得你的付款資料。

本網站

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

資料刪除

刪除 App 即會移除裝置上的所有遊戲資料。已送到 Game Center 的成績與成就請透過 Apple 的 Game Center 設定管理；已存在的 iCloud 備份須透過 Apple 的備份設定另外管理；刪除本機資料不會刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。你可以寄信要求刪除支援來信。

兒童

登天勇者適合一般大眾，並非專為兒童設計，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

聯絡我們

若有隱私或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱與訊息內容，僅用於回覆與排查問題。

Summary

This policy covers the Spireling (登天勇者) iOS app and this support website. Spireling is an idle tower-climbing RPG: four paper-puppet heroes battle automatically and climb ever higher. The app is maintained by an independent developer, needs no account, has no developer-operated server, and does not collect usage analytics itself. Google's advertising service and Apple Game Center have their own data practices.

Data stored

Game progress, companions, equipment, talents and quests stay on your device. If you enable iCloud backup, this data may also be included in your own iCloud backup. The app does not upload your save to any developer server.

Game Center

Game Center sign-in is optional; the game is fully playable without it. When signed in, your highest floor, Season Tower result, fastest time to floor 100 and achievements are sent to Apple Game Center, shown on leaderboards and visible to friends according to your Game Center settings. Apple processes this data under its Privacy Policy; the developer never receives your Apple Account details.

Ads

Spireling uses Google AdMob. Rewarded videos are optional and play only when you tap a button. Interstitial ads can appear only after your second rebirth, at most once every ten minutes. The Season Tower has no ads. Before loading ads, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising consent. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not track you across apps.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, and performance and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure.

Purchases

In-game currency cannot be bought. If the app offers a one-time Remove ads purchase, Apple's App Store handles purchases and restoration; Spireling never receives your payment details.

This website

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data from your device. Scores and achievements already sent to Game Center are managed in Apple's Game Center settings; existing iCloud backups are managed in Apple's backup settings; local deletion does not erase data already held by advertising providers. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Children

Spireling is for a general audience, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Contact

For privacy or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply and troubleshoot.
