四位紙偶勇者自動戰鬥、一路登天的放置型爬塔 RPG。每週還有一座所有人條件相同的賽季塔，在 Game Center 排行榜上一較高下。

常見問題

Boss 打不過怎麼辦？

隊伍會自動回到前一層累積實力。到「隊伍」頁升級勇者、換裝備或調整技能節奏，再按「挑戰 Boss」。

離開遊戲時還會繼續爬嗎？

會。離線最多累積 8 小時的收益，解鎖天賦後可達 12 小時。回到遊戲時會顯示這段期間的收穫。

登天重生是什麼？

登上第 60 層後可以重生：回到第 1 層並依最高樓層獲得羽毛，用來購買永久天賦。夥伴、裝備與天賦都會保留。

賽季塔怎麼玩？

每週一 00:00（UTC）換一座新塔。所有人的樓層、本週加成都相同，勇者能力也統一，只比隊伍組合與技能節奏。每天 3 次挑戰，沒有廣告，也不能用任何方式增加次數或能力。

一定要登入 Game Center 嗎？

不用。不登入也能完整遊玩；登入後才會把成績送上排行榜並解鎖成就。

進度會不見嗎？

進度保存在裝置上；刪除 App 會一併刪除進度。若開啟 iCloud 備份，從備份還原裝置時進度也會一起還原。

廣告多嗎？

獎勵影片完全自願（離線金幣加倍、每日一張招募券、Boss 立即重試）。插頁式廣告只可能在第二次重生之後出現，至少間隔 10 分鐘。遊戲貨幣無法購買。廣告相關的資料處理請參閱 隱私權政策。

FAQ

I can't beat a boss. What now?

Your party automatically farms the previous floor. Level up heroes, change gear or skill rhythms on the Party tab, then tap Challenge boss.

Does my party climb while I'm away?

Yes. Offline rewards build up for up to eight hours, or twelve with a talent. You'll see a summary when you return.

What is rebirth?

From floor 60 you can rebirth: return to floor 1 and earn feathers based on your highest floor, then spend them on permanent talents. Companions, equipment and talents are kept.

How does the Season Tower work?

A new tower opens every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Everyone gets the same floors and weekly modifier, and heroes are normalized, so only party choice and skill rhythms matter. Three attempts a day, no ads, and nothing can add attempts or power.

Do I need Game Center?

No. The game is fully playable without it; sign in to post scores to leaderboards and earn achievements.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device; deleting the app also deletes it. With iCloud backup enabled, progress is restored along with your device backup.

How many ads are there?

Rewarded videos are optional (double offline gold, one recruit ticket a day, retry a boss now). Interstitial ads can appear only after your second rebirth, at most once every ten minutes. In-game currency cannot be bought. See the Privacy Policy for ad data practices.
