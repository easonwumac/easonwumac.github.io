常見問題

需要什麼裝置？如何開始導航？

需要 iPhone 與 iOS 26 以上。點選「你想去哪裡？」、搜尋地點，再點結果中的導航即可開始；不用先啟動相機。地圖、搜尋及重新規劃需要網路。實景與錄影需要相機權限及前景開啟；影片不含聲音。

GPS 不穩或進入隧道時會怎樣？

GPS 不穩時保留路線並暫停精確轉向、AR 指引、測速判斷及抵達判定。長隧道提供粗略路段範圍，BLE OBD、相機與里程牌不能取代精確定位。

LiDAR、裝置端 AI 與 AR 有哪些限制？

LiDAR 與裝置端 AI 依硬體及系統可用性啟用；AR 不提供車道級指引。

測速資料與離線資料包包含什麼？

測速來源覆蓋不完整，方向、道路或樓層不足時僅提示附近資料。離線資料包只含公開測速資料，不含離線地圖、路況或重新規劃。

CarPlay 可以使用嗎？

CarPlay 尚需 Apple 核准導航 entitlement 並完成車機驗證；不提供 CarPlay 相機錄影。

回報問題

請附上 iPhone 型號、iOS/App 版本、操作步驟與錯誤訊息。實測紀錄可手動啟用並匯出；它含私人位置，請先確認內容。傳送前請移除私人位置、影像及識別資訊。不需要提供帳號密碼或完整私人行程。

FAQ

What device do I need, and how do I start navigation?

Requires an iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Tap “Where do you want to go?”, search for a place, then tap navigation in the result. You do not need to start the camera first. Maps, search, and rerouting require a network connection. Live view and recording require camera permission and the app in the foreground; videos have no audio.

What happens when GPS is unreliable or I enter a tunnel?

When GPS is unreliable, the route is retained while precise turn guidance, AR guidance, speed-camera checks, and arrival detection are paused. Long-tunnel assistance gives a coarse segment range. BLE OBD, the camera, and mileage signs cannot replace precise positioning.

What are the limits of LiDAR, on-device AI, and AR?

LiDAR and on-device AI depend on hardware and system availability. AR does not provide lane-level guidance.

What do speed-camera data and offline packs cover?

Speed-camera sources have incomplete coverage. When direction, road, or level information is insufficient, only nearby data is indicated. Offline packs contain public speed-camera data only, with no offline maps, traffic, or rerouting.

Is CarPlay available?

CarPlay still requires Apple approval of the navigation entitlement and vehicle validation. CarPlay camera recording is not provided.

Report a problem

Remove private locations, images, and identifying information before sharing. You do not need to provide account passwords or complete private trips.
