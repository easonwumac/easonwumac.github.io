摘要

本政策適用於 Tuck（收好）iOS App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護。Tuck 不需要帳號，也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。只有你在項目中選擇「高階辨識」時，該項目才會經由開發者營運的轉送伺服器送到 Anthropic 的 Claude 判讀，詳見下方說明。Google 廣告服務有其自身的資料處理方式。

資料存放

Tuck 使用 Apple Vision 進行文字辨識，並在支援的裝置上使用 Apple 的裝置端語言模型整理資訊；其他裝置使用本機文字與規則處理。除了你主動選擇的「高階辨識」以外，所有項目的讀取、辨識與理解都在你的裝置上完成，不會將內容上傳至伺服器或雲端模型進行處理。

高階辨識（Claude）

「高階辨識」是選用功能，每次由你在單一項目中點選，第一次使用前會先徵求同意，也可在設定中關閉。你也可以在設定中開啟「新項目自動高階辨識」（預設關閉）：開啟後，之後收進來的項目（鎖定的資料夾與類型除外）會在裝置上辨識完後自動送出，結果直接套用。送出時，該項目的原始圖片（縮小後）或第一頁、辨識出的文字與標題，會經由開發者營運的 Cloudflare 轉送伺服器傳給 Anthropic 的 Claude 模型（錄音與影片只傳送裝置上轉出的逐字稿文字，不傳送聲音或影像檔），判讀結果傳回你的裝置，由你確認後才會套用。轉送伺服器不保存項目內容或判讀結果，只保存計算使用次數所需的資料：一組隨機帳號識別碼（存在你的 iCloud 鑰匙圈，並作為購買的 appAccountToken）、Apple App Attest 金鑰、每日使用次數、剩餘次數與購買交易編號。Anthropic 依其商業服務條款處理 API 資料，預設不會用於訓練模型；請參閱 Anthropic 隱私權政策。Cloudflare 可能處理連線紀錄，請參閱 Cloudflare 隱私權政策。

加入 Apple 錢包

在「出示條碼」選擇「加入 Apple 錢包」時，票卡（標題、日期、地點與條碼）在你的裝置上製作。Apple 規定票卡須由開發者簽章，因此 App 只把票卡各檔案的 SHA-1 雜湊值清單經由同一個轉送伺服器送去簽章；票卡內容本身不會離開你的裝置，伺服器也不保存任何資料。

你儲存的截圖、照片、收據、訂位資料、PDF、網址與文字，以及原始檔、辨識文字、整理出的資訊、資料夾、搜尋索引與提醒設定，都保存在你的裝置上。若你啟用 iCloud 備份，這些資料也可能包含在你自己的 iCloud 備份中，依你的 App 與 Apple 系統設定處理。

iCloud 同步預設關閉。只有你在「設定 → iCloud 同步」開啟後，項目、資料夾與原始檔才會透過 Apple CloudKit 存放在你自己 iCloud 帳號的私人資料庫，並同步到登入同一帳號的裝置。開發者無法存取這些資料，也不會建立 Tuck 帳號。關閉後不再同步，已同步的資料可在 iOS 的 iCloud 設定中管理。

「加入行事曆」使用系統的行程編輯畫面，由你確認後才會新增行程；Tuck 不會讀取你的行事曆。「加入 Apple 錢包」同樣由你確認。

只有你主動分享、選取、貼上或掃描內容時，App 才會匯入項目或使用相關的照片、檔案與相機功能。

錄音：只有在你開啟錄音畫面時才會使用麥克風，畫面上會顯示計時。語音透過 Apple 的語音辨識在裝置上轉成文字，錄音檔留在你的裝置上。若你選擇把錄音的待辦事項加入「提醒事項」，App 只會在你確認後新增，不會讀取你既有的提醒。

整理相簿：開啟「整理相簿」時會請求照片圖庫權限，照片的分類在裝置上完成，只有你勾選的照片才會存進 Tuck。照片若帶有拍攝位置，App 會先詢問你，同意後才把位置送到 Apple 地圖取得地名；可隨時在「設定」關閉。翻譯使用 Apple 的系統翻譯功能。

共享資料夾：只有你選擇分享某個資料夾時，該資料夾的項目與原始檔才會透過 Apple CloudKit 共享給你邀請的人，他們可以檢視與編輯；共享資料存在 iCloud，開發者無法存取。你可以隨時停止分享或退出。

通知只用於你自行設定並確認的提醒，由裝置排程本機通知。App 不會自行開啟建議的提醒。只有你在項目中選擇「提醒我」、設定時間並確認後，才會排程通知。你可取消提醒，或在 iOS 設定中關閉 Tuck 的通知權限。

廣告與追蹤

Tuck 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告：資料庫下方的橫幅廣告，以及離開資料夾時偶爾出現的全螢幕插頁廣告；購買移除廣告或訂閱 Tuck Pro／Tuck Max 後兩者都不再出現。在載入廣告前，App 透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）處理適用的廣告同意流程，並透過 Apple App Tracking Transparency（ATT）請求追蹤授權。廣告及追蹤依你的選擇與適用要求處理；拒絕追蹤不影響儲存、整理與搜尋功能。

AdMob 可能蒐集裝置識別碼、IP 位址、使用與廣告互動資料，以及效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、分析與服務運作。未允許追蹤不代表廣告服務完全不處理資料。

購買

你可以在「設定」一次購買 Remove ads（移除廣告）或 10 次高階辨識的次數包，或訂閱 Tuck Pro 或 Tuck Max（月繳或年繳的自動續訂訂閱：移除廣告、可整理整個相簿，並可使用高階辨識；Tuck Pro 每天 10 次、每 7 天最多 50 次，Tuck Max 每天 50 次、每 7 天最多 250 次）。未購買時每天可免費使用 1 次高階辨識。訂閱會自動續訂，可在 Apple 帳號設定中管理或取消；轉送伺服器只記錄訂閱的方案與到期時間，以決定訂閱是否有效。購買與還原由 Apple App Store 處理；Tuck 不會取得你的完整付款資料。重新安裝或換裝置後，可在「設定 → 還原購買」恢復移除廣告與訂閱；次數包的剩餘次數記錄在轉送伺服器的帳號識別碼下，使用同一個 iCloud 帳號的裝置可繼續使用。

第三方服務

當你分享網址時，App 才會連線至該網頁取得標題與文字，並在裝置上處理；該網站可能依其隱私政策處理連線資料。

廣告資料處理請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

資料刪除

你可以打開個別項目並選擇「刪除」，或在「設定 → 刪除所有資料」清除 Tuck 保存在裝置上的項目、原始檔與相關資料。開啟 iCloud 同步時，刪除也會從你的 iCloud 與其他裝置移除。刪除 App 也會移除本機 App 資料。已存在的 iCloud 備份須透過 Apple 的備份設定另外管理；刪除本機資料不會立即刪除既有備份，也不會刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。你可以寄信要求刪除支援來信，或要求刪除轉送伺服器上與你帳號識別碼相關的使用次數紀錄（請在 App 的「設定」中查看識別碼）。

若有隱私、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與你附上的資料，用於回覆與排查問題。請先遮蔽截圖中的個人資料，不要寄送私人文件、密碼或付款資料。

Summary

This policy covers the Tuck (收好) iOS app and this support website. The app is maintained by an independent developer. Tuck requires no account and does not collect usage analytics itself. Google’s advertising service has its own data practices. Only when you choose Advanced reading on an item is that item sent through a developer-operated relay to Anthropic’s Claude, as described below.

Data stored

Tuck uses Apple Vision for text recognition and, on supported devices, Apple’s on-device language model to organize information. Other devices use local text processing and rules. Apart from Advanced reading, which you choose per item, all reading, recognition, and understanding of items happens on your device. Content is not uploaded to a server or cloud model for processing.

Advanced reading (Claude)

Advanced reading is optional: you tap it on one item at a time, the app asks for consent before the first use, and you can turn it off in Settings. You can also turn on “Read new items automatically” in Settings (off by default): items added afterwards, except those in locked folders or of locked types, are sent once read on your device, and the result is applied directly. When an item is sent, that item’s original image (downscaled) or first page, its recognized text and its title are sent through a developer-operated Cloudflare relay to Anthropic’s Claude model (for a recording or video, only the transcript text made on your device, never the audio or video file); the reading comes back to your device and is applied only after you confirm. The relay does not store item content or readings. It keeps only what counting usage needs: a random account identifier (kept in your iCloud Keychain and used as the purchase appAccountToken), the Apple App Attest key, daily usage counts, remaining readings and purchase transaction IDs. Anthropic handles API data under its commercial terms and does not use it to train models by default; see Anthropic’s Privacy Policy. Cloudflare may process connection records; see Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Add to Apple Wallet

When you choose Add to Apple Wallet under Show code, the pass (title, date, place and code) is made on your device. Apple requires passes to be signed by the developer, so the app sends only the list of SHA-1 hashes of the pass files through the same relay to be signed; the pass content itself never leaves your device, and the relay stores nothing.

Saved screenshots, photos, receipts, bookings, PDFs, URLs, and text, together with originals, recognized text, extracted information, folders, search indexes, and reminder settings, stay on your device. If you enable iCloud backup, this data may also be included in your own iCloud backup according to your app and Apple system settings.

iCloud sync is off by default. Only if you turn on Settings → iCloud sync are items, folders, and originals stored through Apple CloudKit in the private database of your own iCloud account and synced to devices signed in to the same account. The developer cannot access this data, and no Tuck account is created. Turning it off stops syncing; synced data can be managed in iOS iCloud settings.

Add to Calendar uses the system event editor and adds an event only after you confirm; Tuck does not read your calendar. Add to Apple Wallet also needs your confirmation.

The app imports items or uses photo, file, and camera features only when you choose to share, select, paste, or scan content.

Recordings: the microphone is used only while the recorder screen is open, which shows a timer. Speech becomes text on your device through Apple’s speech recognition, and the audio stays on your device. If you choose to add a recording’s to-dos to Reminders, the app adds them only after you confirm and does not read your existing reminders.

Sort photos: opening Sort photos asks for photo library access. Photos are sorted on your device, and only the photos you tick are saved to Tuck. For photos with a location, the app asks first and, only if you allow it, sends the location to Apple Maps to get a place name; you can turn this off in Settings. Translation uses Apple’s system translation.

Shared folders: only when you share a folder are its items and originals shared through Apple CloudKit with the people you invite, who can view and edit them; shared data is stored in iCloud and the developer cannot access it. You can stop sharing or leave at any time.

Notifications are only for reminders you set and confirm, scheduled locally on your device. The app does not activate suggested reminders on its own. It schedules a notification only after you choose Remind me on an item, set the time, and confirm. You can cancel reminders or turn off Tuck’s notification permission in iOS Settings.

Ads and tracking

Tuck uses Google AdMob to display ads: a banner below your library and an occasional full-screen interstitial when you leave a folder; Remove ads, Tuck Pro or Tuck Max turns both off. Before loading ads, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising consent and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) to request tracking permission. Ads and tracking follow your choices and applicable requirements. Declining tracking does not affect saving, organizing, or searching.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, usage and ad interaction data, and performance and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and service operation. Declining tracking does not mean the advertising service processes no data.

Purchases

In Settings you can buy Remove ads or a pack of 10 Advanced readings once, or subscribe to Tuck Pro or Tuck Max (auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscriptions: no ads, sorting your whole photo library, and Advanced readings: Tuck Pro 10 a day and up to 50 in 7 days, Tuck Max 50 a day and up to 250 in 7 days). Without a purchase, one Advanced reading a day is free. The subscription renews automatically; manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings. The relay keeps only the subscription's plan and expiry to decide whether it is active. Apple App Store handles purchases and restoration; Tuck does not receive your full payment details. After reinstalling or switching devices, use Settings → Restore purchases to restore Remove ads and your subscription; remaining pack readings are kept under your account identifier on the relay and stay available on devices with the same iCloud account.

Third-party services

When you share a URL, the app connects to that page to retrieve its title and text, then processes them on your device. The visited website may handle connection data under its own privacy policy.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure for advertising data practices.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Open an individual item and choose Delete, or use Settings → Delete all data to clear Tuck’s locally stored items, originals, and associated data. With iCloud sync on, deletions are also removed from your iCloud and other devices. Deleting the app also removes local app data. Existing iCloud backups must be managed separately through Apple’s backup settings. Local deletion does not immediately erase existing backups or data already held by advertising providers. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence. You can also ask us to delete the usage records kept on the relay for your account identifier (shown in the app’s Settings).

For privacy, data, or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments to reply and troubleshoot. Hide personal details in screenshots and avoid sending private documents, passwords, or payment information.
