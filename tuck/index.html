收好 Tuck－AI 截圖整理

Tuck: AI Screenshot Organizer

截圖越存越多、要用時找不到？收好的 AI 會讀懂你存下的每張截圖與檔案：訂位、收據、保固，還有截下來的餐廳、食譜和想買的東西。自動分好類，之後搜幾個字就找回。辨識在手機上完成，支援 Apple Intelligence；也可以選用 Claude 高階辨識。

從分享選單送進來，或用相片、掃描、檔案與貼上加入。存下就好，不用先填表格。Tuck 會在裝置上讀取內容，認出訂位、票券、收據、訂單、保固與文件，整理日期、金額與地址，原始檔也會保留。

下次只記得店名或文件裡的幾個字，就用搜尋找回。打開資料，可以查看原圖、複製重點，或為重要日期確認一個提醒。

• 開啟收好時自動收好新截圖（可選），收好後一鍵從相簿刪除
• 餐廳、食譜和想買的東西自動放進各自的資料夾；地點可直接在地圖開啟
• 從控制中心或動作按鈕存下最新截圖
• 每週日提醒你整理這週的截圖
• 分享圖片、PDF、連結與文字；相片可一次加入多張
• 搜尋標題、圖片中的文字與資料重點，也能從 iPhone 系統搜尋找回
• 也能用地點、資料夾、金額、日期或訂位代號搜尋，最符合的排最前面
• 用「即將到來」、「收據」與「保固」快速查看相關資料
• 電子發票的 QR Code 自動帶入發票號碼、日期與總額
• 收據依月份分組，並顯示當月合計
• 保固顯示到期日，到期前一個月提醒你
• 行程資料夾依日期列出班機、住宿與訂位代號，證件快到期時提醒你
• 在搜尋直接問，例如「香港那趟花了多少？」，看到答案與出處
• 依類別統計每月花費；整月收據可匯出 CSV 與 PDF 報帳
• 自動找出到期、續約與退貨期限，並建議提醒時間
• 存下 Apple 錢包票券並加入錢包；訂位與票券可加入行事曆
• 捷徑：問收好、下一趟行程、快到期、匯出收據、存到收好、找項目、打開項目、移到資料夾、建立資料夾、設定提醒、花費
• 可選擇用 iCloud 在裝置間同步
• 依類型篩選；可修改名稱、類型、日期、金額與保固期間
• 同一個檔案存兩次，只會保留一份
• 用資料夾整理，最多兩層；Tuck 會建議分類，由你決定。可重新命名、合併或刪除，也能一次選取多筆移動或刪除
• 放大原圖、查看 PDF，輕點複製日期、金額與地址
• 用「提醒我」確認時間，讓通知在需要時提醒你
• 在 iPad 與展開的 iPhone Duo 上，清單與打開的資料左右並排

不用登入。

Tuck 會在資料庫的項目之間顯示橫幅廣告，並在離開資料夾等告一段落時偶爾顯示全螢幕廣告；一次購買即可移除廣告。

高階辨識：每天免費 1 次。收好 Pro 是可選的自動續訂訂閱（月繳或年繳），每天 10 次高階辨識、每週最多 50 次，並且沒有廣告。收好 Max 同樣月繳或年繳，每天 50 次、每週最多 250 次。費用由你的 Apple 帳號扣款；除非在本期結束至少 24 小時前取消，否則會自動續訂，可在 Apple 帳號設定中管理或取消。

Screenshots piling up? Tuck's AI reads every screenshot and file you save: bookings, receipts and warranties, and the restaurants, recipes and things to buy you screenshot. It files each one and finds it again from a few words. Reading happens on your device, with Apple Intelligence where available; Advanced reading by Claude is optional.

Share something to Tuck and it's saved right away. Add photos, scan a document, import a file or paste a link or text. Tuck reads it on your device, recognizes bookings, tickets, receipts, orders, warranties and documents, and picks out useful details such as dates, amounts and addresses. Your originals are kept too.

Search for a shop name or a few words you remember. Open the original, copy a detail or confirm a reminder for an important date.

• New screenshots are tucked automatically when you open Tuck (optional), then cleared from Photos in one tap
• Places, recipes and things to buy go into their own folders; open a place in Maps
• Save your latest screenshot from Control Center or the Action Button
• A Sunday reminder to tidy the week's screenshots
• Save images, PDFs, links and text; add several photos at once
• Search titles, text in images and extracted details, including through iPhone system search
• Find items by place, folder, amount, date or booking code; the closest matches come first
• Browse Upcoming, Receipts and Warranties
• Receipts are grouped by month with a monthly total
• Warranties show when they end and remind you a month before
• Trip folders list flights, stays and confirmation codes by day, and flag an ID that expires soon
• Ask in search, such as "How much did the Hong Kong trip cost?", and see the answer with the items it comes from
• Monthly spending by category; export a month's receipts to CSV and PDF for expense claims
• Expiry, renewal and return-by dates are found, with a suggested reminder to confirm
• Save Apple Wallet passes; add them to Wallet, or add bookings and tickets to Calendar
• Shortcuts: Ask Tuck, Next trip, Expiring soon, Export receipts, Save to Tuck, Find items, Open item, Move to folder, Create folder, Remind me, Spending
• Optional iCloud sync across your devices
• Taiwan e-invoice QR codes fill in the invoice number, date and total
• Filter by type; edit an item's name, type, date, amount or warranty length
• Saving the same file twice keeps just one copy
• Sort items into folders, up to two levels; Tuck suggests folders and you decide. Rename, merge or delete them, and move or delete several items at once
• Zoom into originals, view PDFs and copy dates, amounts or addresses
• Set a local reminder with a date and message ready to confirm
• On iPad and an unfolded iPhone Duo, your library and the open item sit side by side

No account or login needed.

Tuck shows a banner among your library items and an occasional full-screen ad at a natural break, such as leaving a folder. A one-time purchase removes them.

Advanced reading: one a day is free. Tuck Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) with 10 advanced readings a day, up to 50 a week, and no ads. Tuck Max, also monthly or yearly, gives 50 a day, up to 250 a week. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; it renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends, and you can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
