Tuck Pro 訂閱

  • Tuck Pro 是可選的自動續訂訂閱，有月繳與年繳兩種，價格以 App 與 App Store 顯示為準。
  • 訂閱期間：移除廣告，並可使用高階辨識每天 10 次、每 7 天最多 50 次。未訂閱時每天可免費使用 1 次。
  • 確認購買時由你的 Apple 帳號扣款。除非在本期結束至少 24 小時前關閉自動續訂，否則會自動續訂並在本期結束前 24 小時內扣款。
  • 可隨時在「設定 → Apple 帳號 → 訂閱項目」管理或取消；取消後可使用到本期結束。退款由 Apple 依其政策處理。

Tuck Pro subscription

  • Tuck Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, at the price shown in the app and on the App Store.
  • While subscribed: no ads, and 10 Advanced readings a day, up to 50 in 7 days. Without it, one Advanced reading a day is free.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically, and is charged within 24 hours before the period ends, unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before then.
  • Manage or cancel any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions; Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.
