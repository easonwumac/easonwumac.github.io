摘要

劇照機把你拍的照片做成電影劇照，加上字幕、片名與海報，並剪成預告片。照片、影片、字幕與片名都存在你的裝置上；只有在你使用下述功能時，才會有資料離開裝置。

相機、麥克風與語音辨識

相機用於拍照與錄下短片。按住快門錄短片時會使用麥克風；把說的話變成字幕的語音辨識在裝置上進行，聲音不會傳到我們的伺服器。存入照片圖庫只在你按下儲存時進行，App 不讀取你的圖庫。

資料存放與 iCloud

你的劇照、短片、字幕與片名存在裝置上。登入 iCloud 時，會同步到你的 Apple iCloud 私有資料庫，供你的其他裝置使用；我們看不到這些內容。

你在「⋯」選單選「邀朋友一起拍這部片」邀請他人時，那一部片會透過 Apple iCloud 共享給你邀請的人，他們可以加入劇照。你可以隨時停止共享或移除參與者。我們看不到共享內容。

你分享或儲存的海報、劇照與預告片，會交給你選擇的接收方或 App。

Apple Watch、小工具與通知

Apple Watch 遙控器透過 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 直接與你的 iPhone 傳送取景畫面，不經過我們的伺服器。小工具與「本週預告片」通知在裝置上產生；通知以安靜方式送達，可在 iOS 設定關閉。

雲端 AI（劇照機 Pro）

雲端字幕與片名，以及語音字幕的修飾，屬於劇照機 Pro 功能。第一次使用前 App 會先說明並徵求同意，同意前不會傳送任何內容；之後可在「劇照機 Pro」畫面點「關閉雲端 AI」撤回。每次使用時，會傳送：該張劇照縮小後的畫面（長邊 768 像素，不含位置等中繼資料）、所選風格與 App 語言，或裝置辨識出的字幕文字，到我們的伺服器（Cloudflare Workers），再交由 Anthropic 的 Claude 處理後傳回。我們的伺服器不保存畫面或文字。為了確認訂閱，App 會附上 App Store 簽發的訂閱交易資料（不含姓名、電子郵件或付款資料）；伺服器只以單向雜湊後的訂閱識別記錄每月使用次數。Anthropic 依其商業條款與隱私權政策處理這些內容，不以其訓練模型。這些內容不會用於廣告。主體去背、最佳鏡頭、構圖與語音辨識等其他 AI 功能都在裝置上進行。

廣告與追蹤

劇照機使用 Google AdMob 在片庫與影片畫面顯示橫幅廣告，並在完成輸出（剪好預告片、儲存或分享海報與劇照）後偶爾顯示插頁廣告；相機畫面不顯示廣告。廣告請求一律為非個人化廣告。App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。App 內含 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）SDK，用於確認所在地區的隱私要求；由於只請求非個人化廣告，不會跳出廣告同意表單。你的照片、影片與字幕不會傳給廣告服務。

AdMob 可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。訂閱劇照機 Pro 後不再顯示廣告。

購買

相機、字幕、基本海報與預告片可免費使用（含廣告）。劇照機 Pro 是自動續訂的訂閱（月繳或年繳）：移除廣告、解鎖全部海報與預告片風格、底片版面、預告片不印片尾署名、3D 景深預告片與 AI 功能（雲端 AI 每月 200 次）。訂閱會自動續訂，除非在當期結束前至少 24 小時取消；可在 App Store 帳號設定中管理或取消，價格以 App Store 購買畫面為準。購買由 Apple App Store 處理，App 使用 StoreKit 確認權益，不會取得你的付款資料。在「設定 › 劇照機 Pro › 還原購買」可恢復購買。

意見回饋與其他 App

「設定 › 意見回饋」可以寫下問題並附上最多 3 張圖片（重新編碼並移除位置等中繼資料）。只有按下「送出」時，才會把你的文字、圖片、選填的 Email、App 版本、裝置型號與 iOS 版本傳到開發者的伺服器 agentmesh.uk（由 Cloudflare 託管），轉成開發者私人 GitHub 儲存庫中的一則待辦；圖片 90 天後刪除。這些資料只用於回覆與改善 App，請勿附上敏感資料。「更多 App」清單由 agentmesh.uk 提供，沒有廣告可顯示時橫幅位置也可能顯示其中一個；不傳送個人資料或識別碼，App 名稱與圖示由 Apple 的 App Store 查詢服務依你的地區取得。

資料刪除

在 App 的片庫刪除劇照，或在作品的「⋯」選單選「刪除作品」；同步的內容會一併從你的 iCloud 刪除。也可在 Apple 裝置的 iCloud 儲存空間管理中移除本 App 資料。意見回饋可來信要求提早刪除。

Summary

Stillreel turns your photos into film stills with subtitles, titles and posters, and cuts them into trailers. Photos, clips, lines and titles stay on your device; data leaves it only when you use the features described below.

Camera, microphone and speech

The camera takes photos and short clips. Holding the shutter records a clip with the microphone; turning what was said into a subtitle happens on the device, and the audio is never sent to our servers. Saving to your photo library happens only when you tap Save; the app does not read your library.

Storage and iCloud

Your stills, clips, lines and titles are stored on your device. When you are signed in to iCloud they sync through your Apple iCloud private database to your other devices; we cannot see them.

When you choose “Shoot this film together” in the ⋯ menu, that film is shared through Apple iCloud with the people you invite, who can add stills to it. You can stop sharing or remove people at any time. We cannot see shared content.

Posters, stills and trailers you share or save go to the recipient or app you choose.

Apple Watch, widgets and notifications

The Apple Watch remote exchanges the viewfinder directly with your iPhone through Apple’s WatchConnectivity, not through our servers. Widgets and the “This week’s trailer” notice are made on the device; the notice is delivered quietly and can be turned off in iOS Settings.

Cloud AI (Stillreel Pro)

Cloud lines and titles, and the clean-up of spoken subtitles, are Stillreel Pro features. Before the first use the app explains this and asks for your consent, and nothing is sent until you agree; you can take it back with Turn off cloud AI on the Stillreel Pro screen. Each request sends the still downsized to 768 pixels on its long side (no location or other metadata), the chosen look and the app language, or the subtitle text recognized on your device, to our server (Cloudflare Workers), which forwards it to Anthropic’s Claude and returns the answer. Our server does not keep the picture or the text. To confirm the subscription the app attaches the App Store’s signed transaction (no name, email or payment details); the server keeps only a monthly request count under a one-way hash of the subscription. Anthropic handles the content under its commercial terms and privacy policy and does not train on it. It is never used for advertising. Subject lift, best take, framing and speech recognition run on the device.

Ads and tracking

Stillreel uses Google AdMob to show banner ads on the library and film screens, and now and then an interstitial ad after an output (a trailer cut, a poster or still saved or shared); the camera never shows ads. All ad requests ask for non-personalized ads. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. The app includes Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to check the privacy requirements of your region; since it requests only non-personalized ads, it shows no ad consent form. Your photos, clips and lines are never sent to the ad service.

AdMob may process IP address, device and ad-interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure. Stillreel Pro removes ads.

Purchases

The camera, lines, basic posters and trailers are free, with ads. Stillreel Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) that removes ads and unlocks every poster and trailer style, the film strip layout, trailers without the credit, 3D depth trailers and the AI features (200 cloud AI requests a month). It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. The App Store purchase screen shows local prices. Apple processes purchases; the app verifies them with StoreKit and never receives your payment details. Restore with Settings › Stillreel Pro › Restore purchases.

Feedback and our other apps

Settings › Send feedback lets you describe a problem and attach up to 3 pictures (re-encoded, with location and other metadata removed). Nothing is sent until you tap Send; then your text, pictures, the email you may add, the app version, device model and iOS version go to the developer’s server agentmesh.uk (hosted on Cloudflare), which files them as an item in the developer’s private GitHub repository; pictures are deleted after 90 days. This is used only to reply and improve the app; please leave out sensitive information. More apps lists our other apps from agentmesh.uk, and when no ad is available the banner may show one of them; no personal data or identifiers are sent, and names and icons come from Apple’s App Store lookup service for your region.

Deleting your data

Delete stills in the app’s library, or a whole film with Delete film in its ⋯ menu; synced copies are removed from your iCloud too. You can also remove this app’s data in your Apple device’s iCloud storage settings. Email us to have feedback deleted sooner.
