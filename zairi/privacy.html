適用範圍與聯絡方式

本政策適用於「栽日」iOS App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護。若有資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。

儲存在裝置上的資料

植物資料、照片、附件、日誌、任務、位置、庫存、行事曆、通知設定及廣告顯示頻率計數儲存在你的裝置。App 沒有帳號或開發者營運的後端，也不自行上傳這些植物資料。裝置備份可能依你的 Apple 系統設定保留 App 資料。

你可以自行匯出包含植物資料與媒體檔的備份，或選擇檔案還原。備份檔儲存或分享的位置由你決定；分享給他人或雲端服務後，適用該服務的隱私規則。

相機、照片與通知

只有你選擇拍照或挑選照片時，App 才會使用相機或照片選取器。照片用於你建立的植物紀錄。若你開啟任務通知，App 會在裝置上排程本機提醒；可隨時在 App 或 iOS 設定中關閉。

Apple Intelligence

在支援的裝置上，你可以主動要求 Apple Foundation Models 根據照片與既有紀錄產生日誌草稿、成長摘要或下一步觀察建議。App 不會自動儲存 AI 草稿；你可先檢查與修改。功能是否可用由裝置及系統決定。

廣告、同意資訊與購買

免費版整合 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）及 User Messaging Platform（UMP），用於插頁廣告及適用的隱私選擇。Google 和參與廣告供應的服務可能處理 IP 位址推估的大致位置、裝置或 App 範圍識別碼、廣告與互動資料、效能及當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、分析與服務運作。App 不要求精確位置權限。App 可能顯示 Apple 的跨 App 追蹤授權提示；你可以拒絕，植物記錄功能仍可使用。

App 設定使用非個人化廣告，但非個人化不代表完全不蒐集資料。可請求的廣告依地區、SDK 狀態和有效隱私選擇而定。沒有廣告時，植物記錄功能仍可使用。請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及 Google 如何使用合作夥伴 App 的資訊。

一次性去廣告內購由 Apple StoreKit 處理。App 以 Apple 提供的交易狀態判斷是否顯示廣告；付款資料由 Apple 處理。

你的選擇、資料刪除與支援來信

  • 當 SDK 要求提供隱私選項時，可在 App「設定 → 廣告隱私」重新開啟表單。
  • 可在 App 設定或 iOS 設定中關閉任務通知。
  • 刪除 App 可移除本機 App 資料；Apple 裝置備份及你自行匯出的備份須另外管理。刪除 App 不會撤回廣告同意或刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。
  • 如主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與你附上的截圖或診斷資料，用於回覆與排查問題。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼或付款資料。可寄信要求刪除支援來信；對第三方持有的資料，請使用其政策所列控制方式。

網站與更新

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線及技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。政策更新將在本頁顯示日期。

Scope and contact

This policy covers the ZaiRi iOS app and this support website. For privacy, support, or ad concerns, email easonwumac@gmail.com.

Data on your device

Plant records, photos, attachments, journals, tasks, locations, inventory, calendar entries, notification settings, and ad frequency counters are stored on your device. The app has no account or developer-operated backend and does not itself upload plant records. Apple device backups may retain app data under your system settings. You may export or restore a complete backup; you choose where to store or share that file.

Camera, photos, notifications, and Apple Intelligence

The app accesses the camera or photo picker only when you choose to capture or select a photo. Local task reminders require your permission and can be disabled. On supported devices, you may ask Apple Foundation Models to draft a journal entry or summarize your records. Generated text is not saved automatically; review it before saving.

Advertising, consent, and purchases

The free app uses Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform for interstitial ads and applicable privacy choices. Google and participating ad services may process approximate location inferred from IP address, device or app-scoped identifiers, ad interactions, performance information, and crash diagnostics for delivery, measurement, analysis, and service operation. The app does not request precise location. It may show Apple's cross-app tracking authorization prompt; you may decline and still use plant records. The app requests non-personalized ads, but this does not mean no data is collected. Core plant features continue when ads are unavailable. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Apple StoreKit handles the one-time remove-ads purchase. The app checks Apple's transaction status to decide whether to show ads; Apple handles payment information.

Your choices and deletion

When required, reopen ad privacy choices from Settings. Turn off reminders in the app or iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes local app data; Apple backups and backups you exported must be managed separately. Deletion does not withdraw ad consent or erase data already held by ad providers. If you email support, we receive your address, message, and any attachments you send to resolve the issue. Do not send passwords, codes, or payment details. You may request deletion of support messages by email.

Website and updates

This site uses GitHub Pages without developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date.
