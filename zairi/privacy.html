摘要

本政策適用於「栽日」iOS 植物日誌 App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護，沒有帳號或開發者營運的後端，也不自行上傳植物資料。

資料存放

植物資料、照片、附件、日誌、任務、位置、庫存、行事曆、通知設定儲存在你的裝置。裝置備份可能依你的 Apple 系統設定保留 App 資料。

你可以自行匯出包含植物資料與媒體檔的完整備份，並在系統檔案選擇器中存到 iCloud Drive，之後再選擇檔案還原。備份需手動執行，不會自動同步；備份檔儲存或分享的位置由你決定；分享給他人或雲端服務後，適用該服務的隱私規則。

只有你選擇拍照或挑選照片時，App 才會使用相機或照片選取器。照片用於你建立的植物紀錄。若你允許並開啟任務通知，App 會在裝置上排程本機提醒；可隨時在 App 或 iOS 設定中關閉。

在支援的裝置上，你可以主動要求 Apple Foundation Models 根據照片與既有紀錄產生日誌草稿、成長摘要或下一步觀察建議。App 不會自動儲存 AI 草稿；你可先檢查與修改。功能是否可用由裝置及系統決定。

廣告與追蹤

栽日整合 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）及 User Messaging Platform（UMP），用於插頁廣告及適用的隱私選擇。Google 和參與廣告供應的服務可能處理 IP 位址推估的大致位置、裝置或 App 範圍識別碼、廣告與互動資料、效能及當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、分析與服務運作。App 不要求精確位置權限。App 可能顯示 Apple 的跨 App 追蹤授權提示；你可以拒絕，植物記錄功能仍可使用。

App 設定使用非個人化廣告，但非個人化不代表完全不蒐集資料。可請求的廣告依地區、SDK 狀態和有效隱私選擇而定。沒有廣告時，植物記錄功能仍可使用。當 SDK 要求提供隱私選項時，可在 App「設定 → 廣告隱私」重新開啟表單。

第三方服務

廣告資料處理請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及 Google 如何使用合作夥伴 App 的資訊。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線及技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

資料刪除

刪除 App 可移除本機 App 資料；Apple 裝置備份及你自行匯出的備份須另外管理。刪除 App 不會撤回廣告同意或刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。可寄信要求刪除支援來信；對第三方持有的資料，請使用其政策所列控制方式。

聯絡我們

如有隱私、支援、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。如主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與你附上的截圖或診斷資料，用於回覆與排查問題。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼或付款資料。

Summary

This policy covers the Zairi iOS plant journal app and this support website. The app is maintained by an individual developer. It has no account or developer-operated backend and does not itself upload plant records.

Data stored

Plant records, photos, attachments, journals, tasks, locations, inventory, calendar entries, and notification settings are stored on your device. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your system settings.

You can export a complete backup containing plant records and media files, save it to iCloud Drive through the system file picker, and select that file later to restore it. Backups are manual and do not sync automatically. You choose where to store or share the backup; once shared with others or cloud services, their privacy rules apply.

The app uses the camera or photo picker only when you choose to capture or select a photo. Photos are used in plant records you create. Local task reminders require your permission and are scheduled on your device when enabled. You can turn them off in the app or iOS Settings at any time.

On supported devices, you can ask Apple Foundation Models to use photos and existing records to draft journal entries, summarize growth, or suggest what to observe next. AI drafts are not saved automatically; you can review and edit them first. Availability depends on the device and system.

Ads and tracking

Zairi integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for interstitial ads and applicable privacy choices. Google and participating ad services may process approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device or app-scoped identifiers, advertising and interaction data, performance information, and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, analysis, and service operation. The app does not request precise-location permission. It may show Apple’s cross-app tracking authorization prompt; you can decline and still use plant records.

The app requests non-personalized ads, but this does not mean no data is collected. Available ad requests depend on region, SDK status, and valid privacy choices. Plant-record features remain available when ads are unavailable. When required by the SDK, reopen the form in Settings → Ad privacy.

Third-party services

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps for advertising data practices.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes local app data. Apple device backups and backups you exported must be managed separately. Deleting the app does not withdraw ad consent or erase data already held by ad providers. You can email us to request deletion of support correspondence; for information held by third parties, use the controls listed in their policies.

Contact

For privacy, support, data, or ad concerns, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots or diagnostics you attach to reply and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment details.
