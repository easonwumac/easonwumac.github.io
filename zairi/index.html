把植物的每一天，好好留住。

栽日是原生 iPhone 植物日誌。隨手拍照記錄生長、寫下觀察、安排澆水與換盆任務，並整理用品、位置和行事曆。資料與照片儲存在你的 iPhone，不需要帳號或伺服器；完整備份可自行匯出和還原。

在支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置上，栽日可根據你的照片與紀錄草擬日誌、整理成長變化，並提出下一步可觀察的方向。所有 AI 內容都由你檢查後決定是否儲存。

• 快速記下澆水、施肥與換盆

Give every plant day a place to grow.

ZaiRi is a native iPhone journal for your plants. Capture growth photos, write observations, plan watering and repotting, and organize supplies, locations, and calendar events. Your records and photos stay on your iPhone. No account or server is required. You can export and restore a full backup.

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence can draft journal entries from your photos and records, summarize changes, and suggest what to observe next. You review every AI draft before saving it.

FAQ

Is there a plant app that reminds me to water?

ZaiRi schedules watering, feeding and repotting for each plant with notifications, and a Home Screen widget lists today's care.

How do I keep a record of my plants' growth?

Take growth photos and notes in ZaiRi, then turn them into a time-lapse or before-and-after view; data stays on your iPhone with no account.

Does ZaiRi cost anything?

Every plant-journal feature is free with occasional full-screen ads; a one-time purchase removes ads.

Is there a plant journal app that identifies plants from photos?

ZaiRi can identify a plant from a photo and keeps growth photos, care logs and custom fields, plus growth time-lapses and before-and-after images. All plant journal features are free.

How can I tell if a spot has enough light for a plant?

ZaiRi has a camera light meter you can use where the plant sits, and lets you browse and manage plants by location.

Does ZaiRi need an account, and where is my data stored?

No account or server is needed. Data and photos stay on your iPhone, and you can export and restore a full backup yourself.
