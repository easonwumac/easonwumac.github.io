把植物的每一天，好好留住。

栽日是原生 iPhone 植物日誌。隨手拍照記錄生長、寫下觀察、安排澆水與換盆任務，並整理用品、位置和行事曆。資料與照片儲存在你的 iPhone，不需要帳號或伺服器；完整備份可自行匯出和還原。

在支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置上，栽日可根據你的照片與紀錄草擬日誌、整理成長變化，並提出下一步可觀察的方向。所有 AI 內容都由你檢查後決定是否儲存。

功能包括：
• 依位置瀏覽植物、搜尋與管理歷史植物
• 拍照記錄、成長照片、照護日誌與自訂欄位
• 快速記下澆水、施肥與換盆
• 照護任務、到期通知、行事曆與用品庫存
• 主畫面「今日照護」小工具、成長縮時影片與前後對照圖、從照片辨識植物，以及相機光度計
• 完整資料與照片備份還原
• 在展開的 iPhone Duo 上，左頁是植物或照護任務，右頁是植物資料

栽日免費使用，偶爾會出現全螢幕廣告；一次購買即可移除廣告，所有植物日誌功能都維持免費。

Give every plant day a place to grow.

ZaiRi is a native iPhone journal for your plants. Capture growth photos, write observations, plan watering and repotting, and organize supplies, locations, and calendar events. Your records and photos stay on your iPhone. No account or server is required. You can export and restore a full backup.

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence can draft journal entries from your photos and records, summarize changes, and suggest what to observe next. You review every AI draft before saving it.

Features:
• Browse plants by location, search, and archive past plants
• Growth photos, care notes, attachments, and custom fields
• Quick watering, fertilizing, and repotting records
• Care tasks, due-date reminders, calendar, and supply inventory
• Today’s care widget on the Home Screen, growth timelapses and before & after images, plant look-up from photos, and a camera light meter
• Full backup and restore of records and photos
• On an unfolded iPhone Duo, your plants or care tasks sit on the left page and the plant’s details on the right

ZaiRi is free with occasional full-screen ads. A one-time purchase removes them; every plant-journal feature stays free.
