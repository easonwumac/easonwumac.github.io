App 問題

請提供 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本及重現步驟。如附截圖，請先遮蔽個人資料。備份檔可能包含照片與完整植物紀錄，請勿在公開留言附上。

不適當廣告

請提供廣告出現的時間與時區、廣告內容及當時操作。請勿為了回報而點擊廣告。

請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼、付款資料或私鑰。

Get help

Email the app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Remove personal information from screenshots. Backup files may contain your photos and full plant records; do not post them publicly.

Report an inappropriate ad

Include the time and time zone, ad content, and what you were doing. Do not click an ad just to report it.

Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or private keys.
