摘要

本政策適用於「拼哪裡」iOS 拼圖輔助 App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護，沒有帳號，也不會把你的照片或拼圖資料傳給開發者。

資料存放

盒蓋照片、拼圖名稱、片數、已找到的位置與設定儲存在你的裝置。裝置備份可能依你的 Apple 系統設定保留 App 資料。

只有在你拍盒蓋、找位置或找邊框片時，App 才會使用相機；也可以改從照片選取器挑一張盒蓋照片。畫面辨識（比對拼圖片位置、找出邊框片）全部使用裝置上的 OpenCV 與 Apple Vision 完成，影像不會離開裝置，找片時的即時畫面也不會被儲存。

只有你選擇把進度圖存到照片時，App 才會要求「加入照片」的權限；分享完成卡片時，由你在系統分享選單中決定分享對象。主畫面小工具透過 App 群組讀取裝置上的拼圖進度，不會傳出裝置。

廣告與追蹤

拼哪裡整合 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）及 User Messaging Platform（UMP），用於插頁廣告及適用的隱私選擇。顯示廣告時，Google 和參與廣告供應的服務可能處理 IP 位址推估的大致位置、裝置或 App 範圍識別碼、廣告與互動資料、效能及當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量與服務運作。App 不使用「App 追蹤透明度」追蹤你，也不要求位置權限。

App 設定使用非個人化廣告，但非個人化不代表完全不蒐集資料。廣告只在找片結束回到首頁時出現，第一次使用期間不顯示。測試版不顯示廣告。當 SDK 要求提供隱私選項時，可在 App「設定」重新開啟表單。

若日後提供「移除廣告」購買，將由 Apple 的 App 內購買處理；我們不會收到你的付款資料。

意見回饋與其他 App

設定中的「意見回饋」只在你按下「送出」時，把你輸入的內容、選填的 Email 與 App 版本傳到開發者的伺服器 agentmesh.uk（由 Cloudflare 託管），轉成開發者私人 GitHub 儲存庫中的一則待辦後，伺服器即刪除副本；每日送出次數以當天更換的 IP 雜湊計算，隔日刪除。內容只用於回覆與改善 App，請勿填寫敏感資料，可來信要求刪除。設定底部的「開發者的其他 App」清單由 agentmesh.uk 提供，不傳送個人資料或識別碼；App 名稱、圖示與評分由 Apple 的 App Store 查詢服務（itunes.apple.com）依你的地區取得，點選不會被追蹤。

第三方服務

廣告資料處理請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及 Google 如何使用合作夥伴 App 的資訊。TestFlight 測試期間，Apple 可能依其政策收集當機報告與你主動送出的意見回饋，請參閱 Apple 隱私權政策。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線及技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

資料刪除

在 App 內刪除拼圖即移除該拼圖的照片與進度；刪除 App 可移除所有本機資料。Apple 裝置備份須另外管理。刪除 App 不會撤回廣告同意或刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。

聯絡我們

如有隱私、支援、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。如主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與你附上的截圖，用於回覆與排查問題。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼或付款資料。

Summary

This policy covers the PuzzSpot iOS jigsaw helper app and this support website. The app is maintained by an individual developer. It has no account and never sends your photos or puzzle data to the developer.

Data stored

Box photos, puzzle names, piece counts, found places and settings are stored on your device. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your system settings.

The app uses the camera only while you photograph a box lid, place a piece or look for edge pieces; you can also pick a box photo with the photo picker. All recognition (placing a piece, finding edge pieces) runs on the device with OpenCV and Apple Vision; images never leave the device, and the live camera view is not stored.

The app asks to add to your photo library only when you choose to save a progress picture; when you share a finished-puzzle card, you choose the recipient in the system share sheet. The Home Screen widget reads puzzle progress on your device through an app group; nothing leaves the device.

Ads and tracking

PuzzSpot integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for interstitial ads and applicable privacy choices. When ads are shown, Google and participating ad services may process approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device or app-scoped identifiers, advertising and interaction data, performance information, and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and service operation. The app does not track you through App Tracking Transparency and does not request location permission.

The app requests non-personalized ads, but this does not mean no data is collected. Ads appear only when you return to Home after finding pieces, never during your first session. Beta builds show no ads. When required by the SDK, reopen privacy choices in the app's Settings.

If a Remove ads purchase is offered later, it is handled by Apple In-App Purchase; we do not receive your payment details.

Feedback and our other apps

Send feedback in Settings sends what you type, the email you may add and the app version only when you tap Send, to the developer’s server agentmesh.uk (hosted on Cloudflare), which files it as an item in the developer’s private GitHub repository and then deletes its copy; the daily send limit counts a hash of your IP address that changes every day and is deleted the next day. It is used only to reply and improve the app; please leave out sensitive information, and email us to have it deleted. More from the developer at the bottom of Settings lists our other apps from agentmesh.uk with no personal data or identifiers sent; names, icons and ratings come from Apple’s App Store lookup service (itunes.apple.com) for your region, and taps are not tracked.

Third-party services

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps for advertising data practices. During TestFlight testing, Apple may collect crash reports and feedback you choose to send under its Privacy Policy.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Deleting a puzzle in the app removes its photo and progress; deleting the app removes all local data. Apple device backups must be managed separately. Deleting the app does not withdraw ad consent or erase data already held by ad providers.

Contact

For privacy, support, data, or ad concerns, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots you attach to reply and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment details.
