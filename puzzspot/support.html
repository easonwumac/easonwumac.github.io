常見問題

怎麼開始？

拍下拼圖盒蓋（或從照片挑一張），選擇片數，再把一片拼圖放到鏡頭下，盒蓋圖上就會亮出它的位置。盒蓋平放、光線均勻、拼圖片的圖案朝上，最容易找到。

找邊框片怎麼用？

點首頁的「找邊框片」，從正上方對著攤開的拼圖片，邊框片會亮青綠色、角片亮黃色。拼圖片之間留一點空隙、不要疊在一起；片數很多時靠近一點拍，一次看一小堆。

需要帳號嗎？資料存在哪裡？

不需要帳號，App 沒有開發者營運的後端。盒蓋照片、拼圖進度與設定都存在你的裝置，辨識全部在裝置上進行，不會上傳。Apple 裝置備份可能依系統設定保留 App 資料。

為什麼找不到位置？

請確認盒蓋照片清楚、沒有反光，片數設定與盒子上一致，並把拼圖片放在單色桌面上、圖案朝上。顏色很單調的區域（天空、海）本來就較難判斷，可以試著換個角度或靠近一點。

回報問題

請提供 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本及重現步驟。如附截圖或照片，請先遮蔽個人資料。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼、付款資料或私鑰。

FAQ

How do I start?

Photograph the puzzle box lid (or pick a photo), choose the piece count, then hold a piece under the camera: its place lights up on the box picture. A flat lid, even light and pieces picture side up work best.

How does the edge finder work?

Tap Find edge pieces on Home and point the camera straight down at loose pieces: edge pieces light up teal, corners gold. Leave a little space between pieces and keep them from overlapping; with many pieces, move closer and look at one small pile at a time.

Do I need an account, and where is my data stored?

No account is needed and the app has no developer-operated backend. Box photos, puzzle progress and settings stay on your device, and all recognition runs on the device; nothing is uploaded. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your settings.

Why can't it find a piece?

Check that the box photo is sharp and free of glare, the piece count matches the box, and the piece lies picture side up on a plain surface. Plain areas such as sky or sea are harder to place; try another angle or move closer.

Report a problem

Include the app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Hide personal information in screenshots or photos. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or private keys.
