摘要

本政策適用於 AutoCombo iOS App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護，不需要帳號，不會把你的截圖或盤面傳到任何伺服器，也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。

照片與截圖

你按「讀取最新截圖」時，App 會在你允許照片權限後讀取最新的一張截圖；從相簿挑選時只取得你選的那一張。圖片的辨識（找盤面、判斷符石，以及 Apple Vision 的外觀特徵）全部在裝置上完成，不會上傳，也不會另外保存圖片。

存放在裝置上的資料

你修正過的格子外觀特徵、盤面版面、條件與設定保存在裝置上，用來讓之後的辨識更準。「設定 → 清除學習過的符石與版面」可清除；刪除 App 也會一併刪除。

廣告

AutoCombo 使用 Google AdMob 顯示橫幅與插頁廣告，且只請求非個人化廣告；App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。你的截圖與盤面不會傳給廣告服務。在適用地區，App 會在載入廣告前透過 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）處理廣告同意。插頁廣告只在開啟新盤面時偶爾出現（每 10 個盤面最多一次，間隔至少 10 分鐘），不會打斷路徑播放。

即使是非個人化廣告，AdMob 仍可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

聯絡我們

若有隱私、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與附件，只用於回覆。

Summary

This policy covers the AutoCombo iOS app and this support website. The app is maintained by an independent developer, needs no account, never sends your screenshots or boards to any server, and does not collect usage analytics itself.

Photos and screenshots

When you tap Use the latest screenshot, the app reads your newest screenshot after you allow photo access; picking from your library gives it only the one photo you choose. Recognition (finding the board, reading orbs and Apple Vision look features) runs entirely on your device; images are never uploaded and not kept.

Data on your device

The look features of cells you corrected, board layouts, conditions and settings stay on your device to make later reads more accurate. Settings → Forget learned orbs and layouts clears them; deleting the app removes them too.

Ads

AutoCombo uses Google AdMob to show banner and interstitial ads and requests non-personalized ads only. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. Your screenshots and boards are never sent to the ad service. Where required, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising consent before loading ads. Interstitials appear now and then when you open a new board (at most once every 10 boards, at least 10 minutes apart), never while a path plays.

Even for non-personalized ads, AdMob may process IP addresses, device and ad interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Contact

For privacy, data, or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments, used only to reply.
