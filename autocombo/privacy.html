摘要

本政策適用於 AutoCombo iOS App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護，不需要帳號，不會把你的截圖或盤面傳到任何伺服器（除非你自己送出回報或意見），也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。

照片與截圖

你按「讀取最新截圖」時，App 會在你允許照片權限後讀取最新的一張截圖；從相簿挑選時只取得你選的那一張；從其他 App 用「分享」傳給 AutoCombo，或在「捷徑」中傳入的圖片，只會暫存在裝置上 AutoCombo 自己的空間，開啟後讀取就刪除。圖片的辨識（找盤面、判斷符石，以及 Apple Vision 的外觀特徵）全部在裝置上完成，不會上傳，也不會另外保存圖片。

回報圖片與意見回饋

「回報這張圖」會打開意見表單，先放好這張圖（縮小並移除位置等照片中繼資料）和辨識結果；設定中的「傳送意見」也可以從相簿附上最多 3 張圖。你可以移除圖片或補充文字，只有按下「送出」時，才會把你的文字、圖片、選填的 Email、App 版本、裝置型號與 iOS 版本傳到開發者的伺服器 agentmesh.uk（由 Cloudflare 託管），轉成開發者私人 GitHub 儲存庫中的一則待辦。文字在轉成待辦後即從伺服器刪除；圖片存放在只有該待辦才有連結的網址，90 天後刪除。每日送出次數以當天更換的 IP 雜湊計算，隔日刪除。這些資料只用於回覆與改善 App，請勿附上敏感資料，可來信要求提早刪除。

存放在裝置上的資料

你修正過的格子外觀特徵（含一張很小的格子縮圖）、盤面版面、條件與設定保存在裝置上，用來讓之後的辨識更準。「設定 → 學習的珠子與位置」可逐筆修改、刪除或全部清除；刪除 App 也會一併刪除。

廣告

AutoCombo 使用 Google AdMob 顯示橫幅與插頁廣告，且只請求非個人化廣告；App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。你的截圖與盤面不會傳給廣告服務。App 內含 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）SDK，但因為只請求非個人化廣告、不追蹤，不會顯示同意表單。插頁廣告只在開啟新盤面時偶爾出現（每 10 個盤面最多一次，間隔至少 10 分鐘），不會打斷路徑播放。一次性購買「移除廣告」可關閉所有廣告；購買由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊。

即使是非個人化廣告，AdMob 仍可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

「設定」中的「開發者的其他 App」每天最多一次向 agentmesh.uk 取得我們其他 App 的清單，並向 Apple 的 App Store 查詢名稱、圖示與評分；請求只帶 App 識別碼、商店地區與語言，不含任何可識別你的資料。廣告位置沒有廣告可顯示時，會改顯示清單中的一款我們自己的 App，不另外傳送資料。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

聯絡我們

若有隱私、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與附件，只用於回覆。

Summary

This policy covers the AutoCombo iOS app and this support website. The app is maintained by an independent developer, needs no account, never sends your screenshots or boards to any server (unless you send a report or feedback yourself), and does not collect usage analytics itself.

Photos and screenshots

When you tap Use the latest screenshot, the app reads your newest screenshot after you allow photo access; picking from your library gives it only the one photo you choose; an image you send with Share or Shortcuts waits only in AutoCombo’s own storage on the device and is deleted once read. Recognition (finding the board, reading orbs and Apple Vision look features) runs entirely on your device; images are never uploaded and not kept.

Reporting an image and feedback

Report this image opens the feedback form with the image (downscaled, with location and other photo metadata removed) and the read result already attached; Send feedback in Settings can also attach up to 3 pictures from your library. You can remove a picture or add text, and nothing is sent until you tap Send. Then your text, pictures, the email you may add, the app version, device model and iOS version go to the developer’s server agentmesh.uk (hosted on Cloudflare), which files them as an item in the developer’s private GitHub repository. The text is deleted from the server once filed; pictures stay at an address only that item links to and are deleted after 90 days. The daily send limit counts a hash of your IP address that changes every day and is deleted the next day. This is used only to reply and improve the app; please leave out sensitive information, and email us to have it deleted sooner.

Data on your device

The look features of cells you corrected (with a small thumbnail of each cell), board layouts, conditions and settings stay on your device to make later reads more accurate. Settings → Learned orbs and layouts lets you relabel or delete them one by one, or all at once; deleting the app removes them too.

Ads

AutoCombo uses Google AdMob to show banner and interstitial ads and requests non-personalized ads only. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. Your screenshots and boards are never sent to the ad service. The app includes Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK but shows no consent form, because it requests non-personalized ads only and does not track you. Interstitials appear now and then when you open a new board (at most once every 10 boards, at least 10 minutes apart), never while a path plays. The one-time Remove ads purchase turns off all ads; Apple handles the payment and we never receive your payment details.

Even for non-personalized ads, AdMob may process IP addresses, device and ad interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure.

“More from the developer” in Settings fetches the list of our other apps from agentmesh.uk and their names, icons and ratings from Apple’s App Store at most once a day; the requests carry only app IDs, the store region and language, nothing that identifies you. When the ad space has no ad to show, it shows one of these apps of ours instead, with nothing more sent.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Contact

For privacy, data, or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments, used only to reply.
