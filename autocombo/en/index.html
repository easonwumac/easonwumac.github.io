AutoCombo

AutoCombo plans your orb-matching moves. Take a screenshot in your game, come back and tap once: AutoCombo finds the board, reads every orb and draws a route you can follow with your finger.

Free on the App StoreSupportFeedback

Features

One tap to read

  • Use your latest screenshot, or pick from the album, paste, or set a board by hand
  • Finds the board and its size by itself: 6×5, 7×6, 5×4 and more, on any iPhone or iPad
  • Tap a cell to fix it; matching orbs on the board follow, and AutoCombo remembers next time
  • Share a screenshot to AutoCombo from Photos, or start it from Shortcuts
  • Fade your screenshot over the read board to check it, and review or undo any correction

Set the conditions once

  • At least, exactly or at most N combos, optionally counting only the first clear
  • Orb counts, groups per type, equal combos, number of types, enhanced orbs, orbs you must not clear
  • Cross, L, T, 3×3, full rows and columns, or draw your own shape
  • Marked cells that must clear, a fixed start and end
  • Frozen, weathered, electrified and locked orbs; cells you can't enter, that stop the drag, can't clear or burn

Follow the path

  • The line shifts from an orange start to a pink end, so crossings read clearly
  • Replay it, or scrub through step by step
  • Flip to the board after the move and check each condition

Reading and solving happen on your device; a screenshot leaves it only if you send one yourself in a report. AutoCombo only shows the route and never controls another app.

AutoCombo is free with ads. A one-time purchase removes the ads.

FAQ

Can an app plan orb-matching routes for me?

AutoCombo reads your game screenshot, finds the board and every orb, and draws a route you can follow with your finger; you can set combos, shapes, must-clear cells and more. It only shows the route and never controls another app; reading and solving stay on the phone.

Does AutoCombo move the orbs for me?

No. AutoCombo only draws a route you can trace yourself; it does not control other apps.

Which boards does AutoCombo support?

Boards such as 6×5, 7×6 and 5×4, on phones of every size and iPad; it finds the board's position and size in the screenshot automatically.

Does AutoCombo upload my screenshots?

Recognition and solving run on the phone; a screenshot leaves it only when you send a report yourself. AutoCombo is free with ads, and one purchase removes them.
