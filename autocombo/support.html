常見問題

怎麼用？

在遊戲裡截圖後回到 AutoCombo，按「讀取最新截圖」。App 會讀出盤面、算出路徑並播放：從橘色圓點開始，沿著編號順序拖到白色圓圈結束。也可以從相簿選圖、貼上，或手動排盤。

讀錯了怎麼辦？

點一下讀錯的格子，選正確的符石與狀態。App 會記住這個外觀，之後遇到同樣的珠子會自動認對；同一張盤面上長得一樣的格子也會一起修正。「設定 → 清除學習過的符石與版面」可清除這些記憶。

支援哪些條件？

連擊數（至少／固定／最多）、消除指定符石或總粒數、指定屬性組數、連擊相等、N 屬性、強化突破、禁消、拼圖盾（可指定位置與組數）、指定位置盾、起手與結束位置，以及只算首批消除。盤面支援 5×4 到 7×6。

完成後的盤面怎麼看？

在結果卡片按格子按鈕，會顯示轉完後的盤面：首批會消除的組合加框並編號，下方列出首批與全部的連擊數和消除粒數。

為什麼要照片權限？

只為了一鍵讀取你最新的一張截圖。不開權限也能從相簿挑選單張照片、貼上或手動排盤。圖片只在你的裝置上分析，不會上傳。

回報問題

請附上 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、盤面大小與發生步驟；若是辨識錯誤，可附上那張截圖。

FAQ

How do I use it?

Take a screenshot in your game, come back to AutoCombo and tap Use the latest screenshot. The app reads the board, finds the path and plays it: start at the orange dot and follow the numbers to the white ring. You can also pick a photo, paste one, or set the board by hand.

A cell was read wrong.

Tap it and choose the right orb and state. The app remembers that look and reads it correctly next time; cells on the same board that look the same are fixed too. Settings → Forget learned orbs and layouts clears this.

Which conditions are supported?

Combo counts (at least, exactly, at most), orbs of a type or in total, combos of a type, equal combos, number of types, enhanced orbs, forbidden types, puzzle shapes (optionally at a fixed position and 1–3 groups), marked cells, start and end cells, and first clear only. Boards from 5×4 to 7×6.

How do I see the finished board?

Tap the grid button on the result card: the board after the move appears with the groups of the first clear outlined and numbered, and the combo and orb counts below.

Why photo access?

Only to read your newest screenshot in one tap. Without it you can still pick a single photo, paste, or set the board by hand. Images are analysed on your device and never uploaded.

Report a problem

Include the app version, device model, iOS version, board size and the steps; for a misread board, attach the screenshot.
