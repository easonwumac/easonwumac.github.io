常見問題

為什麼背景檢查沒有準時執行？

背景重新整理何時執行由 iOS 決定，會受電量、使用習慣與系統資源影響。Tapmate 無法保證固定間隔、準時檢查或每次都執行；通知也要等流程實際執行並偵測到變化後才會發出。

想在指定時間執行，如何設定捷徑？

先在 Tapmate 儲存流程，再打開 Apple「捷徑」→「自動化」，新增「特定時間」的個人自動化，選好時間與重複日期。加入「Run Tapmate flow」動作並選擇流程，將自動化設為「立即執行」。這可在指定時間觸發流程，不必打開 Tapmate；能否完成仍取決於網路、網站與 iOS 當時的執行條件。時間設定可參考 Apple 捷徑指南。

可以讓備用裝置持續檢查嗎？

可以。把備用 iPhone 或 iPad 接上電源、保持連網，讓 Tapmate 留在前景，將流程設為「App 開啟時」執行，並開啟「保持螢幕開啟」。鎖定螢幕或切換到其他 App 後，就無法維持前景的檢查間隔。

為什麼看不到 AI 功能？

AI 功能需要支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置、iOS 26 或以上版本，並啟用 Apple Intelligence 且備妥裝置端模型。不支援或尚未就緒時，你仍可手動選取網頁元素、建立步驟與設定條件。AI 在裝置上運作，部分背景情境可能無法使用；需要 AI 的步驟若無法執行，請查看執行歷程中的說明。

如何清除瀏覽資料？

前往「設定 → 清除瀏覽資料」，移除內建瀏覽器的 Cookie、快取與網站登入狀態。這不會刪除流程或執行歷程，但需要登入的網站可能須重新登入。詳見 隱私權政策。

如何移除廣告或還原購買？

在「設定 → 移除廣告」可選擇一次性購買，沒有訂閱。重新安裝或換裝置後，在「設定 → 還原購買」恢復權益；請連上網路，並確認 App Store 使用與原購買相同的 Apple 帳號。還原成功後不需再次付費；這只還原移除廣告權益，不會還原流程或執行歷程。

網站使用

請遵守各網站的使用條款。Tapmate 不會解答或繞過 CAPTCHA、人機驗證或網站的自動化檢查；遇到驗證或需要重新登入時，流程會停止並通知你開啟網頁手動處理。

回報問題

請附上 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、重現步驟與畫面上的錯誤訊息。如附截圖或執行記錄，請先遮蔽個人資料、私人網址與網頁內容。請勿提供密碼、Cookie 或付款資料。

FAQ

Why don’t background checks run on time?

iOS decides when background refresh runs, taking battery level, usage patterns, and system resources into account. Tapmate cannot guarantee fixed intervals, exact timing, or that every check will run. A change alert is sent only after a flow actually runs and detects a change.

How do I use Shortcuts for a specific time?

Save a flow in Tapmate, then open Apple Shortcuts → Automation and create a Time of Day personal automation. Choose the time and repeat days, add the Run Tapmate flow action, select your flow, and choose Run Immediately. This triggers the flow at the chosen time without opening Tapmate; completion still depends on the network, website, and iOS execution conditions. See Apple’s Shortcuts guide for time options.

Can I keep a spare device checking?

Yes. Plug in a spare iPhone or iPad, keep it online with Tapmate in the foreground, set the flow to run While open, and enable Keep screen on. Locking the screen or switching to another app prevents the foreground interval from continuing.

Why are AI features missing?

AI features require an Apple Intelligence device running iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence enabled and the on-device model ready. If unsupported or not ready, you can still pick page elements, build steps, and set conditions manually. AI runs locally and may be unavailable in some background contexts. If a step needs AI and cannot run, check the explanation in the run history.

How do I clear browsing data?

Go to Settings → Clear browsing data to remove the built-in browser’s cookies, cache, and website sign-in sessions. This does not delete flows or run history, but you may need to sign in to websites again. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?

You can make an optional one-time purchase in Settings → Remove ads, with no subscription. After reinstalling or changing devices, use Settings → Restore purchases while online and signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. Once restored, you do not pay again. Restoration restores your Remove ads entitlement, not flows or run history.

Using websites

Follow each website’s terms. Tapmate does not solve or bypass CAPTCHAs, human verification, or website automation checks. If verification or a fresh sign-in is needed, the flow stops and notifies you to open the page and handle it manually.

Report a problem

Include the app version, device model, iOS version, steps to reproduce, and any displayed error message. Hide personal details, private URLs, and page content in screenshots or run logs. Do not send passwords, cookies, or payment information.
