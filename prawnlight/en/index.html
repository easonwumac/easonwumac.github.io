Prawnlight

Prawnlight is a management game set in a Taiwanese indoor prawn-fishing hall that stays open late into the night. You are the boss, not the angler: order stock, release prawns, lay out the floor, hire staff and keep the guests fishing until closing time.

Free on the App StoreSupportFeedback

Features

  • Pick your site on a map of Taiwan: 8 places, each with its own rent, foot traffic and spending
  • Place ponds, stools, grills, a drinks fridge and dozens more items wherever you like; a good layout means better business
  • Hire cashiers and floor staff, each with their own skills and character
  • Hold fishing contests and on-the-hour prawn releases, and celebrate 7 festivals, from Lunar New Year and the Dragon Boat Festival to Halloween and Christmas, each with its own limited decoration
  • Rent or buy up to 10 neighboring lots to enlarge the hall or build a parking lot, a stir-fry restaurant, a tackle shop or a pavilion, then open branches as you level up
  • Every Monday the revenue league ranks you against 19 rival halls
  • 12 regulars keep coming back, and 16 rare prawns wait for your collection
  • Daily check-in rewards
  • Plays upright or sideways on iPhone, on iPad, and on iPhone Duo folded or unfolded

The hall opens on a loan. Stock, rent and every Sunday's repayment come due before the takings do, so growing too fast can empty the till.

Prawnlight is free to play. Ads play only when you choose to watch one for a reward. Optional in-app purchases include bundles, a monthly card, diamonds, double earnings while you're away and 5× speed.

FAQ

Is there a game about running a Taiwanese prawn-fishing hall?

Prawnlight puts you in charge of an indoor prawn-fishing hall in Taiwan: choose a site, stock prawns, lay out the hall, hire staff and run contests while paying off a loan. Free to play.

Is Prawnlight free?

Yes. Ads play only when you choose to watch one for a reward; optional in-app purchases are available.

What do you do in Prawnlight?

You run a Taiwanese indoor prawn-fishing hall: pick a site on the map of Taiwan, place pools and grills, hire staff, hold contests and hourly prawn releases, then expand or open branches. You start with a loan, so growing too fast runs you out of cash.
