常見問題

怎麼玩？

你是釣蝦場老闆，不用自己下竿。在台灣地圖上選好地點開店，點鐵鎚擺放蝦池和凳子，點蝦池放蝦，再按「開門營業」。客人會進來釣蝦、付錢。下方按鈕可以叫貨、設定價格與活動、請員工、看帳本與營收榜、開分店、看圖鑑和開啟商店。

錢不夠付租金和貸款怎麼辦？

每週日要付租金和貸款。進貨、擺設和擴建都要先花錢，擴張太快現金就會斷；現金不夠付款時會破產，蝦場重新開始。可以先調整蝦價、少開幾池，或到帳本查看收支。

離開遊戲時還會賺錢嗎？

會。離開期間蝦場會繼續營業，回來時領取收益，存滿後就不再增加。允許通知的話，存滿時會提醒你。

可以離線遊玩嗎？

可以。遊戲進度存在裝置上，廣告或商店暫時無法使用時不會影響存檔。廣告服務的資料處理並非完全離線。

換手機或重新安裝後，購買還在嗎？

5 倍速、新手禮包、節慶裝飾與收益加倍可在商店上方的「恢復購買」取回。月卡、禮包與鑽石屬於消耗性項目，無法恢復。遊戲進度存在裝置上，刪除 App 會一併刪除。

如何查看廣告隱私選項？

遊戲只有你自行選擇觀看的獎勵廣告。SDK 要求提供隱私選項時，商店會出現「隱私」按鈕，可重新開啟表單。選擇可能因地區與服務狀態而不同。目前遊戲版本未要求 Apple 跨 App 追蹤授權。

回報問題

遊戲問題：請提供 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、當時的畫面及重現步驟。如附截圖，請先遮蔽個人資料。

購買問題：請提供購買時間與項目，並先試「恢復購買」。退款請透過 Apple 的 回報問題 頁面申請。

不適當廣告：請提供廣告出現的時間與時區及廣告內容。如廣告提供回報功能，也可直接使用。請勿為回報而點擊廣告。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼、付款資料或私鑰。

FAQ

How do I play?

You run the prawn-fishing hall; you never fish yourself. Pick a site on the map of Taiwan, tap the hammer to place ponds and stools, tap a pond to release prawns, then tap Open for business. Guests come in, fish and pay. The buttons at the bottom order stock, set prices and events, hire staff, show the ledger and the revenue league, open branches, show your collection and open the shop.

What if I cannot pay the rent and the loan?

Rent and the loan payment are due every Sunday. Stock, items and expansion all cost money up front, so growing too fast can empty the till; if you cannot pay, the hall goes bankrupt and starts over. Try adjusting prawn prices, running fewer ponds, or checking the ledger.

Does the hall earn while I am away?

Yes. The hall keeps trading while you are away, and you collect the takings when you return, up to a limit. If you allow notifications, the game tells you when the limit is reached.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Progress is stored on your device, and an unavailable ad or shop does not affect your save. Advertising data processing is not fully offline.

Do my purchases carry over to a new phone or a reinstall?

5× speed, the starter pack, festival decorations and doubled takings come back with Restore Purchases at the top of the shop. The monthly card, bundles and diamonds are consumable and cannot be restored. Game progress is stored on the device and is deleted with the app.

How do I review ad privacy choices?

The game only has rewarded ads that you choose to watch. When required by the SDK, the shop shows a Privacy button that reopens the form. Choices vary by region and service status. The current game version does not request Apple’s cross-app tracking authorization.

Report a problem

Game issues: include the app version, device model, iOS version, what was on screen, and steps to reproduce. Hide personal information in screenshots.

Purchase issues: include the purchase time and item, and try Restore Purchases first. Request refunds through Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Inappropriate ads: include the time and time zone and the ad content. You can also use the ad provider’s reporting control if available. Do not click an ad just to report it. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or private keys.
