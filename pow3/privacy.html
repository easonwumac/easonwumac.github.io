摘要

本政策適用於爆三 POW³（App Store 名稱 Pow3）iOS App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護，不需要帳號，不要求任何系統權限，也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。

存放在裝置上的資料

進行中的這一局、最高分、最深樓層、每日挖礦成績與聲音、震動設定都只保存在你的裝置上，不會上傳。刪除 App 會一併刪除。

分享

每日挖礦結束時按「分享」，會用 iOS 分享選單送出一段文字（日期、樓層、分數與表情符號格子），送到哪裡由你決定；App 不會自動傳送。

廣告與購買

爆三使用 Google AdMob 顯示橫幅、插頁與可選的獎勵廣告，且只請求非個人化廣告；App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。插頁廣告只在離開已結束的一局時偶爾出現（至少間隔 4 分鐘，第一次使用不會出現）；獎勵廣告只在你按「看廣告」時播放。

即使是非個人化廣告，AdMob 仍可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

「移除廣告」是一次性購買，由 Apple 處理付款；我們不會收到你的付款資料。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

聯絡我們

若有隱私、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與附件，只用於回覆。

Summary

This policy covers the POW³ iOS app (App Store name Pow3) and this support website. The app is maintained by an independent developer, needs no account, asks for no system permissions, and does not collect usage analytics itself.

Data on your device

Your run in progress, best score, deepest floor, daily dig results and sound and vibration settings stay on your device and are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes them.

Sharing

Tapping Share after a daily dig opens the iOS share sheet with a short text (date, floor, score and an emoji grid); you choose where it goes. The app never sends it on its own.

Ads and purchases

POW³ uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads and requests non-personalized ads only. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. Interstitials appear now and then when you leave a finished run (at least 4 minutes apart, never in your first session); a rewarded ad plays only when you tap Watch ad.

Even for non-personalized ads, AdMob may process IP addresses, device and ad interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure.

Remove ads is a one-time purchase handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

Contact

For privacy, data, or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments, used only to reply.
