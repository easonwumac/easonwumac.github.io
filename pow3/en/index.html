Pow3

Dig down, one level at a time. Matches beside the rock break it.

Free on the App StoreSupportFeedback

Features

Chain reactions

Four in a row makes a jackhammer, an L or T makes a powder keg, five makes a prism, and specials set each other off.

The endless shaft

After level 6 the endless shaft opens: no levels, only depth.

More

Pick a shaft (Old Shaft, Gold Seam or Geode), choose relics on the way down, grow five miners with their own skills, and play the daily dig and the weekly challenge.

Stars and trophies

Up to three stars per level, 18 achievements, and Game Center leaderboards for total stars and endless depth.

Goals you dig for

  • Dig through a set number of metres of rock
  • Break gold nuggets out of the stone
  • Smash a core before your moves run out
  • Finish early and the moves you saved pay out as points, with up to eight jackhammers as a finale.

A mine, not a candy road

  • The level map is a cross-section of the shaft: every stop shows its depth and the rock it is dug through
  • Seven rock materials, from dirt to meteorite iron, shown by texture and cracks
  • A depth ruler runs beside the board
  • Cores fight back: they freeze gems, turn gems to stone and shake the board
  • Deeper down: frozen gems, amethyst, boulders and lava

Made for short breaks

  • Plays offline, no sign-up
  • iPhone and iPad
  • One purchase removes banners and full-screen ads; reward videos stay optional

POW³ is a gem-swapping game set in a mine shaft. Line up three gems and the match cracks the rock beside it; clear a whole row and you sink one metre. Each level is a stop further down the same shaft, with its own goal and a fixed number of moves.

FAQ

Is there a different kind of match-3 game?

Pow3 is gem swapping in a mine shaft: matches crack the rock beside them and a cleared row sinks you one metre, each level with its own digging goal and fixed moves; after level 6 an endless mine ranks you by depth. It plays offline with no sign-up.
