常見問題

怎麼玩？

把托盤裡的寶石放到礦坑任一格，泥土就會被挖開。挖開格子上的紅點代表相鄰地雷的數量。三顆相同寶石相連會合成下一個 3 的次方（1、3、9、27…），衝擊波會炸開周圍泥土並拆除地雷。挖開所有安全格子就會往下一層。

怎麼插旗？插旗有什麼用？

長按泥土格插旗。衝擊波炸到插旗的地雷會觸發 POW，得分三倍。猜錯沒有懲罰，只是那格不能放寶石。

遺物是什麼？

每過一層可以從三個遺物選一個，例如更大的爆破範圍、每層第一次踩雷不扣心的鋼頭靴、每層自動標出一顆地雷的探測擺錘等，效果持續整局。

一局什麼時候結束？

愛心用完，或盤面滿到沒地方放寶石時結束。結算時可以看一次廣告復活（加一顆心，或清掉最小的寶石）。

每日挖礦是什麼？

每天一座固定的礦坑，所有人挑戰同一張盤面。結束後可以分享表情符號格子的成績。

進度會不見嗎？

每一步都自動存在裝置上，關掉 App 再開可以從首頁「繼續」。刪除 App 會清除進度。

買了移除廣告，換手機怎麼辦？

在設定按「恢復購買」，使用同一個 Apple 帳號即可。

回報問題

請附上 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、樓層與發生步驟，有截圖更好。

FAQ

How do I play?

Drop the gem from your tray on any tile of the mine and the soil under it is dug open. Red dots on dug tiles count the mines next to them. Three matching gems in a group merge into the next power of 3 (1, 3, 9, 27…), and the shockwave digs the soil around them and defuses mines. Clear every safe tile to go down a floor.

How do flags work?

Touch and hold a soil tile to flag it. A blast that catches a flagged mine sets off a POW for triple points. A wrong flag costs nothing, but you cannot drop a gem there.

What are relics?

After each floor you pick one of three relics, such as wider blasts, steel boots that make the first mine on a floor cost no heart, or a dowsing pendulum that flags one mine per floor. They last for the whole run.

When does a run end?

When you run out of hearts or the board has no room for a gem. On the result card you can watch one ad to continue (one more heart, or the smallest gems cleared).

What is the daily dig?

One fixed mine a day, the same board for everyone. Share your emoji-grid result when you finish.

Will I lose my progress?

Every move is saved on your device; reopen the app and tap Continue on Home. Deleting the app clears it.

I bought Remove ads. How do I get it on a new device?

Tap Restore purchases in Settings with the same Apple Account.
