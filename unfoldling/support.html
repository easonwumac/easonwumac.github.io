常見問題

怎麼玩？

右頁下方有彩色拉片。拉一次，同色的立體零件（吊橋、梯子、高塔、木筏、升降台、柵門等）都會動；再拉一次就推回原位。每次拉完，小紅帽會盡量走向金鑰匙，走到就過關。書籤絲帶顯示三星還剩幾次拉動；第 21 頁起野狼會在每次拉動後靠近她。靜止時，各拉片會輪流用自己的顏色預告零件、小紅帽和野狼的去向。卡關時可以按燈泡看提示。

愛心和提示用完了怎麼辦？

只有失敗才會用掉 1 顆愛心；重來、離開和過關都不用。愛心每小時回復 1 顆，最多 24 顆，用完時可以自選觀看獎勵廣告取得 1 顆。提示可在提示商店觀看獎勵廣告取得 3 次，或購買 10 次、40 次的提示包。

可以離線遊玩嗎？

書頁可離線遊玩，廣告不可用時不會阻擋遊戲進度保存。廣告服務的資料處理並非完全離線。

換手機或重新安裝後，購買還在嗎？

「移除廣告」可在頁面清單左上角的「恢復購買」取回。提示包屬於消耗性項目，無法恢復。遊戲進度存在裝置上，刪除 App 會一併刪除。

如何查看廣告隱私選項？

SDK 要求提供隱私選項時（例如歐洲經濟區與英國），可在頁面清單點「隱私權選項」重新開啟表單。選擇可能因地區與服務狀態而不同。目前遊戲版本未要求 Apple 跨 App 追蹤授權。

回報問題

遊戲問題：請提供頁數、App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本及重現步驟。如附截圖，請先遮蔽個人資料。

購買問題：請提供購買時間與項目，並先試「恢復購買」。退款請透過 Apple 的 回報問題 頁面申請。

不適當廣告：請提供廣告出現的時間與時區、廣告內容和當時頁數。如廣告提供回報功能，也可直接使用。請勿為回報而點擊廣告。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼、付款資料或私鑰。

FAQ

How do I play?

Coloured pull tabs stick out below the right page. A pull works every pop-up piece of its colour (drawbridges, ladders, towers, rafts, lifts, gates and more); pulling it again works them back. After each pull the girl in the red hood walks as far toward the golden key as she can; reaching it wins the page. Bookmark ribbons count the pulls left for three stars, and from page 21 a wolf steps toward her after every pull. At rest the tabs take turns showing, in their colour, where their pieces, the girl and the wolf will go. When stuck, tap the light bulb for a hint.

What if I run out of hearts or hints?

Only a failed try uses a heart; restarting, leaving and winning are free. Hearts refill one an hour, up to 24, and with none left you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for one. In the hint shop a rewarded ad gives 3 hints, or you can buy a pack of 10 or 40.

Can I play offline?

Pages work offline, and unavailable ads do not prevent game progress from being saved. Advertising data processing is not fully offline.

Do my purchases carry over to a new phone or a reinstall?

Remove Ads comes back with Restore Purchases, top left of the page list. Hint packs are consumable and cannot be restored. Game progress is stored on the device and is deleted with the app.

How do I review ad privacy choices?

When required by the SDK (for example in the EEA and UK), tap Privacy Choices on the page list. Choices vary by region and service status. The current game version does not request Apple’s cross-app tracking authorization.

Report a problem

Game issues: include the page number, app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Hide personal information in screenshots.

Purchase issues: include the purchase time and item, and try Restore Purchases first. Request refunds through Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Inappropriate ads: include the time and time zone, ad content, and page. You can also use the ad provider’s reporting control if available. Do not click an ad just to report it. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or private keys.
