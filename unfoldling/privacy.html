摘要

本政策適用於「展頁 Unfoldling－立體書解謎」iOS 遊戲與本支援網站。本遊戲由個人開發者維護。

資料存放

已解鎖的頁數與星星、愛心數量及其回復時間、提示次數、已看過的故事插畫、已購買的「移除廣告」狀態與交易編號、靜音設定、評分邀請與廣告顯示頻率計數儲存在你的裝置。本遊戲沒有玩家登入、自建帳號系統或開發者營運的遊戲後端，也不自行上傳這些遊戲存檔。裝置備份可能依你的 Apple 系統設定保留 App 資料。本地遊戲資料在你刪除 App 之前保留。

App 內購買

提示包與「移除廣告」透過 Apple 的 App 內購買處理。付款由 Apple 完成，開發者不會收到你的付款資料；遊戲只從 Apple 取得交易結果，用來發放購買內容。頁面清單左上角的「恢復購買」會向 Apple 查詢你已購買的「移除廣告」。

廣告與追蹤

遊戲整合 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）及 User Messaging Platform（UMP），用於提供橫幅、插頁與自選觀看的獎勵廣告，並管理適用的隱私選擇。Google 及參與廣告供應的服務可能處理 IP 位址及推估的大致位置、裝置或 App 範圍識別碼、廣告及互動資料、效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、分析、服務運作與改善。本遊戲不要求精確位置權限。

廣告服務的資料處理並非完全離線；「非個人化廣告」也不代表完全不蒐集資料。可請求的廣告依適用地區、SDK 狀態及有效隱私選擇而定。書頁可離線遊玩，廣告不可用時不會阻擋遊戲進度保存。購買「移除廣告」後不再顯示橫幅與插頁廣告；獎勵廣告仍由你自行選擇是否觀看。

SDK 要求提供隱私選項時（例如歐洲經濟區與英國），可在遊戲的頁面清單點「隱私權選項」重新開啟表單，調整適用的選擇。選擇可能因地區與服務狀態而不同。目前遊戲版本未要求 Apple 跨 App 追蹤授權；你也可以在 iOS「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」管理 App 的系統追蹤權限。

第三方服務

請參閱 Google 隱私權政策、Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或 App 的資訊及 Apple 隱私權政策，了解其處理方式、資料保留與控制選項。第三方資料依各服務商政策保留。

本網站使用 GitHub Pages 託管，沒有自行加入追蹤分析程式。GitHub 可能為提供網站服務處理連線及技術紀錄，詳見 GitHub 隱私聲明。Google、Apple、GitHub 與郵件服務可能在你所在地以外處理資料，依各自政策及適用保障措施運作。廣告或外部連結開啟的網站另適用其自身政策。

資料刪除

刪除 App 可移除本機遊戲資料，Apple 備份須另依系統設定管理。刪除 App 不會撤回廣告同意，也不會刪除廣告服務商或 Apple 已持有的資料。重新安裝後可用「恢復購買」取回「移除廣告」；提示包屬於消耗性項目，無法恢復。

可寄信要求存取、更正或刪除你提供給開發者的支援資料，或提出其他適用隱私權請求。對 Google 或 Apple 持有的資料，請使用其隱私政策所列控制與聯絡方式。

本遊戲並非以兒童為主要對象，也不刻意透過支援功能蒐集兒童個人資料。家長或監護人如認為兒童向我們提供了個人資料，請聯絡下方信箱。

聯絡我們

有關遊戲、個人資料或不適當廣告，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。如果你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與你附上的診斷資訊或截圖，用於回覆、排查問題及處理廣告回報。請勿提供密碼、驗證碼、付款資料或不必要的敏感資訊。

Summary

This policy covers the Unfoldling: Storybook Puzzles (展頁) iOS game and this support website. The game is maintained by an individual developer.

Data stored

Pages unlocked and their stars, hearts with their refill time, hint counts, the story pictures already seen, your Remove Ads purchase status and transaction IDs, the mute setting, and rating-prompt and ad-frequency counters are stored on your device. The game has no player login, developer-operated account system, or game backend, and does not itself upload these save files. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your system settings. Local game data remains until you delete the app.

In-app purchases

Hint packs and Remove Ads are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment and the developer never receives your payment details; the game only receives the transaction result from Apple to deliver what you bought. Restore Purchases, top left of the page list, asks Apple for your Remove Ads purchase.

Ads and tracking

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for banner, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded ads and applicable privacy choices. Google and participating ad services may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device or app-scoped identifiers, advertising and interaction data, performance information, and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, analysis, and service operation or improvement. The game does not request precise-location permission.

Advertising data processing is not fully offline. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is collected. Available ad requests depend on region, SDK status, and valid privacy choices. Pages work offline, and unavailable ads do not prevent game progress from being saved. After you buy Remove Ads, banner and interstitial ads stop; rewarded ads remain your choice to watch.

When required by the SDK (for example in the EEA and UK), tap Privacy Choices on the page list to review applicable choices. Availability varies by region and service status. The current game version does not request Apple’s cross-app tracking authorization. You can also manage system tracking permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Third-party services

See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner sites and apps, and Apple’s Privacy Policy for processing, retention, and controls. Third-party information follows each provider’s retention policy.

This website uses GitHub Pages without developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records to serve the website; see its Privacy Statement. Google, Apple, GitHub, and email services may process information outside your country under their policies and applicable safeguards. External websites and ad destinations have their own policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes local game data; manage Apple backups separately through system settings. It does not withdraw advertising consent or delete information already held by ad providers or Apple. After reinstalling, Restore Purchases brings back Remove Ads; hint packs are consumable and cannot be restored.

Email us to request access, correction, or deletion of support information you provided, or to exercise other applicable privacy rights. For information held by Google or Apple, use the controls and contact methods in their policies.

Children

The game is not primarily directed to children, and we do not knowingly seek children’s personal information through support. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information to us can contact the email address below.
