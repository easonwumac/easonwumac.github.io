摘要

空間稿 RoomDraft 提供室內空間掃描、居家規劃、量測、配置與設計圖組製作工具。App 使用相機與 LiDAR 建立室內空間資料；相機畫面用於掃描，不會由我們的伺服器接收即時影像。使用家具掃描時，拍攝的照片與重建中的暫存檔只存在裝置上，用於建立 3D 模型，完成或取消後即刪除；建立的家具模型隨專案儲存。

資料存放

你輸入的專案名稱、客戶資訊、地址、設計備註、尺寸、配置與掃描模型會儲存在裝置上。使用者登入 iCloud 並同步時，專案和掃描檔會儲存在其 Apple iCloud 私有資料庫，供其裝置同步。

你主動匯出 PDF、DXF、SVG、USDZ、GLB、PNG 渲染圖或專案備份並分享時，資料會交給你選擇的接收方或 App。你應確保對輸入及分享的房屋、客戶與設計資料有適當權利，並自行保存重要備份。

廣告與追蹤

目前 App 不使用第三方廣告或分析 SDK。

購買

掃描、建模、多專案及個人 iCloud 同步可免費使用。Pro 解鎖成品匯出（PDF 圖組、DXF、SVG、USDZ、GLB 與高解析 3D 渲染圖），為透過 Apple App Store 購買的自動續訂服務。期間、當地價格與試用條件以購買畫面為準。

訂閱由 Apple App Store 處理。App 使用 StoreKit 確認購買權益；我們不在 App 中取得付款卡號。你可於 Apple 帳號的訂閱設定管理或取消；取消後權益通常持續至已付款期間結束。退款依 Apple 的政策處理。

在 App 的 Pro 頁面點選「恢復購買」，並使用原購買 Apple 帳號。

第三方服務

iCloud 資料由 Apple 的 iCloud 服務處理，受 Apple 的條款與隱私政策約束。Apple App Store 處理訂閱，StoreKit 用於確認購買權益。主動分享的資料交由你選擇的接收方或 App。

資料刪除

本機專案由你在 App 的專案列表刪除。同步專案刪除後，App 會將刪除狀態同步至 iCloud；裝置離線時需待重新連線。也可在 Apple 裝置的 iCloud 儲存空間管理中移除本 App 資料。Apple 帳號或裝置上的備份可能另依 Apple 設定保留。客服郵件可寄信要求刪除。

Summary

RoomDraft provides tools for room scanning, home planning, measurement, layouts, and interior drawing sets. The app uses the camera and LiDAR to create indoor space data. Camera frames are used for scanning and are not streamed to our servers. When you scan furniture, the photos and reconstruction files stay on your device, are used only to build the 3D model, and are deleted when capture finishes or is cancelled; the resulting furniture model is saved with your project.

Data stored

Project names, client information, addresses, design notes, dimensions, layouts, and scan models you enter are stored on your device. When you sign in to iCloud and sync, projects and scan files are stored in your Apple iCloud private database for use across your devices.

When you export and share a PDF, DXF, SVG, USDZ, GLB, PNG render, or project backup, data goes to the recipient or app you select. You must have suitable rights to enter and share property, client, and design information, and keep backups of important projects.

Ads and tracking

The app currently has no third-party advertising or analytics SDK.

Purchases

Scanning, modeling, multiple projects, and sync through your personal iCloud account are free. Pro unlocks finished exports (PDF drawing set, DXF, SVG, USDZ, GLB, and high-resolution 3D renders) through an automatically renewing subscription purchased in the Apple App Store. Duration, local price, and any trial terms appear on the purchase screen.

Apple App Store processes subscriptions. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlements and does not receive your payment card number. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings; access generally continues through the paid term. Apple handles refunds under its policies.

Tap Restore Purchases on the app’s Pro screen using the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Third-party services

Apple processes iCloud data under its terms and privacy policy. Apple App Store handles subscriptions, and StoreKit verifies purchase entitlements. Data you choose to share goes to your selected recipient or app.

Deleting your data

Delete local projects from the app’s project list. For synced projects, the app sends the deletion to iCloud; an offline device must reconnect first. You can also remove this app’s data in your Apple device’s iCloud storage settings. Device or Apple Account backups may remain under your Apple settings. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.
