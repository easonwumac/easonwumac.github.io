適用範圍與聯絡方式

本政策適用於「極限停車場 / Precision Parking 3D」iOS 遊戲與本支援網站。本遊戲由個人開發者維護；有關遊戲、個人資料或不適當廣告，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。

遊戲儲存在裝置的資料

關卡進度、星星、最佳時間、成就、教學狀態、設定及廣告顯示頻率計數儲存在你的裝置。本遊戲沒有玩家登入、自建帳號系統或開發者營運的遊戲後端，也不自行上傳這些遊戲存檔。裝置備份可能依你的 Apple 系統設定保留 App 資料。

廣告與同意資訊

遊戲整合 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）及 User Messaging Platform（UMP），用於提供插頁廣告與管理適用的隱私選擇。Google 及參與廣告供應的服務可能處理 IP 位址及推估的大致位置、裝置或 App 範圍識別碼、廣告及互動資料、效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、分析、服務運作與改善。本遊戲不要求精確位置權限。

廣告服務的資料處理並非完全離線；「非個人化廣告」也不代表完全不蒐集資料。可請求的廣告依適用地區、SDK 狀態及有效隱私選擇而定。核心關卡可離線遊玩，廣告不可用時不會阻擋遊戲進度保存。

請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或 App 的資訊，了解其處理方式、資料保留與控制選項。

你的選擇與資料刪除

  • SDK 要求提供隱私選項時，可在遊戲「設定 → 一般 → 隱私選項」重新開啟表單，調整適用的選擇。選擇可能因地區與服務狀態而不同。
  • 目前遊戲版本未要求 Apple 跨 App 追蹤授權。你也可以在 iOS「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」管理 App 的系統追蹤權限。
  • 「重設遊戲進度」會清除本地進度、星星與成就，並保留個人設定；此操作不會撤回廣告同意，也不會刪除廣告服務商已持有的資料。刪除 App 可移除本機 App 資料，Apple 備份須另依系統設定管理。
  • 可寄信要求存取、更正或刪除你提供給開發者的支援資料，或提出其他適用隱私權請求。對 Google 持有的資料，請使用其隱私政策所列控制與聯絡方式。

支援來信、保留與安全

如果你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與你附上的診斷資訊或截圖，用於回覆、排查問題及處理廣告回報。請勿提供密碼、驗證碼、付款資料或不必要的敏感資訊。我們僅在處理請求、必要服務紀錄及適用法律要求所需期間保留來信；可透過上述信箱提出刪除請求。本地遊戲資料在你重設或刪除之前保留；第三方資料依各服務商政策保留。

我們採取合理措施保護所處理的資訊，但網路傳輸及儲存無法保證絕對安全。除服務提供、回覆你提出的要求或法律要求外，我們不會主動將你的支援來信公開。

網站與第三方服務

本網站使用 GitHub Pages 託管，沒有自行加入追蹤分析程式。GitHub 可能為提供網站服務處理連線及技術紀錄，詳見 GitHub 隱私聲明。Google、GitHub 與郵件服務可能在你所在地以外處理資料，依各自政策及適用保障措施運作。廣告或外部連結開啟的網站另適用其自身政策。

兒童與政策更新

本遊戲並非以兒童為主要對象，也不刻意透過支援功能蒐集兒童個人資料。家長或監護人如認為兒童向我們提供了個人資料，請聯絡上述信箱。政策更新會公布於本頁並調整更新日期；若資料用途有重大改變，會在適用時另行通知或取得必要同意。

Scope and contact

This policy covers the Precision Parking 3D iOS game and this support website. The game is maintained by an individual developer. For game support, privacy requests or inappropriate-ad reports, contact easonwumac@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, best times, achievements, tutorial status, preferences and ad-frequency counters are stored on your device. The game has no player login, developer-operated account system or game backend, and does not itself upload these save files. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your system settings.

Advertising and consent

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for interstitial advertising and applicable privacy choices. Google and participating advertising services may process IP-derived approximate location, device or app-scoped identifiers, advertising and interaction data, performance information and crash diagnostics for advertising, measurement, analytics and service operation or improvement. The game does not request precise-location permission.

Non-personalized ads do not mean that no data is collected. Available ad requests depend on region, SDK status and valid privacy choices. Core levels work offline, and unavailable ads do not prevent completed progress from being saved. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps for processing, retention and controls.

Your choices and deletion

  • When required by the SDK, open Settings → General → Privacy options to review applicable choices. Availability varies by region and service status.
  • The current game version does not request Apple's cross-app tracking authorization. System permissions can also be managed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset game progress clears local progress, stars and achievements while keeping preferences. It does not withdraw advertising consent or delete information already held by advertising providers. Deleting the app removes its local data; manage Apple backups separately through system settings.
  • Email us to request access, correction or deletion of support information you provided, or to exercise other applicable privacy rights. For information held by Google, use the controls and contact methods in Google's policy.

Support messages, retention and security

If you email us, we receive your address, message and any diagnostics or screenshots you attach, to respond, troubleshoot and handle ad reports. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. Messages are retained only as needed to address requests, maintain necessary service records or meet applicable legal obligations; you may request deletion by email. Local game data remains until reset or deletion. Third-party information follows the provider's retention policy.

We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no transmission or storage system is absolutely secure. We do not intentionally publish your support messages except as necessary to provide services, fulfill your request or comply with law.

Website and external services

This website uses GitHub Pages and adds no developer-operated analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records to serve the website; see its Privacy Statement. Google, GitHub and email services may process information outside your country under their policies and applicable safeguards. External websites and ad destinations have their own policies.

Children and updates

The game is not primarily directed to children, and we do not knowingly seek children's personal information through support. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided information to us can contact the address above. Updates will appear here with a revised date. Material changes to data use will receive additional notice or necessary consent where applicable.
