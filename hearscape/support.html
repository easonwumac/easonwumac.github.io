常見問題

怎麼找出噪音從哪裡來？

在雷達頁按「轉一圈掃描」，螢幕朝向自己慢慢原地轉一圈。App 會用每個方向的音量畫出熱度環，標出最響的方向，並分別列出低音、中音、高音各自的方向。手機靜止不動時無法判斷方向，一定要轉一圈。

分貝準確嗎？

手機麥克風大約有 ±2–3 dB 的誤差，數值僅供參考，不能取代經認證的聲級計或作為法律證據。若有標準聲級計，可在設定裡輸入校正值。

整夜監測怎麼用？

在監測頁設定門檻後按「開始監測」，可鎖定螢幕放著。超過門檻的聲音會自動錄下片段；早上按「停止並儲存」，在紀錄頁查看時間軸、播放片段或分享 PDF 報告。建議接上充電器。

錄音會上傳嗎？

不會。聲音只在裝置上分析，錄音和報告只存在裝置上，除非你自己按分享。

換手機或重新安裝後，移除廣告還在嗎？

在設定頁按「恢復購買」即可取回。紀錄存在裝置上，刪除 App 會一併刪除。

回報問題

請提供 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、當時的畫面及重現步驟。購買問題請先試「恢復購買」；退款請透過 Apple 的 回報問題 頁面申請。不適當廣告請提供出現時間與內容，請勿為回報而點擊廣告。

FAQ

How do I find where a noise comes from?

On the Radar tab, tap Sweep 360° and turn slowly in a full circle with the screen toward you. The app draws a heat ring from the level in each direction, marks the loudest one and lists the direction of the low, mid and high ranges. A phone held still cannot tell direction, so the turn is needed.

How accurate are the decibels?

Phone microphones are about ±2–3 dB, so readings are for reference only and are not a certified sound level meter or legal evidence. If you have a calibrated meter, enter an offset in Settings.

How does overnight monitoring work?

Set a threshold on the Monitor tab, tap Start monitoring and lock the screen. Sounds over the threshold are saved as clips. In the morning tap Stop and save, then open the Records tab for the timeline, the clips and a PDF report to share. Keep the phone charging.

Are recordings uploaded?

No. Sound is analysed on the device, and clips and reports stay on the device unless you share them yourself.

Does Remove Ads carry over to a new phone or a reinstall?

Yes, tap Restore Purchases in Settings. Records are stored on the device and are deleted with the app.

Report a problem

Include the app version, device model, iOS version, what was on screen and steps to reproduce. For purchases, try Restore Purchases first; request refunds through Apple’s Report a Problem. For inappropriate ads, include the time and the ad content; do not click an ad just to report it.
