摘要

本政策適用於「聲跡 Hearscape－噪音分貝計」iOS App 與本支援網站。本 App 由個人開發者維護。

麥克風與錄音

App 使用麥克風即時計算分貝、頻譜與聲音方向，聲音只在你的裝置上分析。整夜監測時，超過門檻的片段會錄成短音檔，連同音量紀錄與 PDF 報告只存在你的裝置上，不會上傳到開發者或任何伺服器。只有你自己按「分享」時，檔案才會透過 iOS 分享表單送到你選擇的地方。監測期間螢幕鎖定時仍會繼續收音，iOS 會顯示錄音指示；按「停止並儲存」即停止。

相機

雷達頁的「AR 掃描」使用相機顯示畫面，並在畫面上標出聲音方向。相機影像不會被錄下、儲存或上傳。

Apple 健康（HealthKit）

你在「紀錄」頁按「連接 Apple 健康」並同意後，App 會讀取「健康」裡的環境音量與耳機音量（例如 Apple Watch「噪音」或 AirPods 記錄的資料），只為了在紀錄頁顯示最近 7 天的趨勢。App 只讀取、不寫入「健康」；這些資料只在你的裝置上處理，不會上傳、不會提供給廣告服務或任何第三方，也不會用於廣告或行銷。你可以隨時在「健康」App 關閉取用權限。

Apple Watch 與通知

Apple Watch App 透過 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity，在你的 iPhone 與手錶之間直接傳送監測狀態、即時音量與開始／停止指令，不經過任何伺服器。若你在「監測」頁開啟「超標時提醒」，App 會在 iPhone 上發出不出聲的本機通知（iPhone 鎖定時會顯示在 Apple Watch 上）；通知由 iOS 在本機產生，不使用推播伺服器。

動作（AirPods 頭部追蹤）

在雷達頁按「用 AirPods 跟隨頭部」時，App 會讀取支援機型 AirPods 的頭部轉動方向，只用來即時顯示該轉向哪裡。這些動作資料不會被儲存或上傳。

其他資料存放

校正值、門檻、語言等設定與購買狀態都儲存在你的裝置。本 App 沒有登入、帳號系統或開發者營運的後端。裝置備份可能依你的 Apple 系統設定保留 App 資料。資料在你刪除紀錄或 App 之前保留。

App 內購買

「移除廣告」是一次性的 App 內購買，由 Apple 處理付款，開發者不會收到你的付款資料；App 只從 Apple 取得交易結果。設定頁的「恢復購買」會向 Apple 查詢已購買的項目。

廣告與追蹤

App 整合 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK），在頻譜、分貝計與紀錄頁顯示橫幅廣告，並在儲存監測紀錄後偶爾顯示插頁廣告；首次使用時不顯示廣告。App 只請求非個人化廣告，不使用 Apple 的 App 追蹤透明度（ATT），不詢問追蹤權限，也不存取廣告識別碼（IDFA）。Google 及參與廣告供應的服務仍可能處理 IP 位址及推估的大致位置、App 範圍識別碼、廣告互動資料、效能與當機診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、防詐與服務運作。「非個人化廣告」不代表完全不蒐集資料。麥克風聲音與錄音不會提供給廣告服務。

意見回饋與其他 App

設定中的「意見回饋」只在你按下「送出」時，把你輸入的內容、選填的 Email 與 App 版本傳到開發者的伺服器 agentmesh.uk（由 Cloudflare 託管），轉成開發者私人 GitHub 儲存庫中的一則待辦後，伺服器即刪除副本；每日送出次數以當天更換的 IP 雜湊計算，隔日刪除。內容只用於回覆與改善 App，請勿填寫敏感資料，可來信要求刪除。設定底部的「開發者的其他 App」清單由 agentmesh.uk 提供，不傳送個人資料或識別碼；App 名稱、圖示與評分由 Apple 的 App Store 查詢服務（itunes.apple.com）依你的地區取得，點選不會被追蹤。

第三方服務

請參閱 Google 隱私權政策、Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或 App 的資訊及 Apple 隱私權政策。本網站使用 GitHub Pages 託管，沒有自行加入追蹤分析程式；GitHub 可能處理連線紀錄，詳見 GitHub 隱私聲明。

資料刪除

可在「紀錄」頁刪除單筆紀錄及其錄音片段；刪除 App 會移除所有本機資料。Apple 備份須另依系統設定管理。刪除 App 不會刪除廣告服務商或 Apple 已持有的資料。

兒童

本 App 並非以兒童為主要對象。家長或監護人如認為兒童向我們提供了個人資料，請聯絡下方信箱。

聯絡我們

有關 App、個人資料或不適當廣告，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。來信僅用於回覆與排查問題，並在處理所需期間保留；可來信要求刪除。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼或付款資料。

Summary

This policy covers the Hearscape: Sound Locator (聲跡) iOS app and this support website. The app is maintained by an individual developer.

Microphone and recordings

The app uses the microphone to compute decibels, the spectrum and the direction of sound live; sound is analysed only on your device. During overnight monitoring, moments over your threshold are saved as short clips. Clips, level logs and PDF reports stay on your device and are never uploaded to the developer or any server. A file leaves the device only when you tap Share and pick a destination in the iOS share sheet. Monitoring keeps listening with the screen locked, shown by the iOS recording indicator, until you tap Stop and save.

Camera

AR scan on the Radar tab uses the camera to show the room and mark where sound comes from. Camera images are not recorded, stored or uploaded.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you tap Connect Apple Health on the Records tab and allow it, the app reads environmental and headphone audio levels from Health (for example those logged by an Apple Watch's Noise app or AirPods) only to show the last seven days on the Records tab. The app only reads and never writes to Health; this data is processed on your device only, never uploaded, never given to ad services or any third party, and never used for advertising or marketing. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app.

Apple Watch and notifications

The Apple Watch app sends monitor status, the live level and start/stop commands directly between your iPhone and watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity, with no server in between. If you turn on Alert when over the limit on the Monitor tab, the app posts silent local notifications on the iPhone (shown on Apple Watch while the iPhone is locked); iOS creates them on the device and no push server is used.

Motion (AirPods head tracking)

When you tap Follow with AirPods on the Radar tab, the app reads the head direction from supported AirPods only to show live where to turn. This motion data is not stored or uploaded.

Other data

Calibration, thresholds, language and purchase status are stored on your device. There is no login, account system or developer-operated backend. Apple device backups may keep app data according to your settings. Data remains until you delete a record or the app.

In-app purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles payment and the developer never receives your payment details; the app only receives the transaction result from Apple. Restore Purchases in Settings asks Apple for items you bought.

Ads and tracking

The app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for banner ads on the Spectrum, Meter and Records tabs and an occasional interstitial after a monitoring session is saved; no ads appear in the first session. The app requests non-personalized ads only, does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT), never asks to track you and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google and participating ad services may still process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, app-scoped identifiers, ad interaction data, performance and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is collected. Microphone audio and recordings are never given to ad services.

Feedback and our other apps

Send feedback in Settings sends what you type, the email you may add and the app version only when you tap Send, to the developer’s server agentmesh.uk (hosted on Cloudflare), which files it as an item in the developer’s private GitHub repository and then deletes its copy; the daily send limit counts a hash of your IP address that changes every day and is deleted the next day. It is used only to reply and improve the app; please leave out sensitive information, and email us to have it deleted. More from the developer at the bottom of Settings lists our other apps from agentmesh.uk with no personal data or identifiers sent; names, icons and ratings come from Apple’s App Store lookup service (itunes.apple.com) for your region, and taps are not tracked.

Third-party services

See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner sites and apps and Apple’s Privacy Policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages without developer-added analytics; GitHub may process connection records, see its Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Delete a record and its clips on the Records tab; deleting the app removes all local data. Manage Apple backups through system settings. Deleting the app does not delete information already held by ad providers or Apple.

Children

The app is not primarily directed to children. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information to us can contact the email below.

Contact

For app support, privacy requests or inappropriate-ad reports, email easonwumac@gmail.com. Messages are used only to reply and troubleshoot, kept as long as needed, and deleted on request. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment details.
