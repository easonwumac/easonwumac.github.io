Hearscape

Hearscape turns your phone into a sound radar and noise log.

Free on the App StoreSupportFeedback

Features

RADAR

  • A big live dB(A) reading in the centre of a glowing dial.
  • Directional mic: on the Radar tab Hearscape uses the back directional microphone, so the level rises when the phone’s back faces the source (on devices that have one).
  • Sweep: turn slowly in a full circle and the dial paints a 360° heat ring, then marks the loudest direction. Low, mid and high sounds each get their own bearing, so a near hum and a distant voice point different ways.
  • AR scan: turn with the camera; after half a turn a beacon in the room marks the loudest direction, and loud moments leave markers where the phone was pointing, fading over 30 seconds.
  • A phone held still cannot tell where a sound comes from; turning finds it.

SPECTRUM

  • Sound mix: how much is low (engines, air conditioning), mid (voices, barking) and high (hiss, birds) right now.
  • Spectrum with peak hold and the last 5 seconds as a spectrogram, both split into the same three ranges.
  • The dominant pitch with its musical note.
  • Staff: hum, whistle or play a single melody and watch its notes appear live on a grand staff.

METER

  • A, C and Z weighting, Fast and Slow response.
  • Leq, Lmax, Lmin, L10 and L90 since the last reset, plus a calibration offset.
  • On-device sound recognition names what it hears: traffic, dog barking, music, speech, construction and more.

NIGHT MONITOR

  • Leave it running with the screen locked: a timeline of the whole night.
  • Thresholds follow Taiwan's noise control standards by zone and time of day, or set your own.
  • When the level goes over, Hearscape saves a clip from five seconds before, with the time and the kind of sound.

RECORDS AND REPORTS

  • Play and share each clip.
  • Export a PDF report with the timeline, Leq per period against the limit and every event.

APPLE WATCH, HEALTH AND AIRPODS

  • Apple Watch app: see the live level on your wrist, start or stop a night's monitor, and feel a tap when it goes over the limit. A complication shows whether it is monitoring and how many times it went over.
  • Health: Records shows seven days of the surroundings noise your Apple Watch logged and your headphone levels (read only, never written, never leaves this phone).
  • AirPods: after a sweep, wear AirPods with head tracking and simply turn your head to face the loudest direction.

Recordings and records stay on this device: no account, and audio is never uploaded.

Measured with a phone microphone: for reference, not a certified sound level meter.

FAQ

How can I find which direction a noise comes from?

Open the Radar in Hearscape and turn slowly in a full circle: it paints a 360° heat ring and marks the loudest direction, with separate bearings for low, mid and high sounds. A phone held still cannot tell direction; turning finds it.

Can I log noise overnight?

Yes. Hearscape keeps monitoring with the screen locked and records the night's decibel curve; when a threshold you set is passed it keeps a clip starting 5 seconds earlier, and you can export a PDF report. It measures with the phone's microphone and is not a certified sound level meter.
