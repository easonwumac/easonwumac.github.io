摘要

本政策適用於 Daikon（蘿蔔日記）iOS App 與本支援網站。此 App 由個人開發者維護。蘿蔔日記不需要帳號，你的記錄不會傳到開發者營運的伺服器，App 也不自行蒐集使用分析資料。你的經期記錄只屬於你，絕不會用於廣告。

資料存放

你登記的經期日期、週期與經期長度設定、提醒設定、日記（心情、身體感覺、文字、手寫、照片與語音備忘錄），以及由這些資料算出的預測，都保存在你的裝置上。若你的裝置已登入 iCloud，這些記錄會透過 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit）同步到你自己的私人資料庫，只有你的 Apple 帳號能存取；開發者無法讀取。

預測（下次經期、排卵日、受孕期）全部在裝置上計算，僅供參考，不是醫療建議，也不能作為避孕方法。

通知只在你於設定中開啟提醒後，才由裝置排程本機通知。你可隨時關閉提醒，或在 iOS 設定中關閉通知權限。

「設定 → 備份 → 匯出備份」會把你登記的記錄、日記（含照片與語音）和週期設定，用你設定的密碼加密成一個檔案，存到你在系統檔案選擇器選的位置；從 Apple 健康匯入的日期不包含在內。密碼不會保存，開發者無法打開或找回備份。檔案存放或分享到哪裡由你決定，之後適用該服務的隱私規則。

日記

日記只在你主動加入時才使用照片與麥克風：「相簿」只取得你選取的照片；「錄一段語音」會詢問麥克風權限，語音備忘錄只保存錄音本身。手寫辨識，以及在支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置上「寫一段回顧」，都只在你的裝置上完成，不會傳給開發者或任何第三方。照片、語音與手寫和其他記錄一樣保存在裝置上，並可透過你的私人 iCloud 同步；「設定 → 刪除所有記錄」也會一併刪除。

Apple 健康

「設定 → 同步至 Apple 健康」預設關閉。只有在你開啟並於系統畫面允許後，蘿蔔日記才會把你登記的經期日期以「月經」資料寫入 Apple 健康，之後隨你的修改更新。App 會讀回自己寫入的資料，用來避免重複。「設定 → 匯入過往記錄 → 從 Apple 健康匯入」只在 iCloud 同步關閉時提供，經你允許後會讀取其他 App 寫入的月經資料，加入你的經期記錄；不會讀取其他種類的健康資料。匯入的日期另外存放在只留在這台裝置上的資料庫，不會同步到 iCloud、不會納入裝置備份，即使之後登入 iCloud 也一樣；也不會寫回 Apple 健康。你手動記錄的內容仍照常透過 iCloud 同步。從 Apple 健康取得的資料只在你的裝置上使用，不會傳到 iCloud、開發者、廣告服務或任何第三方，也不會用於廣告或行銷。關閉同步會停止寫入，已寫入的資料仍保留在健康 App；使用「設定 → 刪除所有記錄」時，蘿蔔日記寫入的資料也會一併刪除。你也可以在健康 App 或 iOS「設定 → 健康 → 資料取用權限與裝置」中隨時撤銷權限或刪除資料。

廣告

蘿蔔日記使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告，且只請求非個人化廣告；App 不使用 Apple 廣告追蹤（ATT），不會詢問追蹤權限。你的經期與健康相關資料不會傳給廣告服務，也不會用於廣告或行銷。App 只請求非個人化廣告，因此不顯示 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）的廣告同意表單；蘿蔔日記不在歐盟／歐洲經濟區上架。廣告以全螢幕插頁廣告的形式，偶爾在記錄完成後出現，不會打斷記錄過程。

即使是非個人化廣告，AdMob 仍可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與廣告互動資料，以及效能與診斷資料，用於廣告投放、頻率控制、防詐欺與衡量。詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 AdMob 資料使用說明。

購買

你可以在「設定 → 移除廣告」一次購買移除廣告，沒有訂閱。購買與還原由 Apple App Store 處理；蘿蔔日記不會取得你的付款資料。

我們的其他 App

1.6 起，設定頁底部的「更多 App」會列出我們的其他 App：清單內容與順序由 agentmesh.uk 提供，不傳送任何個人資料或經期記錄；App 名稱與圖示由 Apple 的 App Store 查詢服務（itunes.apple.com）依你所在地區取得，Apple 會如處理一般網路請求般看到你的 IP 位址。點選後開啟該 App 的 App Store 頁面。

設定中的「傳送意見」會打開意見表單，可附上最多 3 張你選的圖片。只有你按下「送出」時，才會把你的文字、圖片、選填的 Email、App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本與語言傳到開發者的伺服器 agentmesh.uk（由 Cloudflare 託管），轉成開發者私人 GitHub 儲存庫中的一則待辦；文字轉成待辦後即從伺服器刪除，圖片存放在只有該待辦才有連結的網址，90 天後刪除。每日送出次數以當天更換的 IP 雜湊計算，隔日刪除。內容只用於回覆與改善 App，請勿附上敏感資料，可來信要求提早刪除。表單不會自動附上任何經期記錄或日記。

資料刪除

在月曆點一下已登記的日期即可取消該天的記錄；在「設定 → 刪除所有記錄」可清除全部記錄，若已開啟 iCloud 同步，你的其他裝置與 iCloud 中的記錄也會一併刪除。只刪除 App 不會刪除 iCloud 中的記錄，你可在 iOS「設定 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 管理儲存空間」中刪除蘿蔔日記的資料。

本網站透過 GitHub Pages 提供，沒有開發者加裝的分析程式。GitHub 可能處理連線與技術紀錄，請參閱其 隱私權聲明。

聯絡我們

若有隱私、資料或廣告相關問題，請聯絡 easonwumac@gmail.com。若你主動寄信，我們會收到寄件信箱、訊息與附件，只用於回覆。請不要寄送你的經期記錄或其他健康資料。

Summary

This policy covers the Daikon (蘿蔔日記) iOS app and this support website. The app is maintained by an independent developer. Daikon requires no account, never sends your logs to a developer-operated server, and does not collect usage analytics itself. Your period logs belong to you and are never used for advertising.

Data stored

The period dates you log, your cycle and period length settings, reminder settings, your diary (mood, how you feel, text, handwriting, photos and voice memos), and the predictions calculated from them are stored on your device. If your device is signed in to iCloud, these records sync through Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) to your own private database, which only your Apple Account can access; the developer cannot read it.

Predictions (next period, ovulation, fertile window) are calculated entirely on your device. They are estimates, not medical advice, and must not be used as contraception.

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device only after you turn on a reminder in Settings. You can turn reminders off at any time or disable notifications in iOS Settings.

Settings → Backup → Export backup encrypts the days you logged, your diary (photos and voice memos included) and cycle settings with a password you choose, into one file saved where you pick in the system file picker; days imported from Apple Health are left out. The password is not stored, and the developer cannot open or recover a backup. You decide where the file is kept or shared; that service’s privacy rules then apply.

Diary

The diary uses photos and the microphone only when you add something: Library gets only the photos you pick; Record a voice memo asks for microphone access and keeps the recording only. Handwriting recognition and, on devices with Apple Intelligence, Write a recap all run on your device and are never sent to the developer or any third party. Photos, voice memos and handwriting are stored on your device like your other logs and can sync through your private iCloud; Settings → Delete all logs deletes them too.

Apple Health

Settings → Sync to Apple Health is off by default. Only after you turn it on and allow access in the system sheet does Daikon write the period days you log to Apple Health as menstrual flow, and keep them in step with your edits. The app reads back the samples it wrote itself, to avoid duplicates. Settings → Bring in past logs → Import from Apple Health, offered only while iCloud sync is off, reads menstrual flow other apps saved, after you allow it, and adds those days to your period logs; it never reads any other kind of Health data. Imported days are kept in a separate store on this device that never syncs to iCloud and is not included in device backups, even if you sign in to iCloud later, and they are not written back to Health. The logs you enter yourself keep syncing through iCloud. Data obtained from Apple Health is used only on your device and is never sent to iCloud, the developer, the ad service or any third party, and is never used for advertising or marketing. Turning sync off stops writing and leaves what is already in Health; Settings → Delete all logs also deletes the samples Daikon wrote. You can revoke access or delete the data at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Ads

Daikon uses Google AdMob to display ads and requests non-personalized ads only. The app does not use Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and never asks to track you. Your period and health-related data is never sent to the ad service and is never used for advertising or marketing. Because the app requests non-personalized ads only, it does not show Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form; Daikon is not offered in the EU/EEA. Ads appear as full-screen interstitial ads, now and then after you finish a log, never in the middle of one.

Even for non-personalized ads, AdMob may process IP addresses, device and ad interaction data, and performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure.

Purchases

You can make a one-time Remove ads purchase in Settings → Remove ads. There is no subscription. Apple App Store handles purchases and restoration; Daikon never receives your payment details.

Our other apps

From version 1.6, More apps at the bottom of Settings lists our other apps: the list and its order are fetched from agentmesh.uk, with no personal data or period logs sent, and app names and icons are read from Apple’s App Store lookup service (itunes.apple.com) for your region; Apple sees your IP address as with any network request. Tapping one opens its App Store page.

Send feedback in Settings opens a form where you can attach up to 3 pictures you choose. Only when you tap Send do your text, pictures, the email you may add, the app version, device model, iOS version and language go to the developer’s server agentmesh.uk (hosted on Cloudflare), which files them as an item in the developer’s private GitHub repository. The text is deleted from the server once filed; pictures stay at an address only that item links to and are deleted after 90 days. The daily send limit counts a hash of your IP address that changes every day and is deleted the next day. It is used only to reply and improve the app; please leave out sensitive information, and email us to have it deleted sooner. The form never attaches your period logs or diary.

Deleting your data

Tap a logged day in the calendar to remove it, or use Settings → Delete all logs to clear every record; with iCloud sync on, this also removes them from your other devices and iCloud. Deleting only the app does not delete records stored in iCloud; remove Daikon’s data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no developer-added analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection and technical records; see its Privacy Statement.

For privacy, data, or ad questions, email easonwumac@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments, used only to reply. Please do not send your period logs or other health data.
