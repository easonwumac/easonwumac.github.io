每天一點，溫柔照顧。

蘿蔔日記是一款簡單、開心的經期記錄工具，也是懂你週期的日記。記下月經，看一看接下來的小日子，讓幾隻白白的蘿蔔寶寶陪著你。經期來了，蘿蔔就變紅蘿蔔；預測排卵日，寶寶會戴上一朵小花。

一眼看懂你的節奏
彩色週期圓環標示經期、預測受孕期、排卵日與今天。下三次預測經期、下次排卵與受孕期日期，也一起整理好了。

小小記一筆
按下「月經來了」或「月經結束了」，寶寶們就會跳起來、轉個圈，灑下葉片與花瓣。過去的日期可以在月曆直接編輯，相鄰經期日會自動合併。

懂你週期的日記
每天一頁：心情、身體感覺、幾句話、用 Apple Pencil 或手指寫的手寫，最多九張照片，還有語音備忘錄。一眼看完整個月的心情與照片、搜尋每一頁、回顧往年的同一天，也能把日記匯出成 PDF。

規律與回顧
連續幾個週期記下身體感覺，蘿蔔日記會找出反覆出現的狀況，例如經期前兩天總是經痛。上個週期也有一份回顧；在支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置上，還能直接在裝置上幫你寫好。

你的紀錄
週期有多規律、下次可能從哪天開始、每一次週期，還能匯出 PDF 給醫生看。記錯可以復原、可以暫停預測，也能從 CSV 或 Apple 健康匯入過往記錄。

Widget、Siri 與鎖定
主畫面與鎖定畫面 Widget、控制中心按鈕、Siri，以及在鎖定畫面顯示經期第幾天。也能用 Face ID、Touch ID 或密碼鎖住蘿蔔日記。

漏記了，預測也會繼續
最多參考最近六次週期，也能使用你設定的長度。即使沒有記錄新的經期，預測仍會持續往後推算，並溫柔提醒你補上記錄。

剛剛好的自選提醒
經期快來了、預測受孕期開始，或是「月經來了嗎？」的小問候，全部預設關閉。由你決定要不要開啟，提醒只使用本機通知。

也能同步到 Apple 健康
在設定中開啟「同步至 Apple 健康」，記錄的每個經期日都會以月經流量寫入「健康」App。預設關閉；關閉同步時會保留「健康」中的資料，「刪除所有記錄」也會一併移除蘿蔔日記寫入的項目。蘿蔔日記的 iCloud 關閉時，也可以從「健康」匯入過去的經期。

私人的小蘿蔔園
不需要帳號，沒有社群或訂閱。記錄與日記（包括照片和語音）儲存在裝置上；iCloud 可用時，可透過你的私人 iCloud 同步。手寫辨識和回顧都在裝置上完成。健康資料絕不傳給廣告 SDK，也不會用來投放廣告。開啟 Apple 健康同步時，經期日也會存到「健康」。

在展開的 iPhone Duo、直向的 13 吋 iPad 或橫向的 iPad 上，今天與月曆左右並排。

支援 iPhone、iPad、英文、繁體中文、簡體中文、日文、韓文、西班牙文、法文與德文，提供暖色淺色／深色外觀，並尊重「減少動態效果」設定。

廣告：非個人化插頁廣告只可能在記錄與動畫完成後出現，最多每四分鐘一次。首次使用期間不顯示廣告，廣告不會阻止記錄。在設定中一次購買「移除廣告」即可永久關閉廣告，其他裝置可用「還原購買」。

預測僅供參考，並非醫療建議，也不能用於避孕。身體的週期可能有所變化。

A little care, every day.

Daikon is a simple, joyful period tracker and a diary that knows your cycle. Log your period, see what may be coming next, and let a few little white radishes brighten your day. When your period arrives, they turn into red carrots. On estimated ovulation day, one wears a flower.

YOUR RHYTHM AT A GLANCE
A colorful cycle ring shows period days, the estimated fertile window, ovulation, and today. See the next three predicted periods and upcoming fertility dates.

ONE LITTLE LOG
Tap “My period started” or “My period ended.” The babies jump, spin, and shower you with leaves. Edit past days in the calendar; neighboring days join automatically.

A DIARY THAT KNOWS YOUR CYCLE
Every day gets a page: your mood, how you feel, a few words, handwriting with Apple Pencil or your finger, up to nine photos and voice memos. See a month of moods and photos, search every page, look back on the same day in past years, and export your diary as a PDF.

PATTERNS AND RECAPS
Mark how you feel for a few cycles and Daikon shows what keeps coming back, like cramps two days before your period. A recap sums up your last cycle; on devices with Apple Intelligence, it can be written for you on the device.

YOUR HISTORY
How regular your cycles are, where the next one could start, every past cycle and a PDF for your doctor. Undo a log, pause predictions, or bring in past logs from a CSV file or Apple Health.

WIDGETS, SIRI AND LOCK
Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center button, Siri, and the period day on your Lock Screen. Lock Daikon with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

PREDICTIONS THAT KEEP GOING
Daikon learns from up to six recent cycles, or uses a cycle length you choose. If you haven’t logged a new period, estimates keep rolling forward. A gentle prompt helps you update your history.

GENTLE, OPTIONAL REMINDERS
Choose a period reminder, an estimated fertile-window reminder, or a “Did your period start?” check-in. All are off until you turn them on. Reminders are local notifications.

APPLE HEALTH, IF YOU WANT IT
Turn on “Sync to Apple Health” in Settings to save each logged period day as menstrual flow in the Health app. It’s off by default and keeps your Health data if you turn it off. “Delete all logs” also removes what Daikon wrote there. While iCloud is off for Daikon, you can also import past periods from Health.

PRIVATE LITTLE GARDEN
No account, community, or subscription. Your logs and diary, photos and voice memos included, are stored on your device and can sync through your private iCloud when available. Handwriting and recaps are recognized and written on your device. Your health data is never sent to the advertising SDK or used to target ads. If you turn on Apple Health sync, period days are also saved in Health.

On an unfolded iPhone Duo, a 13-inch iPad in portrait or any iPad in landscape, today and the calendar sit side by side.

Designed for iPhone and iPad, in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, and German, with a warm light and dark appearance and support for Reduce Motion.

Advertising: non-personalized interstitial ads may appear after logging and its animation finish, at most once every four minutes. Your first session is ad-free. Ads never block logging. A one-time “Remove ads” purchase in Settings turns ads off for good, with “Restore purchases” on your other devices.

Predictions are estimates, not medical advice or contraception. Cycle timing can vary.
