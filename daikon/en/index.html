Daikon

A little care, every day.

Free on the App StoreSupportFeedback

Features

YOUR RHYTHM AT A GLANCE

A colorful cycle ring shows period days, the estimated fertile window, ovulation, and today.

More

Today shows your next predicted period and fertile window; the calendar shows the ones after. Only tracking your period? Hide fertility in Settings.

ONE LITTLE LOG

Tap “My period started” or “My period ended.” The babies jump, spin, and shower you with leaves.

More

Edit past days in the calendar; neighboring days join automatically.

A DIARY THAT KNOWS YOUR CYCLE

Every day gets a page: your mood, how you feel, a few words, handwriting with Apple Pencil or your finger, up to nine photos and voice memos.

More

See a month of moods and photos, search every page, look back on the same day in past years, and export your diary as a PDF.

PATTERNS AND RECAPS

Mark how you feel for a few cycles and Daikon shows what keeps coming back, like cramps two days before your period.

More

A recap sums up your last cycle; on devices with Apple Intelligence, it can be written for you on the device.

YOUR HISTORY

How regular your cycles are, where the next one could start, every past cycle and a PDF for your doctor.

More

Undo a log, pause predictions, or bring in past logs from a CSV file or Apple Health.

WIDGETS, SIRI AND LOCK

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center button, Siri, and the period day on your Lock Screen.

More

Lock Daikon with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

PREDICTIONS THAT KEEP GOING

Daikon learns from up to six recent cycles, or uses a cycle length you choose.

More

If you haven’t logged a new period, estimates keep rolling forward. A gentle prompt helps you update your history.

GENTLE, OPTIONAL REMINDERS

Choose a period reminder, an estimated fertile-window reminder, a “Did your period start?” check-in, a reminder to restock supplies a few days ahead, or a daily pill reminder.

More

All are off until you turn them on. Reminders are local notifications.

APPLE HEALTH, IF YOU WANT IT

Turn on “Sync to Apple Health” in Settings to save each logged period day as menstrual flow in the Health app.

More

It’s off by default and keeps your Health data if you turn it off. “Delete all logs” also removes what Daikon wrote there. While iCloud is off for Daikon, you can also import past periods from Health.

PRIVATE LITTLE GARDEN

No account, community, or subscription.

More

Your logs and diary, photos and voice memos included, are stored on your device and can sync through your private iCloud when available. Handwriting and recaps are recognized and written on your device. Your health data is never sent to the advertising SDK or used to target ads. If you turn on Apple Health sync, period days are also saved in Health.

Daikon is a simple, joyful period tracker and a diary that knows your cycle. Log your period, see what may be coming next, and let a few little white radishes brighten your day. When your period arrives, they turn into red carrots. On estimated ovulation day, one wears a flower.

On an unfolded iPhone Duo, a 13-inch iPad in portrait or any iPad in landscape, today and the calendar sit side by side.

Designed for iPhone and iPad, in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, and German, with a warm light and dark appearance and support for Reduce Motion.

Advertising: no ad when you open the app. A non-personalized interstitial ad may appear only after you log a period start or end and its animation finishes, at most once a day. A small banner (an ad, or one of our own apps) sits at the bottom of Diary and History. Your first session is ad-free. Ads never block logging. A one-time “Remove ads” purchase in Settings turns ads off for good, with “Restore purchases” on your other devices.

Predictions are estimates, not medical advice or contraception. Cycle timing can vary.

FAQ

Is there a simple, cute period tracker app?

Daikon shows your period, predicted fertile window and ovulation on a cycle ring, logs a period with one tap, and keeps you company with little radish characters.

Can a period tracker also be a diary?

Yes. Daikon has a page per day for mood, body notes, text, handwriting, up to nine photos and voice memos, and spots symptoms that recur at the same point in your cycle.

Can I export Daikon records for my doctor?

Cycle records export as PDF, past records import from CSV or Apple Health, and the app can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode.

Does a period tracker like Daikon upload my data?

Daikon needs no account and has no social features or subscription. Records and journal entries, including photos and voice memos, stay on the device and sync only through your private iCloud when available. Health data is never sent to ad SDKs or used for advertising.

Can a period log sync with Apple Health?

Yes. Turn on Sync to Apple Health in Daikon's settings and each period day is written to the Health app as menstrual flow; it is off by default. Past periods can also be imported from Health or a CSV file.

If I forget to log my period, does the prediction keep going?

Yes. Daikon uses up to your last six cycles or a length you set; without a new entry the prediction keeps rolling forward and gently reminds you to log. Predictions are for reference only, not medical advice, and not for contraception.
